# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka tiakina e te kaupapa here te 30% o te whenua me ngā moana, ka whakaorangia te 30% i te iti rawa o te horahanga repo kua kino, ka aukatia ngā keri hōu i runga i te whenua tiaki, ā, ka whakawhānuitia a Jobs for Nature. Me kōwhiri tētahi i ngā wāhi tuatahi, me utu hoki te mahi.

System: Te tiaki ā-wāhi me te kawenga whakaora repo

Scenario: Ka neke te whenua me ngā moana ki te whāinga tiaki
When ka whakahouria ngā ture tiaki taiao me ngā kararehe mohoao hei whakamutu, hei huri whakamuri hoki i te ngaronga taiao
Then ka pā ngā here whakamahi kaha ake ki ngā wāhi ka kaute ki te whāinga tiaki
Output 30% o te whenua me ngā moana kua tiakina
Outcome ka riro i ngā momo taketake me ngā pūnaha rauropi te mātāmua ā-ture i te kaha haere o te tiaki
But ka ngaro i ngā kaipupuri whenua me ngā kaiwhakamahi i ngā heketea kati tuatahi ngā whakamahi o nāianei ina gazetitia ngā wāhi

Scenario: Ka whakaorangia ngā repo kua kino
When ka tiakina ngā repo māori katoa e toe ana
And ka whakaora tētahi mahere i te horahanga repo kua kino
Then ka tīmata te mahi whakaora me te huri whakamahi whenua i ngā riu kua kōwhiria
Output i te 30% i te iti rawa o te horahanga repo kua kino kua whakaorangia
Outcome ka tīmata te hoki mai o ngā repo kua kino i ngā riu kua kōwhiria
But kei ngā kaipupuri whenua me ngā kaunihera te utu whakaora kia tohua rā anō te pūtea

Scenario: Ka mutu te keri i runga i te whenua tiaki, ā, ka whānui a Jobs for Nature
When ka aukatia ngā keri hōu i runga i te whenua tiaki, tae atu ki te stewardship land
And ka whakawhānuitia te pūtea mō Jobs for Nature – Mahi mō te Taiao
Then kāore e taea te whakatuwhera i ngā maina hōu i tērā whenua, ā, ka taea te tupu o ngā mahi taiao ā-rohe
Outcome ka kati te tango rawa ā-kamupene i runga i te whenua tiaki
But ka riro i te Karauna tētahi pire mutunga-kore mō Jobs for Nature me te whakapau tiaki whānui ake
