Read our policy

Our policy will recognise and resource marae as critical social and cultural infrastructure. 

OUR MĀORI MANIFESTO

The Green Party supports Māori to make decisions about the destiny of their own whānau, hapū and iwi.

OUR TE TIRITI O WAITANGI POLICY

All Green Party policies are rooted in Te Tiriti o Waitangi and must be aligned with this foundational policy.

## The Problem

Despite their importance, marae across the motu continue to face a compounding number of challenges that threaten both their operational capability and cultural integrity.

Funding programmes are often short-term, capped, and prescribed rather than community-led. Many are fragmented and difficult to access. This makes it difficult for marae committees to undertake long-term planning, restore important infrastructure, build capability, or invest in projects that strengthen their communities for future generations.

> Together, we can ensure future generations inherit a vibrant, resilient, and self-determining community and Aotearoa.

— Green Party Co-leader Marama Davidson

## Our Solutions

Uphold tino rangatiratanga and self-governance

Facilitate nationwide wānanga to establish the trustee body, two funds, and governance mechanisms

Create Te Rangatiratanga Trust, owned and governed by hapū and iwi of Aotearoa

**Establish Te Rangatiratanga Trust**

Dedicated Crown funding of $2.44 billion total for Te Rangatiratanga Trust

The Trust will govern immediate investment and long-term sustainability via two complementary funds

**Build a restoration fund that provides for urgent and immediate needs**

Implement Te Rākau Whakarauora Restoration Fund for immediate and urgent priorities

Allocate $400 million for immediate and urgent projects over four years

**Build an intergenerational fund that provides for generations to come**

Implement Te Tōtara Haemata Intergenerational Fund for long-term investment in marae and community development

Allocate $2 billion in a diversified portfolio to generate returns for annual distribution
