# What the page states

> The right to asylum is to be defended: anyone who seeks protection gets a fair examination, and anyone who is granted protection is to get a foothold with their family. Permanent residence permits mean people can plan, learn Swedish and enter society, while temporary permits make integration harder. Maintenance requirements for family reunification are rejected, so that the right to live with those one loves is not steered by the size of the dwelling or by income. Tax-funded welfare is the precondition for integration.

System: Permanent protection without a maintenance requirement for the family

Scenario: Anyone granted protection may stay with their family
Given temporary residence permits make integration harder
And maintenance requirements and the size of the dwelling today steer who may reunite
When anyone granted protection gets a permanent residence permit
Then people can plan their future and start to build a life
And an effort to learn Swedish and enter Swedish society becomes possible
And the family is reunited without a requirement of how much one earns or how large a dwelling one has
Outcome the right to asylum is held, and split families are not the tool
And robust welfare and policy for full employment carry integration for newcomers and for everyone else
