# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tika, ka taea hoki ngā huarahi heke, me ngā huarahi noho pūmau mō ngā manene kua roa e noho ana, tae atu ki te hunga kua pau ō rātou visa. Ka whakakorehia ngā Acceptable Standards of Health, ka taea e ngā kaipupuri visa mahi te huri kaituku mahi kāore he wā tatari, ā, ka whakawāteahia ngā pūranga whakakotahi whānau, ka whakawhānuihia ngā tika mahi a ngā hoa, ka whakahokia te kirirarau i te whānautanga. Ka tū te manene i runga i Te Tiriti, me te tuku whakatau ki ngā hapū me ngā iwi, ka piki te rahi rerenga ki te 5,000 hei te 2029, ā, ka tere ake te tukatuka tono pikihere, kāore he mauhere, he aroturuki hiko rānei.

System: He manene tika, he whakanoho rerenga hoki

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere manene me ngā rerenga
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere manene me ngā rerenga
Then ka waihangatia ngā huarahi noho pūmau mō ngā manene kua roa e noho ana, tae atu ki te hunga kua pau ō rātou visa
And ka whakakorehia ngā herenga Acceptable Standards of Health
And ka taea e ngā kaipupuri visa mahi manene te huri kaituku mahi, ā, ka tangohia te wā tatari 12 marama i waenga i ngā visa
And ka whakawāteahia ngā pūranga whakakotahi whānau o ngā rerenga, ka whakawhānuihia ngā tika mahi a ngā hoa, ka whakahokia te kirirarau i te whānautanga, ā, ka tangohia ngā tauārai moni whiwhi mō te tautoko mātua
And ka tū te manene i runga i Te Tiriti, me te tuku whakatau ki ngā hapū me ngā iwi, me te mātauranga Te Tiriti mō ngā manene me ngā rerenga
And ka piki haere te rahi rerenga ki te 5,000 hei te 2029, me ētahi atu pūtea whakanoho, me tētahi visa atawhai mō ngā whānau o ngā tāngata o Aotearoa i ngā wāhi pakanga
And ka whai rauemi a Immigration New Zealand kia whakawātea i te whakakotahi whānau taumata 1 me te taumata 2, kia tere te tukatuka tono pikihere, kia whakamutua hoki te mauhere me te aroturuki hiko o te hunga tono pikihere
Outcome ka whakakapi ngā huarahi tika ki te noho pūmau i ngā ture whakawhiwhi
