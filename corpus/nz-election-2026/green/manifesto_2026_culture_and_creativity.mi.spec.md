# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka pūteatia tika ngā ringatoi me ngā kaihanga, me ngā utu oranga i ngā kaupapa tūmatanui, me te tautoko pumau mō ngā kaupapa nui pērā i Te Matatini me Polyfest. Ka neke te pūtea toi mohoa roa i te pūtea petipeti, ka whakahokia ngā taonga i tāwāhi ki ngā iwi me ngā hapū, ā, mā te tūāhanga i waenga i ngā manatū e whakakaha te ōhanga auaha. Ka piki te pūtea mō Radio New Zealand, Whakaata Māori me ngā pāpāho hapori. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te pūtea toi, ahurea me te auaha

Scenario: Ka whakamanahia te mahere ahurea me te auaha
When ka whakamanahia e te kāwanatanga te mahere ahurea me te auaha
Then me utu te utu oranga iti rawa ki ngā ringatoi i ngā kaupapa katoa e pūteatia ana e te tūmatanui, ā, ka whānui ake ngā noho toi e utua ana
And ka tukua he pūtea pumau mō Te Urungi, Te Matatini me Polyfest
And ka tukua he pūtea toi mohoa roa, toitū, kāore e whakawhirinaki ki te pūtea petipeti, i te taha o ngā wāhi kāore he waipiro
And ka whakahokia ngā taonga i tāwāhi ki ngā iwi, ngā hapū me ngā whānau Māori
And mā te tūāhanga i waenga i ngā manatū e whakakaha te ōhanga auaha me te ahurea
And ka piki te pūtea mō Radio New Zealand me Whakaata Māori
And ka rawa ngā pāpāho me ngā reo irirangi ākonga, hapori, ā-rohe, Māori me Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
Outcome he toitūtanga mohoa roa, he whakangungu, he wāhi hoki ō ngā ringatoi
