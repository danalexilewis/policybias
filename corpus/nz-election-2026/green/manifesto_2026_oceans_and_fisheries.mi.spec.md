# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ko te mauri me te kaitiakitanga kei mua i te tango rawa ā-arumoni poto te tiaki moana. Ka whakahouhia ngā wāhi rāhui moana kia tiakina te 30% rawa o te rohe moana hei te 2030, ā, ka aukatia ngā tikanga hī ika whakamōrearea me te keri takere moana. Ka horapa ngā kāmera ki te katoa o te waka hī ika, ā, ka neke te Quota Management System ki te whakahaere i runga i te pūnaha rauropi. Kāore he nama tahua i homai.

System: Te tiaki moana me te hī ika

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere moana me te hī ika
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere moana me te hī ika
Then ka whakahouhia te ture mō ngā wāhi rāhui moana
And ka aukatia te kūmene takere ki ngā maunga moana, me te kupenga tū me te keri
And ka aukatia te keri takere moana i te rohe moana takutai me te rohe ōhanga motuhake
And ka whakaurua te kaitiakitanga, ā, ka pūteatia te iwi me te hapū kia ārahi i te tiaki moana
And ka horapa ngā kāmera ki te katoa o te waka hī ika, me ngā tepenga mate e pā ana ki te hī ika mō ngā momo rāhui
And ka whakamanahia te Agreement on Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction, me te tautoko i te tiaki moana i ngā moana nui me te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa
And ka arotakehia te Quota Management System kia anga ki te whakahaere hī ika i runga i te pūnaha rauropi
Output ka tiakina te 30% rawa o te rohe moana o Aotearoa
Outcome ka ārahi te tangata whenua i te tiaki moana i runga i te mātauranga me te tikanga
