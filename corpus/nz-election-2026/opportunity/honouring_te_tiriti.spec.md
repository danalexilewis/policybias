# What the page states

> The Crown is treated as a serious Treaty partner protecting taonga and redressing historical wrongs. If passed, the Treaty Principles Bill and Marine and Coastal Area Amendment Bill would be repealed. Aotearoa history including Te Tiriti is taught at all levels, te reo teacher numbers grow, and health and justice services devolve where outcomes are unequal. Detailed policy is still to launch.

System: Honouring Te Tiriti

Scenario: Crown Treaty settings change
When the Crown acts as a Treaty partner
Then the Treaty Principles Bill and the Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Amendment Bill are repealed if they have passed
And Aotearoa history, including Te Tiriti, is taught at all levels of the education system
And the number of qualified te reo teachers is grown so te reo has a place at school
And health and justice services are devolved, with real resources and autonomy, where outcomes are unequal
And Treaty settlements are supported to be equitable
Outcome taonga are protected and historical wrongs are redressed
