# What the page states

> Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine has thrown Europe into the worst security-policy crisis since the Second World War. Sweden's defence is in a historic shift. The effort is to be 5 percent of GDP, split between 3.5 percent military and 1.5 percent defence-related. NATO membership creates obligations. Civil defence is to be rebuilt.

System: Total defence at five percent of GDP

Scenario: Defence appropriations are to cover both weapons and civil protection
Given Sweden after decades of disarmament needs to rebuild defence from the ground up
And Sweden is a full member of NATO
When defence spending rises to the combined GDP share the page names
Then the military defence grows quickly with more soldiers, sailors and officers
And electricity supply, healthcare, transport and food supply are to function even in war
And civil conscription is one step and emergency stocks are reintroduced
And the EU steps up production of defence matériel as a complement to NATO
Output 5 percent of GDP for defence and defence-related investment
Outcome defence is to be deterrent enough to prevent attacks before they happen
And Sweden is to be able to give and receive support quickly and effectively
