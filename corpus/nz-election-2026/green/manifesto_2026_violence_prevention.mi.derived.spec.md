# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka utua e te kaupapa here a Te Aorerekura, ka whai rauemi ngā otinga tūkino whānau me te tūkino hōkakatanga nā te kaupapa Māori me te hapori, ka hara te mana-kōpeke, ā, ka whakahokia ngā ārahitanga mātauranga hononga. Kāore he tahua i tāpirihia mō te pūtea rautaki; ko te huringa Crimes Act te mīhini ōrite kua whakaingoatia anō i raro i te taurite ira.

System: Te pūtea rautaki me te ture mana-kōpeke, kāore he reriwe ratonga kua utua
