# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka noho ngā tamariki me ngā rangatahi ki te pūtake o tētahi pūnaha e whakamua ana i te whānau. Ka tuhia te reo o te tamaiti ki te ture, ka toro te Best Start ki ia tamaiti i raro i te toru tau, ā, ka whakangungua, ka rawa te kaimahi mō te tiaki e ū ana ki te ahurea. Mā te whakahou i te Ture Oranga Tamariki e tiaki ngā tamariki Māori mā te whai wāhi a te whānau me te mana o te iwi, o te hapū rānei. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te kaupapa here tamariki e whakamua ana i ngā tamariki, ngā rangatahi me te whānau

Scenario: Ka whakamanahia te mahere tamariki
When ka whakamanahia e te kāwanatanga te mahere tamariki
Then mā te ture e noho ai te reo o te tamaiti ki te pūtake, mā te Child Impact Assessment Tool, ā, mā te kōkuhu i ngā Rights of the Child ki te ture
And ka piki, ka toro te utu Best Start ki ia tamaiti i raro i te toru tau hei tautoko pūtea tamariki whānui
And ka pūteatia te whakangungu, ā, ka whakamua te rautaki whakawhanake kaimahi i ngā ara Māori, Moana-nui-a-Kiwa, āniwaniwa, hauātanga, me ngā kaiwhakarato hapori
And ka pai ake te uru ki te tātari me te tautoko mō ngā tamariki e whai hauātanga ana, e hiahia tautoko ako rānei
And ka whakahoungia te Ture Oranga Tamariki kia tiakina ngā tamariki Māori, kia kōkuhua te whai wāhi a te whānau me te mana o te iwi, o te hapū rānei
Outcome ka tautokona ngā tamariki Māori mā ngā rongoā e ū ana ki te ahurea, e ārahi ana te whānau, e hāngai ana ki te whakapapa me te tuakiri
