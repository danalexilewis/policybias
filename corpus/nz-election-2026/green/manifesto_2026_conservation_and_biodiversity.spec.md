# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover conservation law reform, a mining ban on conservation land, Jobs for Nature, wetland protection and Predator Free 2050. The page commits to protecting 30% of land and oceans and restoring at least 30% of degraded wetland area.

System: Conservation and biodiversity protection

Scenario: The conservation plan is put into effect
When the government puts the conservation plan into effect
Then conservation and wildlife laws are reformed to give effect to Te Tiriti and halt and reverse nature loss
And new mining is banned on conservation land including stewardship land
And funding for Jobs for Nature – Mahi mō te Taiao is expanded
And all remaining natural wetlands are protected
And Action for Nature and indigenous biodiversity guidance including taonga species are funded
And overall public conservation funding is increased including for Predator Free 2050
Output 30% of land and oceans protected
And at least 30% of degraded wetland area restored
Outcome native species and ecosystems are protected from mining on conservation land
