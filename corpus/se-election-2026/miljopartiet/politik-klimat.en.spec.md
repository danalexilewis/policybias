# What the page states

> The climate crisis is already felt through heatwaves, drought, floods and rising sea levels. Emissions are to come down in line with the Paris Agreement, including consumption. The state is to invest more in renewable energy, railways and climate adaptation, and households are to get cheaper sustainable travel.

System: Faster emission cuts and a just transition

Scenario: Fossil subsidies are dropped and sustainable travel becomes cheaper
Given the climate crisis is already felt through heatwaves, drought, floods and rising sea levels
And Sweden's emissions need to fall quickly
When a carbon budget is introduced and fossil subsidies are stopped
Then emissions from consumption are counted in as well
And the polluter pays
And public procurement and state-owned companies are steered towards science-based climate targets
And Klimatklivet and Industriklivet are strengthened and a recycling lift for critical raw materials is introduced
And the state takes greater responsibility for protection against cloudbursts, heatwaves, drought, fires and rising seas
Output 500 kronor a month as a cap for Sverigekortet in public transport
Outcome households with low incomes in rural and sparsely populated areas get a green rebate when the transition raises costs
And nursing homes, preschools and schools are to be fitted for long heatwaves
