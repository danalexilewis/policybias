### What we’ll do

*   Build Māori-led education authority and solutions.
*   Embed mātauranga Māori properly across learning.
*   Remove barriers that block whānau from education and training.
*   Support Māori governance and decision-making in education.

### Why it matters

Education should never teach our tamariki to leave themselves at the door. It should strengthen identity, grow confidence, open pathways, and prepare our people to lead in the world as Māori.
