# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka katia ngā āputa utu ira tangata me te iwi mā te ōritetanga utu kua whakahokia, te māramatanga me te pūrongo. Ka whakawhānuitia te Best Start ki ngā tamariki katoa i raro i te toru tau, ka tautoko ngā ratonga kotahi o te pā harakeke i te whānau i te hapūtanga me te mātua tōmua, ā, ka tere ake te taurima endometriosis mā ngā aratohu me ngā whare haumanu motuhake. Ka tutuki te tūkino whānau me te tūkino ā-ai ki te pūtea kaupapa Māori me te ārahitanga ā-hapori, ka whakaraupapahia te mana whakahaere kōkiri, ka whakahoungia te ture whakaae, ā, ka piki te wehenga mātua utua ki te 15 marama mō te kaitiaki matua.

System: Te ōritetanga ira tangata me te oranga whānau

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere ōritetanga ira tangata
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere ōritetanga ira tangata
Then ka katia ngā āputa utu ira tangata me te iwi mā te whakakore i ngā huringa ōritetanga utu, te tīmata anō i ngā kerēme, te māramatanga utu me te pūrongo āputa utu
And ka whakapikia, ka whakawhānuitia te utu Best Start ki ngā tamariki katoa i raro i te toru tau hei tautoko pūtea tamariki mō te katoa
And ka tuku ngā ratonga kotahi o te pā harakeke i ngā ratonga tāwharau i te hapūtanga me te mātua tōmua
And ka tere ake te tautuhi me te rongoā i te endometriosis me te mamae hope, mā ngā aratohu haumanu me ngā whare haumanu motuhake
And ka pūteatia ngā rongoā tūkino whānau me te tūkino ā-ai e ārahitia ana e te kaupapa Māori me te hapori
And ka whakatikahia te Crimes Act kia whakaraupapa te mana whakahaere kōkiri, kia whakatika hoki te ture whakaae, tae atu ki te aukati i te whakaae kōroiroi
And ka piki te wehenga mātua utua ki te 15 marama mō te kaitiaki matua, me te wehenga hoa kua rāhuihia me ngā utu kua piki
Outcome ka whakahekehia te tūkino ā-ira me te koretake ōhanga
