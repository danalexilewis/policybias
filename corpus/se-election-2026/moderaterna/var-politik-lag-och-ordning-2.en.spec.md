# What the page states

> Ordinary people should not have to stay indoors because dangerous people are out and about. Victims of crime should receive redress. Municipalities should get security guards, forfeited proceeds should go to victims and violence against women should be met with new offences.

System: Security guards, forfeiture fund and protection for women

Scenario: A municipality faces public order disturbances without police
Given people around the country are affected by public order disturbances that erode safety in the local environment
When a new professional category of security guards is introduced
Then municipalities should receive greatly expanded tools against public order disturbances, volume crime and local insecurity
Outcome security guards should be able to complement police and municipal enforcement officers

Scenario: A victim of crime should receive damages and women should receive protection
Given 46,000 people have unpaid debts of over 10 billion kronor in criminal damages with the Enforcement Authority
And roughly 15 women are murdered each year by a current or former partner
And one in four women is subjected to psychological or physical violence in a close relationship at some point
When a forfeiture fund is introduced and economic and psychological violence is criminalised
Then forfeited criminal proceeds should go to support for victims of crime and crime prevention
And the offence of particularly aggravated rape should be introduced with life imprisonment in the sentencing scale
And the patient fee should be abolished for women seeking care after rape
Outcome victims of crime and women should be met with stronger protection and redress
