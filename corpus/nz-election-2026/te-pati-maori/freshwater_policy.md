### What we’ll do

*   Assert Māori rights and authority over freshwater.
*   Stop decisions being made without Māori consent.
*   Ensure resources are protected and benefits are shared fairly.

### Why it matters

Water is life. It carries whakapapa, sustains our whenua, feeds our people, and binds generations together. Decisions about wai must sit with those who have carried responsibility for it since time immemorial.
