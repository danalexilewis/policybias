# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka neke te kaupapa here waipiro me ētahi atu tarukino i te whiu ki te hauora me te whakaiti tūkino. Ka whakakapia te Misuse of Drugs Act ki te uru cannabis e waeturehia ana, te whakawhānui i te whakaiti tūkino me te tautoko waranga, te raihana rongoā hinengaro, me ngā ture Good Samaritan me te naloxone me ngā wāhi kai i raro i te tirotiro hauora. Ka heke haere te wātea o te nicotine, ka neke te ture tarukino i te taraiwa ki te whakamātautau ngoikoretanga, ā, ka whakamutua haeretia ngā pānui hokohoko me ngā tautoko waitohu waipiro, me te pūtea reeti waipiro mō te whakaiti tūkino. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

And ka whakapikia te pūtea mō te whakaiti tūkino me te tautoko waranga, tae atu ki ngā hōtaka kaupapa Māori, rainbow me ngā taiohi

And ka paahitia ngā ture Good Samaritan, ā, ka utua te wātea o te naloxone me ngā wāhi kai i raro i te tirotiro hauora
And ka utua ngā ratonga whakamutu vape me te momi hikareti, ka heke haere te wātea o te nicotine, ā, ka kaha ake te waeture kaihoko

And ka whakamutua haeretia ngā pānui hokohoko me ngā tautoko waitohu waipiro, ā, mā te reeti waipiro e utu tētahi kauhau ā-motu whakaiti tūkino
