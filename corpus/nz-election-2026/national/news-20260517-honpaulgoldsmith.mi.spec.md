> Ka aukatihia ngā kaiwhakawā ki te whakaaro i te āhuatanga pai hei take whakangāwari i te whakawhiu mō ngā hara ā-moe katoa. E kore e taea e ngā kōrero āhuatanga a ngā kaiako, ngā kaituku mahi me te whānau te whakaheke i ngā whakawhiu i ēnei keehi. Ka hanga tēnei panoni i runga i ngā whakahou hekenga whakawhiu, pehi ingoa me te whaiwhai o mua.
