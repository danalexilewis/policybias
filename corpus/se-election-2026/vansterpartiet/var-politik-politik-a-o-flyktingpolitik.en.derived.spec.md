# Our understanding

> Permanent status lets a household plan; dropping the income and dwelling tests lets relatives arrive without those gates. Integration is then asked of the same tax-funded welfare that already serves everyone, with no count of extra homes, school places or caseworkers.

System: Rights expansion carried by an unsized welfare residual

Scenario: Reunion proceeds without an income or dwelling gate
Given family reunion today can fail on pay or the size of the home
When those tests are removed for people granted protection
Then more relatives become eligible at once
Outcome crowding and rent land on the local housing stock
But a waiting family still has no stated extra dwelling

Scenario: Permanence is asked to do the work of an integration programme
Given temporary permits are blamed for weak settlement
When permits become permanent
Then a person can plan Swedish study and work
Outcome the welfare model is the integration machine for newcomers and for those already here
But language teaching, jobs and case time remain uncounted
