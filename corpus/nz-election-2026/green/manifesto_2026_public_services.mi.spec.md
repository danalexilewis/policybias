# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka noho ngā ratonga tūmatanui ki ngā ringa tūmatanui, ka ū Te Tiriti puta noa, ā, ka pūteatia ngā rōpū Māori mō ngā kaupapa ā-rohe. Ko te manawaroa me te oranga roa te aronga o te whakahounga pūtea tūmatanui, ehara i te tapahi poto. Ka whakahokia te tūranga aroturuki a te Children's Commissioner, ā, ka whiwhi te rāngai hapori i te pūtea haumaru, ngāwari, me te pai ake o te utu me te whakangungu i te rāngai tūmatanui. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Ngā ratonga tūmatanui me te pūtea tūmatanui

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere ratonga tūmatanui
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere ratonga tūmatanui
Then ka ū Te Tiriti puta noa i ngā ratonga tūmatanui, ā, ka pūteatia ngā rōpū Māori, te iwi, te hapū me ngā marae mō ngā kaupapa ā-rohe
And ka noho ngā ratonga tūmatanui me ngā kamupene nā te kāwanatanga i te pūtea tika, ki ngā ringa tūmatanui
And ka whakahouhia te pūnaha pūtea tūmatanui mō te manawaroa roa, te kaha whakaputa me te oranga
And ka whakahokia te tūranga aroturuki a te Children's Commissioner i te taha o te māngai
And ka pai ake te pupuri kaimahi mā te ahunga umanga, te utu, ngā tuku mahi me te whakangungu kua whakatauritea, me te utu ōrite mō ngā tūranga ōrite
And ka whiwhi te rāngai hapori i te pūtea katoa, haumaru, ngāwari
And ka pai ake te whakangungu Te Tiriti me te reo mō ngā kaimahi tūmatanui katoa
And ka ārahi ngā ratonga tūmatanui i te mahi e urupare ana ki te hauātanga
Outcome ka whakamahia te oranga roa ki te whakahaere tahua
