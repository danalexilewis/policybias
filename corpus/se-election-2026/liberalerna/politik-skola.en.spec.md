# What the page states

> Knowledge, high expectations and order in the classroom are the core of school. A mobile-phone ban and a focus on reading, writing and mathematics with real books are already in place. What remains is a class cap, stretch for those who are ahead, phasing out profit, and moving municipal schools to the state.

System: A knowledge school with state main responsibility

Scenario: A class where study calm fails and screens set the pace
Given many pupils leave compulsory school without sufficient knowledge in reading and mathematics
And screen time is rising and more and more young people feel unwell
And a mobile-phone ban during the school day has already been introduced
When a cap on pupils per class is introduced and municipal schools are taken into state hands
Then smaller classes give more study calm and better school results
And pupils who repeatedly disrupt teaching get support in separate help classes
And pupils who are well ahead get stretch classes and advanced groups
And tablets and pupil computers are removed in preschool and the early years
And everyone has a right to real textbooks
And the profit motive in the independent-school sector is phased out while choice remains
And responsibility moves from municipalities to the state so that quality does not differ too much between different parts of Sweden
And teachers spend the time on teaching with more teaching assistants and stronger support from pupil health
Outcome school is marked by order and high expectations of every pupil
And municipal politicians cannot deprioritise school
