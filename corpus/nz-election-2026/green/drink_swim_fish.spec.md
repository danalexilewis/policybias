# What the page states

> Water from mountain glaciers to wild coasts shapes life across Aotearoa. The rules protecting water would be fixed so people can drink, swim and fish in the places they love. The Drink Swim Fish programme names three of eight interventions: stronger drinking-water limits, tighter wastewater discharge rules, and a phase-out of destructive bottom trawling. Numeric limits, budgets and dates are not given.
