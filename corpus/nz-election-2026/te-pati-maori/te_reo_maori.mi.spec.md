# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakakaha, ka whakanoho noatia te reo Māori ki ngā wāhi katoa. Ka haumitia ngā ara ako i te reo me te whakarauora nā te hapori e ārahi ana. Ka herea te kāwanatanga kia tiakina te reo Māori.

System: Te whakarauora i te reo Māori

Scenario: Ka whakakaha, ka whakanoho noatia te reo Māori
Given ko te reo Māori te tuakiri, te mahara, te mana, me te oranga
When ka whakakaha, ka whakanoho noatia te reo Māori ki ngā wāhi katoa
Then ka haumitia ngā ara ako i te reo me te whakarauora nā te hapori e ārahi ana
And ka herea te kāwanatanga kia tiakina te reo Māori
Outcome ka puāwai te reo, ā, ka pono ake te motu ki tōna ake āhua
