# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakanoho te kaupapa here i tētahi Digital Duty of Care ki ngā pae, ka whakatakoto ture AI, ka kaha ki te kawe hua hangarau ao ki tāwāhi, ka tango i te kāwanatanga raraunga Māori puta noa i te rāngai tūmatanui, ā, ka whakakaha i ngā whiu tūmataiti. Kāore he taipitopito kawenga, he taputapu tāke, he taumata whiu rānei i tāpirihia.

System: Te kawenga pae kāore he taputapu kua tau

Scenario: Ka pā ki ngā pae tētahi Digital Duty of Care
Given ko ngā ihirangi kino e whara ana i te hauora hinengaro me te whakapono, i te wā e noho ana ngā pae hei hanganga matihiko taketake
When ka tono tētahi Digital Duty of Care i te mārama pūnaha, i ngā pūnaha amuamu, i tētahi kaiwhakahaere ā-motu motuhake
Then me whakautu ngā pae mō te whara i raro i tētahi kawenga kua whakaingoatia
Outcome ka whiwhi ngā kaiwhakamahi i tētahi huarahi amuamu me tētahi kerēme mārama
But ka whakamāori tonu ngā pae i te tiaki ā rātou ake kia tuhi rā anō te kaiwhakahaere i ngā kawenga me ngā whiu

Scenario: Ka tākea kaha ake ngā hua hangarau ao kei tāwāhi
When ka kaha te patu i te karo tāke me te kawe hua ki tāwāhi a ngā kamupene hangarau ao pērā i a Meta, Amazon me Microsoft
Then me noho nui ake ngā hua o aua kamupene ki te pūtake tāke o Aotearoa
Outcome ka taea e te Karauna te kerēme i tētahi wāhanga nui ake o te moni whiwhi pae
But kāore anō kia whakaingoatia he taputapu mā Te Tari Taake hei kohi i te moni whiwhi tāpiri

Scenario: Ka tangohia te kāwanatanga raraunga Māori puta noa i te rāngai tūmatanui
When ka tangohia te Māori Data Governance Model me te Māori AI Governance Framework a Te Kāhui Raraunga puta noa i te rāngai tūmatanui
Then me whakahaere ngā tari Karauna i ngā raraunga Māori i raro i aua pou tarāwaho
Outcome kei ngā raraunga Māori tētahi huinga ture ā-rāngai tūmatanui
But kāore ngā pae tūmataiti e here aunoatia e te tango ā-rāngai tūmatanui anake
