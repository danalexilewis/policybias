# What the page states

> Organised crime is described as a threat to society and ordinary people. Before the next election, gang crime is to carry double sentences, and the first of two constitutional decisions is to be taken to make it illegal to belong to a criminal organisation. Sentences have already been tightened for several gang offences, and special aggravating rules apply to violent settlements that risk hitting innocents.

System: Tougher sentences and banned gang membership

Scenario: A gang crime is committed before the next election
Given organised crime is a threat to society and ordinary people
And sentences have been tightened for fraud, assault, bribery, forgery and smuggling committed by gang criminals
And special aggravating rules aim at violent settlements that risk hitting innocents
When double sentences are introduced for crimes committed by gangs and the first constitutional decision is taken
Then it is to become illegal to belong to a criminal organisation once both constitutional decisions have been taken
And anyone who is a gang criminal is to be inside
Outcome the fight against the gangs is sharpened through sentences and a coming membership ban
