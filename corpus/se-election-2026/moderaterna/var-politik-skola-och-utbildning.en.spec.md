# What the page states

> The knowledge school is to be restored with high expectations, skilled teachers and study calm. New grades and curricula are to give a fairer picture of knowledge. Teachers are to have time to teach. Swedish is to weigh more heavily, and mother-tongue instruction is to be reformed.

System: A knowledge school with new grades and more Swedish

Scenario: A pupil leaves compulsory school without eligibility for upper secondary
Given one in six pupils leaves compulsory school without eligibility for upper secondary school
And that corresponds to 800 school classes each year
When a new grading system and new curricula with a knowledge focus are introduced
Then screening in the early years is to be expanded so that support comes in time
And support teaching in smaller groups is to become easier to offer
Output introduction from the autumn term 2028
Outcome effort is to pay and grades become more equivalent across the country

Scenario: A trained teacher does not work in school
Given 35 000 trained teachers do not work in school
And almost 30 percent of teachers in compulsory school are unqualified
And four in ten teacher students do not graduate
When teachers' teaching duty is regulated again
Then all early-years teachers are to be offered in-service training in evidence-based reading instruction
And teacher education receives new content and raised entry requirements
Output a regulated teaching duty from autumn 2027
And reformed teacher education during 2027 and 2028
Outcome teachers are to be able to be teachers with a concrete method for teaching children to read

Scenario: Study calm is lacking and Swedish weighs unevenly
Given every other schoolchild aged 10-16 has been exposed to abuse or bullying
And every third teacher has been exposed to abuse, threats or violence
And six in ten teachers in after-school centres have been exposed, almost 15 000 teachers
And 314 000 compulsory-school pupils have a right to mother-tongue instruction
And mother-tongue instruction costs about 2.2 billion kronor a year
And at 20 of 30 inspected preschools the staff spoke too poor Swedish
When head teachers' and teachers' mandate to ensure safety and study calm is made clear
Then expectation documents with rules and consequences are to be introduced at every school
And Swedish as a second language is abolished and replaced with basic language instruction
And mother-tongue instruction is reformed with a requirement for licensed teachers
And language and suitability tests are introduced at hiring in preschool and school
Output a clearer mandate and the possibility to suspend pupils from autumn 2026
Outcome every child is to have the same chance to master the Swedish language
