# What the page states

> Family and sexual violence prevention centres Te Aorerekura with kaupapa Māori and community-led solutions resourced. The Crimes Act criminalises coercive control and reforms consent while relationship and sexuality education guidelines are restored in all schools. Counselling and mental health support increases for everyone engaging with the justice system. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Family and sexual violence prevention

Scenario: The violence prevention plan is put into effect
When the government puts the violence prevention plan into effect
Then the Te Aorerekura action plan is funded centred on Te Tiriti
And kaupapa Māori and community-led family and sexual violence solutions are resourced for women, Pasifika, rainbow and disabled communities
And workforce and public understanding of violence drivers and safe response improves
And the Crimes Act is amended to criminalise coercive control and amend consent including precluding reluctant consent
And relationship and sexuality education guidelines on healthy relationships and consent are restored in all schools
And counselling and mental health support increases for victims, whānau, witnesses, defendants and legal professionals
Outcome communities lead culturally grounded safety solutions
