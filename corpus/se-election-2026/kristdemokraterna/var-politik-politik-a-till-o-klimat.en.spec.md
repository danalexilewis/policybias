# What the page states

> The climate question is described as the greatest challenge of our time. For climate neutrality, coal is to be phased out of the EU's electricity production at once, and tariffs aimed at countries' use of coal power outside the EU. Effort is to increase on biofuels, new nuclear power, emissions trading, electrification of the vehicle fleet, and capture and storage of carbon dioxide.
