# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakatūria he mana ture e ārahitia ana e te Māori, me ngā otinga ā-hapori. Ka whakahoungia ngā ture e whakahara ana i te pōharatanga me te whaiora. Ka neke te kaupapa here i te mauhere nui ki te hurihanga wā-roa.
