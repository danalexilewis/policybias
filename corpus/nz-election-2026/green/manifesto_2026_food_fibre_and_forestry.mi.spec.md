# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka neke te kai, te muka me te ngahere i te hua pokerepū kaha ki te mahi whakahou e tiaki ana i ngā wai me ngā hapori. Ka whiwhi ngā kaipāmu i te pūtea whakawhiti, ka whakamahia te wūru me ētahi atu muka māori ki ngā whare tūmatanui, ka aukatihia te palm kernel expeller, ā, mā ngā here tuatua katoa me tētahi National Soil Strategy e tiaki te whenua mōrearea ki te horo. Ka whānui ake te tukatuka rākau ā-rohe me te ngahere e ārahitia ana e te Māori i runga i te whenua Māori, me tētahi kaupapa whiwhinga kanorau koiora hei kōwhiringa ETS ā-noho. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te kai, te muka me te ngahere whakahou

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere kai, muka me te ngahere
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere kai, muka me te ngahere
Then ka tautoko ngā pūtea tārewa iti-hua me ngā takuhe i ngā kaipāmu e neke ana ki te mahi toitū ake
And ka whakamahia ngā muka māori i whakatipuria ki Aotearoa, tae atu ki te wūru, ki ngā kura me ngā whare tūmatanui, me tētahi pūtea whakataetae mō te hangarau muka toitū
And ka aukatihia te palm kernel expeller, ā, me tiki toitū te kai tāpiri
And ka whakatinanahia ngā here tuatua katoa, te whakawhiti whenua mōrearea-horo ki te ngahere taketake, me tētahi National Soil Strategy
And ka hanga te tukatuka rākau ā-rohe i ngā hua rākau nui ake te uara me ngā biofuel mai i te toenga tuatua
And ka tautokona ngā rōpū ngahere e ārahitia ana e te Māori i runga i te whenua Māori, ā, ka whakamanahia ngā mōtika whakamahi tuku iho
And ka tuku tētahi kaupapa whiwhinga kanorau koiora i tētahi kōwhiringa moni ā-noho ki te Emissions Trading Scheme, e whakaarotau ana i te whenua Māori taha, ina whakaaetia
Outcome ka neke te rāngai i te hua pokerepū kaha ki te oranga wā roa
