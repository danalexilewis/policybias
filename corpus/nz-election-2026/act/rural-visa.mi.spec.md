# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kāore ngā huarahi uruwhenua ā-kaupeka e whakakī i te hapa kaimahi pāmu puta noa i te tau e pupuri ana i ngā ahumahi kaweake. Ka here te Rural Workforce Visa i ngā kaimahi ki ngā kaituku mahi taiwhenua whai tohu mō ngā wā e toru tau, me te whakahou pānui-anō-ka-tuku-anō, me tētahi huarahi Work to Residence i muri i ngā tau e ono tāpiri, ka wehe i te tāke hanganga. Kāore he paearu tohu, he rā tīmata rānei i homai.

System: Rural Workforce Visa mō ngā kaituku mahi o te rāngai tuatahi

Scenario: Ka whakakī tētahi kaituku mahi taiwhenua whai tohu i tētahi tūranga pāmu puta noa i te tau ki tētahi kaimahi manene
Given $60 piriona te kaweake a ngā pāmu, ngā māra hua rākau me ngā waka hī ika o Aotearoa ia tau
And kei te pā ki ngā hapori taiwhenua ngā hapa kaimahi roa, ā, kāore te pūnaha manene o nāianei i te rite ki te whakaoti
And kāore ngā huarahi uruwhenua ā-kaupeka e whakatika i ngā tūranga puta noa i te tau e kawe ana i ngā pāmu ia rā
When ka waihangatia te Rural Workforce Visa hei uruwhenua motuhake e toru tau mō te mahi miraka kau, hipi me te kau mīti, me te mahi pāmu whānui
Then ka herea te uruwhenua ki ngā kaituku mahi taiwhenua whai tohu, ehara ki tētahi rohe whenua
Output ka taea e ngā kaimahi te neke ki tētahi atu kaituku mahi taiwhenua whai tohu, kāore he tono hou, engari kāore e taea te neke ki ngā rāngai ehara i te taiwhenua
And he wā tuatahi e toru tau, kāore he whakahou e hiahiatia ana i roto i tērā wā
And i mua i te mutunga o te wā uruwhenua ka pānui anō ngā kaituku mahi, ā, mēnā kāore he tāngata o Aotearoa e tika ana, ka tukuna anō te uruwhenua mō ētahi tau e toru anō
Outcome mā tētahi awa ahuwhenua hou i runga i te Work to Residence Visa e āhei ngā kaimahi kua pupuri i te RWV mō ngā 72 marama tāpiri ki tētahi kaituku mahi whai tohu, ā, e tutuki ana i ngā herenga paerewa, ki te noho
And ka wehe te Rural Workforce Visa i te tāke hanganga ki ngā wāhi kāore e whai tikanga te tono i ngā hapori taiwhenua
