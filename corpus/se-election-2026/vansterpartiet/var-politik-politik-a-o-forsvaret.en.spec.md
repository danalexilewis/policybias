# What the page states

> The security situation is more serious than it has been for a very long time: the war against Ukraine, cyber attacks, natural disasters, crime and influence operations. Security policy is to rest on peaceful solutions and prevent crises, with welfare as the floor. A modern defence with general gender-neutral conscription and more conscripts is to give popular roots. NATO membership does not replace Sweden's own total defence. A new law is to ban nuclear weapons in Sweden, the Nordic region is to be a nuclear-weapon-free zone, and permanent bases with soldiers from other countries are not to exist.

System: Sweden's own total defence with a nuclear-weapons ban

Scenario: Conscription and a nuclear-weapons law meet a sharpened situation
Given Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine, cyber attacks, division, crime and false news
And socially important activities need resources already in a normal situation in order to manage crisis and, at worst, war
And both military defence and civil defence need more resources, as do many other parts of society
When general gender-neutral conscription is expanded and a law bans nuclear weapons in Sweden
Then defence is made up of people with different backgrounds and personnel supply is strengthened
And the Nordic region is named as a nuclear-weapon-free zone
And permanent bases with foreign soldiers fail to appear
Outcome a popularly rooted total defence that is to keep Sweden and its citizens safe even with NATO membership
And an active independent foreign policy for democracy, international law, women's emancipation and global social justice
But when conflicts arise between defence, civil defence and welfare, a whole-system view is to steer
