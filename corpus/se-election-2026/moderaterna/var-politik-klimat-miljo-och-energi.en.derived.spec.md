# Our understanding

> The state takes risk in new nuclear power so that households should not have to steer life by capacity charges, but tearing down was fast and building takes years. The budget's emission reduction is not enough for ESR 2030.

System: State risk in baseload against households' capacity charges

Scenario: The capacity shortfall in the south is met with reactors that do not yet exist
Given southern Sweden lost 3,800 MW of dispatchable electricity production
When the state steps in with financing, regulation and ownership
Then new nuclear power should in time supply the south
Outcome households are promised not to have to plan around capacity charges
But the years to ground-breaking are borne as German prices in electricity areas three and four

Scenario: The 2030 emissions target is not met with fuel tax
When the budget lowers emissions without raising petrol prices
Then the ESR commitment to 2030 becomes hard to reach
Output 1.5 million tonnes lower emissions by 2030
Outcome the remaining reduction is pushed onto costlier measures or later years
But car-owning households avoid the price signal that would have borne ESR
