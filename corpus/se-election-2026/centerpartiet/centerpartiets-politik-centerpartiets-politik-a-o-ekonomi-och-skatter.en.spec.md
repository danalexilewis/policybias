# What the page states

> Prosperity does not reach everyone. Gaps are growing between town and country and between those with a job and those without. For many firms it is too expensive and complicated to hire, and for many people work pays too little. Tax should be low on work and enterprise and higher on things that harm the environment.

System: Tax shift from work to environmental harm

Scenario: A small firm considers hiring someone far from the labour market
Given gaps are growing between town and country and between those with a job and those without
And it is too expensive and complicated for many firms to hire
And work pays too little for many people
When the costs of hiring are lowered, especially for small firms and for people far from the labour market
Then a green tax shift cuts tax on jobs and enterprise and is financed by higher taxes on environmental damage
And tax is cut for low and middle earners so more people keep more in their pocket
And the tax system and the rules are simplified so it is easier to start and run a firm anywhere in the country
Outcome more jobs are created across the country, those who work keep more money, and it becomes profitable to invest in green solutions
