# What the page states

> Care is to be given according to need, not according to a private health-insurance policy. Profit-seeking in care prioritises profitable patients over those who need care most, so profit-seeking firms are not to run core services with tax money. More care places, a better working environment and skills development, mental support at every health centre, and outreach work in socially vulnerable areas.

System: Care according to need without profit in the core service

Scenario: Profit-seeking care is shut out of the core service
Given large differences in health and access to care depending on where one lives, class, sex and age
And the hunt for profit prioritises patients who are most profitable to treat
And Sweden sits at the bottom of the EU when it comes to care places
When profit-seeking firms are shut out of healthcare's core services
Then care is given according to need instead of according to private health insurance
And primary care becomes more accessible and every health centre can offer mental support
And the working environment and the chance of skills development improve so that more people want to stay
Outcome more care places and stronger preventive public-health work
And outreach health checks in socially vulnerable areas, not least among women with short education
And particular efforts in sparsely populated regions where the costs of running healthcare are especially high
