# What the page states

> People flee war and oppression, and others come here to work. Today's system often leads to long waits, exclusion and uncertainty. The right of asylum is to be protected, work is the best path to integration, and firms need foreign skills. The EU is to take a common and solidarity-based responsibility.

System: Work as the way in and shorter asylum processing

Scenario: A firm wants to hire someone from a country outside the EU
Given today's system often leads to long waits, exclusion and uncertainty
And the right of asylum is a fundamental humanitarian principle that must be protected
And Sweden needs foreign skills for firms to be able to grow
When it becomes easier for firms to hire labour from other countries, for both high- and low-skilled jobs
Then newcomers are to get faster routes into work and self-sufficiency
And the asylum process is to be legally secure and efficient with short processing times
And the policy is to be long-term and predictable so that both people and municipalities know what applies
Outcome exclusion falls when newcomers quickly enter work, and firms get the skills they need
