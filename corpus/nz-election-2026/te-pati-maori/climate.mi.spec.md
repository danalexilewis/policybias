# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tiakina te whenua me te moana i ngā ahumahi tango rawa, tae atu ki te keri takere moana. Ka whakangaohia te manawaroa āhuarangi mō ngā hapori e mōrearea rawa ana. Ka tautokona ngā huarahi whakaora, toitū hoki, e hāngai ana ki ngā uara Māori, kia noho haumaru, mana motuhake ai ngā whānau i runga i ō rātou ake whenua.

System: Te tiaki āhuarangi e hāngai ana ki ngā uara Māori

Scenario: Ka tiakina te whenua me te moana i ngā ahumahi tango rawa
Given ka whakaarohia te whenua, te awa, te moana, te marae, ngā wāhi tapu, te mahinga kai me te whakapapa hei taonga tuku iho, hei kawenga hoki
When ka tiakina te whenua me te moana i ngā ahumahi tango rawa, tae atu ki te keri takere moana
Then ka whakangaohia te manawaroa āhuarangi mō ngā hapori e mōrearea rawa ana
And ka tautokona ngā huarahi whakaora, toitū hoki, e hāngai ana ki ngā uara Māori
Outcome ka taea e ngā whānau te noho haumaru, utu ngāwari, mana motuhake hoki i runga i ō rātou ake whenua
