# What the page states

> Half of rivers are unswimmable and unique biodiversity is at risk. Conservation and biosecurity funding rises for wetlands and native reforestation, water quality gets independent oversight, and landowners are helped to shift to high-value low-impact uses. A nature market pays for biodiversity alongside continuous-cover native forestry and a circular plastics economy aligned to the industry roadmap. Detailed policy is still to launch.

System: Healthy Land

Scenario: Land, water and nature rules change
Given half of rivers unswimmable and unique biodiversity at risk
When conservation and biosecurity funding is increased, with a focus on wetlands, native reforestation and other biodiversity-rich projects
Then water quality is improved through strong regulation and independent oversight
And landowners are supported to shift to high-value, low-impact uses through spatial planning, nature-positive practice and technology that cuts pesticide and artificial fertiliser use
And continuous-cover native forestry and a nature market that pays for biodiversity are invested in
And a circular plastics economy is designed to the New Plastics Economy Roadmap
Outcome purpose-led businesses can grow wealth by restoring nature
