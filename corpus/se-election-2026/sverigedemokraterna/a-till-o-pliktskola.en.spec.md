# What the page states

> Every child present in Sweden is to be covered by compulsory schooling and receive education. Checks are to be strengthened so that children take part in teaching and guardians take their responsibility. The page does not say how the check is carried out.

System: Compulsory schooling for children present in Sweden

Scenario: A child present in the country stays away from teaching
When checks are strengthened that children take part in teaching and that guardians take their responsibility
Then every child present in Sweden is to be covered by compulsory schooling and have access to education
And compulsory schooling is to be upheld
Outcome compulsory schooling is followed, not only prescribed
