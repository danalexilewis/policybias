# Health access and medicines

Reduces health-tagged party dump pages into proposed events versus status-quo framing.

## labour

### Given

One in six New Zealanders cannot afford a GP visit; some practice fees approach $100 (`medicard.md`). General practice has been underfunded by about 7.6% (~$137 million against $1.67 billion practice income) under the enrolment-plus-co-payment model (`medicard.md`). Free prescriptions currently apply only under-14, 65+, or with a Community Services Card; 155,000 people did not collect prescribed medicine last year because of cost (`prescriptions.md`). Maternity scans cost up to $90 each and are free only in some regions (`maternityscans.md`). Cervical cancer is largely preventable but 85% of those diagnosed were never screened or missed screenings (`cervicalscreening.md`). Around 300,000 people are not enrolled with a practice; some areas have closed GP books (`familydoctorloanscheme.md`).

### When

Issue every New Zealander a Medicard for three free doctor or nurse visits per enrolled practice per year (no rollover; excludes after-hours, ACC, and already-free services); card-and-app from birth or on eligibility (`medicard.md`). Remove the $5 prescription co-payment from 1 July 2027 for everyone entitled to free public healthcare (`prescriptions.md`). Make cervical screening free for ages 25–69 from 1 October 2027 via Medicard (`cervicalscreening.md`). Make every referred maternity scan free nationwide from July 2028; load entitlement on Medicard after GP, midwife, or specialist referral (`maternityscans.md`). Stand up an Independent Pricing Authority by July 2028 to set a national GP funding rate; Health NZ must fund rate increases the following year; co-payment revenue shifts into practice funding (`medicard.md`, `familydoctorloanscheme.md`). Free up claimed ~4.5 million GP appointments a year via clinical phone triage, admin technology, long-term-condition advice, and clinic-space grants; telehealth triage resolved cases do not count against the three visits (`medicard.md`). Offer up to 50 Family Doctor Loans a year from 1 July 2027: up to 90% of buy-in cost capped at $500,000, interest-free two years then 3% on the balance, ten-year term, owner-operated practices only, via the Small Business Cashflow Loan Scheme (`familydoctorloanscheme.md`). Fund Medicard package revenue from a 28% tax on profit on commercial or residential property sales after 1 July 2027 (family home and listed exemptions excluded) — see `capitalgainstax.md` for the tax instrument only.

### Money

Medicard and supporting initiatives: $393.3 million in 2027/28, rising to about $553 million a year once fully in place ($323m free visits, $20m Medicard/app, $30m triage, $4.3m digital innovation, $5m self-care, $6m pricing authority, $5m facilities in later years) (`medicard.md`). Prescriptions: $74.5 million a year — funder pointer `labour/capitalgainstax.md`. Cervical screening: $21.6 million first full year from existing health baselines (`cervicalscreening.md`). Maternity scans: $28.6 million a year — funder pointer `labour/capitalgainstax.md`; 24 paid trainee sonographer positions under $3 million a year from Health NZ baselines (`maternityscans.md`). Family Doctor Loan Scheme: if fully subscribed, $25 million a year lent with forgone interest under $8 million a year; administrative cost from existing baselines (`familydoctorloanscheme.md`).

## act

### Given

New Zealand spends 4.9% of its health budget on medicines versus Australia's 12.2% and an OECD average of 13.3%; Australia funds 142 modern medicines not funded here, 38% of them cancer medicines (`closing-medicines-gap.md`). Medicines on Pharmac's Options for Investment list often remain unfunded when the medicines budget is exhausted (`closing-medicines-gap.md`). One in four adults cites appointment wait time as a barrier to seeing a GP; more than 1,000 community pharmacies are under-used (`let-pharmacies-do-more.md`). The End of Life Choice Act's six-month prognosis rule is cited as the biggest access barrier for assisted dying (`strengthening-end-of-life-choice.md`).

### When

Increase the medicines share of the health budget by one percentage point each year until parity with Australia in 2033; pre-commit Pharmac's future funding increases; fund from expected health-budget growth without new taxes or cutting non-medicines health (`closing-medicines-gap.md`). Widen pharmacy treatment of everyday conditions (chest, ear, skin infections, acute pain, sore throats, tonsillitis, school sores, styes, gout, osteoarthritis, vaginal thrush, period pain, STI self-swab); let accredited pharmacists monitor stable hypertension, type 2 diabetes, and high cholesterol within GP-set limits and order blood tests; pilot pharmacy skin-lesion checks with dermatoscope and AI risk tool; remove the five-pharmacy ownership cap and proximity restrictions; enable 24-hour prescription lockers (`let-pharmacies-do-more.md`). Implement all 25 Ministry of Health statutory-review recommendations on the End of Life Choice Act; replace the six-month prognosis with an advanced progressive condition expected to cause death; allow a limited waiver of final consent when capacity is lost after a competent choice of date (`strengthening-end-of-life-choice.md`).

### Money

Medicines gap: one percentage point of health budget reallocated to medicines each year — **sketch** (no total dollar envelope; Seymour cites Budget 2026 Pharmac receiving $13.5 million versus ~$320 million more under the policy as illustration only) (`closing-medicines-gap.md`). Pharmacy scope and end-of-life amendments — unstated (`let-pharmacies-do-more.md`, `strengthening-end-of-life-choice.md`).
