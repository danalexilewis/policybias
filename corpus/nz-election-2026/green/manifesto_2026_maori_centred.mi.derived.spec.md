# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka neke te mana me ngā rawa ki te taha o te tangata whenua, ka haumi ki ngā rerekētanga, ka tautoko tētahi Marae Resilience Fund i ngā mahi nā te marae i ārahi me ngā ohorere taupori, ka mutu ngā rīhi mutunga-kore i runga i te whenua Māori, ā, ka kaha ake ngā whakamātautau Public Works Act e tata ana ki ngā marae me ngā wāhi tapu. Kāore he nama i ngā tāra tahua, i ngā hinonga ka riro, i ngā here whakawhiti rīhi.

System: Te tahua kua whakaingoatia kāore he nui, me te tuku mana kāore te pito ka riro

Scenario: Ka tū te Marae Resilience Fund
When ka pūteatia e tētahi Marae Resilience Fund ngā kaupapa nā te marae i ārahi
And ka tautoko te pūtea haere tonu i ngā marae hei here ohorere taupori
Then kei ngā marae tētahi hongere pūtea kua whakaingoatia mō ngā mahi nā te tikanga i ārahi me ngā ohorere
Outcome kei ngā marae tētahi tahua kua whakaingoatia mō te manawaroa nā te tikanga i ārahi me ngā here ohorere
But kei te tatari ngā komiti marae ki a Te Tai Ōhanga mō te nui o te wehewehenga ā-tau
Activates green-te-waonui-a-taane

Scenario: Ka tukuna te mana me ngā rawa
When ka tohatohatia anō te mana me ngā rawa ki te tangata whenua hei kaipupuri whakatau mō te whānau, te hapū me te iwi
Then ko ngā rōpū Māori te hunga e kīia ana ka pupuri i taua mahi
Outcome ko te tikanga kei te whānau, te hapū me te iwi ngā whakatau me ngā rawa
But kei te whakahaere tonu ngā āpiha i ngā ratonga kia riro rā anō tētahi tinana ture me tētahi rārangi Tahua

Scenario: Ka mutu ngā rīhi mutunga-kore, he kounga te tata Public Works Act
When ka mutu ngā rīhi mutunga-kore i runga i te whenua Māori
And ka ārai te Public Works Act i te rironga o te whenua Māori pupuri-kore me te whenua tikanga, me te whenua e tata e tika ana ki ngā marae, ngā urupā me ngā wāhi tapu
Then ka ngaro i ngā kaipupuri rīhi te pupuri mutunga-kore, ā, ka pā tētahi paepae hou ki te rironga e tata ana ki ngā wāhi tapu
Outcome ka pā ki te Karauna tētahi paepae teitei ake i mua i te rironga whenua e tata ana ki ngā marae, ngā urupā me ngā wāhi tapu
But ka ngau ngā kaipupuri rīhi i runga i te whenua Māori i te huringa pupuri i te wā e pōhēhē tonu ana ngā ture tata me te utu
