# What the page states

> The source page names plan items on health, resourcing, gender-affirming care, human rights law, intersex surgeries and migration law. No figures appear. No timetable is given.

System: Rainbow community rights and health access

Scenario: The rainbow communities plan is put into effect
When the government puts the rainbow communities plan into effect
Then rainbow-appropriate health care services and strategies are developed with cultural and holistic models
And resourcing for rainbow organisations including Māori and Pasifika-led organisations increases
And free non-discriminatory gender-affirming health care on informed consent is provided including reversal of the puberty blocker ban
And the Human Rights Act clarifies discrimination grounds for sexual orientation, gender identity and sex characteristics and Ia Tangata report recommendations are implemented
And non-consensual medically unnecessary surgeries on intersex people are prohibited
And laws and policies enable legal gender recognition, partnership, family reunification and resettlement for rainbow asylum seekers, refugees and migrants
Outcome takatāpui and rainbow communities can access care and legal recognition
