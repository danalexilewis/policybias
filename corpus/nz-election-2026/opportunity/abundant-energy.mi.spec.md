# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> He nui te moni e whakapaua ana e Aotearoa ki ngā wahie mōnehu, ahakoa he nui ngā rawa pūngao whakahou. Mā te Abundant Energy e tāpiri kaha i raro i te whakaaetanga ā-rōpū, e herea ngā hua tohatoha a ngā gentailer mō te haumi pūngao whakahou, e whakakotahi ngā kaiwhakahaere me te whakakotahi i ngā kaiwhakarato, ā, e utu te hiko i te kāinga me te hapori. Ka whakakorea te wehehanga hanganga o ngā gentailer, ā, ka pupuritia a Huntly mō ngā ohorere tūturu, ehara mō te whakaputa hiko o ia rā.

System: Abundant Energy

Scenario: Ka tāpirihia te kaha pūngao whakahou, ka whakahouhia te mākete
Given neke atu i te $20 piriona ia tau e whakapaua ana ki te hoko wahie mōnehu
When ka tāpirihia ētahi 30 GW ki te whatunga hiko i te tau 2050 i raro i te whakaaetanga ā-rōpū
Then ka whakatuwheratia e tētahi kaupapa haumi kaha te pūtea, ā, ka whakatenatenahia ngā ahumahi kia huri ki te hiko
And ka herea ngā hua a te kāwanatanga mai i te whakaputa hiko mō te haumi pūngao whakahou
And ka whakakotahitia ngā kaiwhakahaere pūngao e whā kia kotahi
And ka whakakotahitia ngā kaiwhakarato pūngao 29 kia 8 i te nui rawa
And ka haere ngā pūtea tārewa utu iti, te pūngao ā-hapori, te whakamohu, me te hiko o ngā waka tūmatanui
Output $500 ki te $2,700 ia tau e tiakina ana mō te whānau toharite
And ētahi 5,000 tūranga mahi pūkenga teitei anō i roto i ngā tau 30
And 300% kaha whakaputa hiko whakahou anō, tae atu ki te 30 GW
And 0.35% tipu GDP ā-tau anō
And tata ki te $500 miriona ia tau o ngā hua tohatoha a ngā gentailer e whakapaua ana ki te mahere, ehara ki te pūtea matua a te Karauna
Outcome he mahana ake, he maroke ake ngā kāinga, ā, ka pupuritia te whare hiko wera o Huntly mō ngā ohorere tūturu, ehara mō te whakaputa hiko o ia rā
But me whakakapi tērā whakapaunga hua tohatoha a ngā gentailer i ngā pūkete a te Karauna, tae atu ki tētahi tāke uara whenua
