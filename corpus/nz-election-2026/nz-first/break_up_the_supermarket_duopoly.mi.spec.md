# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ko Woolworths me Foodstuffs ngā kaiwhakahaere nui o te tuku kai. Ka wehewehea a Foodstuffs kia rua ngā koperewhana ā-motu mā te waitohu—ko New World me Four Square tētahi, ko Pak'nSave tētahi—e whakataetae tahi ana ki a Woolworths New Zealand. Ka ōrite ngā whaina a te Commerce Commission ki ērā o Ahitereiria, ka whiwhi te Groceries Commissioner i ngā mana uruhi here, ā, mā tētahi angamahi Commerce Act e arotahi ngā raru uru pāmu-ki-te-papa.

System: Te wehenga o te tokorua toa hokohoko nui me te uruhi kai kaha ake

Scenario: Ka whakaurua he ture hei whakahou i te mākete kai
Given e whakahaere ana a Woolworths me Foodstuffs i neke atu i te 80 ōrau o te mākete kai
And kua kitea e te Commerce Commission e whiwhi ana ngā toa hokohoko nui i te $1 million pea ia rā ngā hua nui rawa
And i whiwhi tētahi kaiwhakatipu i te 60c ia kg pea, i te $5.79 te utu hokohoko
When ka whakaurua he ture hei wehewehe i a Foodstuffs kia rua ngā koperewhana ā-motu
Then ka whakahaere tētahi koperewhana i a New World me Four Square, tētahi i a Pak'nSave, e whakataetae tahi ana ki a Woolworths New Zealand
And ka whiwhi te Groceries Commissioner i ngā mana ki te tūhura, te whakatau here, me te whakatau whaina tōtika
And mā tētahi angamahi hou i raro i te Commerce Act 1986 e āhei te mahi arotahi mō te uru pāmu-ki-te-papa
Output ngā whaina tae atu ki te $10 million, e toru ngā whakarea o te hua, te 10 ōrau rānei o te hurihanga mō ngā takahanga taumaha
Outcome he pēhanga tūturu kia heke ngā utu, kia pai ake te uara, kia tika te atawhai i ngā kaiwhakarato
