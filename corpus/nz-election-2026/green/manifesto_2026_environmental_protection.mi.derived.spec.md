# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakatakoto te kaupapa here i ngā here hauora pūnaha hauropi, ko ngā tangata whenua ngā kaipupuri whakatau tahi, ka whakakorea te Fast-track Approvals Act, ka whakakorea ngā whakaaetanga keri whenua ara-tere, ka whakahokia tētahi Manatū Mō Te Taiao motuhake me ētahi rawa anō mō Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te whakamana, ka whakahoungia ngā paerewa hau, ā, ka whāia ngā whakakoretanga koiora. Kāore he uara here, he tahua whakamana, he wātaka whakakore rānei i tāpirihia.

System: Ngā here me te whakamana, kāore he kaha kua utua

Scenario: Ka whakakorea, ka whakakorea hoki ngā whakaaetanga keri whenua ara-tere
Given te keri whenua whakangaro i ngā wāhi tuawhenua i raro i te Fast-track Approvals Act
When ka whakakorea te Fast-track Approvals Act, ā, ka whakakorea ngā whakaaetanga ara-tere mō te keri waro, kōura me te takere hou
Then ka ngaro i aua kaupapa te huarahi whakaaetanga ara-tere
Outcome ka ora te taiao i te keri whenua i whakaaetia i raro i tērā Ture
But tērā pea ka rapu ngā kaipupuri whakaaetanga i te utu whakaea — kei te Karauna tērā mōrearea

Scenario: Ka here ngā here hauora pūnaha hauropi i ngā whakataunga
When ka whakatinanahia ngā here kaha e whakawhirinaki ana ki te taunakitanga mō te hauora pūnaha hauropi, ko Te Tiriti me ngā tangata whenua ngā kaipupuri whakatau tahi me ngā kaitiaki
Then ka pā ki te whakawhanaketanga tētahi pae raro hauora i whakatakotoria tahitia ki ngā tangata whenua
Outcome ka taea te tiaki i ngā awa, ngā ngahere me ngā oneone hei tūāpapa o ngā hapori
But kāore e taea e ngā kaiwhakawhanake me ngā kaunihera te whakamahi here tau kia whakatauria rā anō ngā uara

Scenario: Ka piki ngā rawa a Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te whakamana
When ka whakahokia tētahi Manatū Mō Te Taiao motuhake, ā, ka piki ngā rawa a Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te tutukitanga, te aroturuki me te whakamana
Then ka pā ki ngā kaipara tētahi kaiwhakahaere kua nui ake ngā rawa
Outcome ka taea te piki o te kawenga mēnā ka rere tonu ngā whakatewhatewha me ngā whakawhiu
But ka mau te Karauna i tētahi pire mutunga kore mō te kaha o Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te whakamana
