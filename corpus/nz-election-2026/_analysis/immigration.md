# Immigration and contribution

Reduces six immigration-tagged party dump pages (ACT three pointers; Green manifesto triplet) into proposed events versus status-quo framing.

## act

### Given

Successive governments turned skilled migration into a general labour tap, failed to enforce rules, and let infrastructure lag (`deport.md`). About 20,980 known overstayers. Accredited Employer Work Visa categories stay open after skill gaps close. Rural farms face year-round labour shortages; seasonal visas do not cover daily roles (`rural-visa.md`). Temporary workers use infrastructure before paying much tax (`fair-contribution.md`).

### When

Deport resident visa holders convicted of offences carrying sentences of 10 years or more, regardless of time in New Zealand — beyond the Government’s 20-year extension proposal (`deport.md`). Auto-expire each AEWV skill category yearly; reopen only with current demand evidence. Impose a five-year welfare stand-down on all residence-class visa holders (no jobseeker support, accommodation supplement, or income-tested benefits). Add a $6-per-day infrastructure surcharge on temporary work visas, on top of existing charges (`deport.md`, `fair-contribution.md` — same levy, two URLs). Extend basic English requirements to all AEWV types; lower standards for seasonal workers. Establish a dedicated overstayer enforcement unit in Immigration New Zealand; require platform employers such as Uber and DoorDash to verify and report work rights; revoke accreditation from employers who facilitate overstaying.

Create a standalone three-year Rural Workforce Visa for dairy, sheep and beef, and general farm work, tied to accredited rural employers rather than regions; allow transfers between accredited rural employers but not into non-rural sectors (`rural-visa.md`). Set an initial three-year term without mid-term renewal; before expiry employers readvertise and reissue for another three years if no suitable New Zealander is available. Add an agricultural stream to the Work to Residence Visa: eligibility after 72 cumulative months on an RWV with an accredited employer meeting standard requirements. Exempt the RWV from the infrastructure surcharge.

### Money

$6 per day infrastructure surcharge on temporary work visas; expected ~$80 million per year (`deport.md`, `fair-contribution.md`). RWV levy exemption stated (`rural-visa.md`). Rural export $60 billion per year — **sketch**, context not programme budget. Welfare stand-down, deportation rule change, AEWV expiry, English rules, overstayer unit — unstated.

## green

### Given

Migrants and refugees face exploitation, compliance targeting, and family separation under current rules (`manifesto_2026_immigration_and_refugees.md`). Pacific visitors face restrictive visa rules; Pacific workers on the Recognised Seasonal Employer scheme lack dignity and open pathways (`manifesto_2026_pacific_peoples.md`). Overseas New Zealanders face student-loan interest, voting limits, and super eligibility rules that Greens say push people away (`manifesto_2026_overseas_new_zealanders.md`).

### When

Create residence pathways for long-term settled migrants, including people whose visas have expired. Abolish Acceptable Standards of Health requirements. Let migrant work visa holders switch employers and remove the 12-month stand-down requiring departure between visas. Clear the refugee family reunification backlog; expand partner work rights; restore birthright citizenship; remove income barriers to parent visas. Devolve immigration resources and decision-making to hapū and iwi under Te Tiriti; provide Te Tiriti education to new migrants and refugees. Progressively raise the refugee quota to 5,000 by 2029; increase refugee resettlement programme funding; create a humanitarian visa for family of New Zealanders in conflict zones. Resource Immigration New Zealand to clear tier 1 and tier 2 family reunification and process asylum claims quickly; end unjust detention and electronic monitoring of asylum seekers (`manifesto_2026_immigration_and_refugees.md`).

Extend visa-free travel for visitors from Pacific Islands (`manifesto_2026_pacific_peoples.md`). Replace the Recognised Seasonal Employer scheme with an alternative offering fair wages, housing, essential services, open work rights, and residency pathways instead of single-employer tying.

Enable a tikanga-based pathway for people who whakapapa Māori to establish citizenship regardless of country of residence or birth. Reduce and rebalance overseas student-loan repayments and interest; extend the repayment holiday; abolish border arrests for repayment violations. Extend time overseas without losing voting eligibility. Restore the 10-year New Zealand residence period for superannuation eligibility. Pursue reciprocal health, welfare, and visa-waiver arrangements with other countries and widen existing agreements to temporary visitors (`manifesto_2026_overseas_new_zealanders.md`).

### Money

Refugee quota target 5,000 by 2029 (`manifesto_2026_immigration_and_refugees.md`). “Increase funding” for refugee resettlement and INZ processing — **sketch**, no amounts. Pacific visa-free travel, RSE replacement, Te Tiriti devolution, tikanga citizenship pathway, student-loan changes, super 10-year rule, reciprocal agreements — unstated.
