# What the page states

> A billion-krona effort on staffing is to give staff more colleagues and the older person more time, support and care.
> A new elderly-care act is to guarantee equal care, and the Elderly Care Boost is made permanent so that staff can train in paid working time.
> Central grants to municipalities are uprated with inflation, and a language requirement is to mean that the older person and the staff understand each other.

System: A staffing effort and a new elderly-care act

Scenario: Home care gets more colleagues and the act sets the floor
Given elderly care where staff lack time to see the whole person and minute planning governs the visits
When a new elderly-care act and a billion-krona effort on increased staffing are introduced
Then staff get more colleagues and more time for care without minute planning
Outcome equal care across the country, with a language requirement and access to doctors and nurses around the clock in care homes and in home care
