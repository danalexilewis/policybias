# What the page states

> The climate targets are to be met. Fossil fuels are to go. The transition is to be made by free people in a growing economy, with more fossil-free electricity and with large-scale capture and storage of carbon dioxide. The target is 10 million tonnes of carbon dioxide in 2030 and 20 million tonnes in 2035.
