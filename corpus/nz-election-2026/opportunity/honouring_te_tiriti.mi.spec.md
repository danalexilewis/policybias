# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka kitea te Karauna hei hoa Tiriti tūturu e tiaki ana i ngā taonga, e whakatika ana i ngā hē o mua. Mēnā ka paahitia, ka whakakorea te Treaty Principles Bill me te Marine and Coastal Area Amendment Bill. Ka whakaakona te hītori o Aotearoa, tae atu ki Te Tiriti, i ngā taumata katoa, ka nui ake ngā kaiako te reo, ā, ka tukuna iho ngā ratonga hauora me te ture ki te wāhi kāore e ōrite ngā hua. Kei te whakarewa tonu te kaupapa here taipitopito.

System: Te whakahōnore i Te Tiriti

Scenario: Ka huri ngā tautuhinga Tiriti a te Karauna
When ka mahi te Karauna hei hoa Tiriti
Then ka whakakorea te Treaty Principles Bill me te Marine and Coastal Area (Takutai Moana) Amendment Bill mēnā kua paahitia
And ka whakaakona te hītori o Aotearoa, tae atu ki Te Tiriti, i ngā taumata katoa o te pūnaha mātauranga
And ka whakatupuria te tokomaha o ngā kaiako te reo tohu kia whai wāhi te reo i te kura
And ka tukuna iho ngā ratonga hauora me te ture, me ngā rawa tūturu me te mana motuhake, ki te wāhi kāore e ōrite ngā hua
And ka tautokona ngā whakataunga Tiriti kia tōkeke
Outcome ka tiakina ngā taonga, ā, ka whakatikaina ngā hē o mua
