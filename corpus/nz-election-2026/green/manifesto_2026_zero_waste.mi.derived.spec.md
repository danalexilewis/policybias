# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka utu ngā kaihanga mō te kaitiakitanga me tētahi kaupapa whakahoki ipu. Ko te tika-ki-te-whakatika me tētahi reiti para teitei ake e pana ana i ngā mea kia whakamahia anō. Kua okioki te para-ki-te-pūngao. Ka tūtaki ngā kāinga me ngā pakihi ki te reiti i mua i te whakatakoto o tētahi reiti.

System: Te kawenga kaihanga, kāore he tohu kua utua

Scenario: Ka tūtaki ngā kaihanga ki te kaitiakitanga here me te whakahoki ipu
Given e hanga ana ngā kaporeihana i ngā hua e kore e roa, e kore rānei e taea te whakatika, i te wā e kawe ana ngā hapori i te poke
When ka whakatūria te kaitiakitanga hua here, tētahi kaupapa whakahoki ipu inu, ngā whakamutunga āhua hua, me te tika-ki-te-whakatika
Then ka kawe ngā kaihanga i te kawenga mō ngā hua puta noa i tō rātou oranga
Outcome ka taea te piki i te whakaora me te whakamahi anō ina herea te kawenga
But ka tau te utu o te kaitiakitanga me ngā whakahokinga ki ngā kaihanga, kātahi ki ngā kaihoko

Scenario: Ka piki te reiti para ki te tikanga pai rawa o te ao
When ka piki te reiti tuku para ki te tikanga pai rawa o te ao, me tētahi reiti ōrite puta noa i ngā momo whenua para, ā, ka utu te moni i te whakaheke para
Then ka nui ake te utu o te whenua para, ā, ka noho te moni reiti ki te whakaheke
Outcome ka taea te huri i te whanonga tuku mēnā he tūturu te ngau o te utu
But ka utu ngā kaiwhakahaere whenua para me ā rātou kiritaki i te reiti e whakatauhia ana e te Pāremata

Scenario: Ka whakatakotoria ngā whāinga whakaheke para here
When ka whakatinanahia ngā whāinga whakaheke para here, e taea te ine, e whā nui, i raro i te whakahounga o te Waste Minimisation Act e whakamana ana i Te Tiriti
Then ka tūtaki ngā tari me ngā kaihanga ki ngā whāinga e taea te whakamana
Outcome ka taea te arotake i te whakaheke ki te ture
But ka tūtaki tonu ngā kaihanga me ngā kaunihera ki ngā whāinga e kore e taea te arotake kia tae mai ngā nama
