# What the page states

> Prison is far costlier per person than schooling, yet remand and warehousing dominate. Courts and probation get more funding to cut remand, drug possession is decriminalised with a health-led response while supply stays an offence, and the youth-court age rises with expanded drug and mental-health courts. Detailed policy is still to launch.

System: Smart on crime

Scenario: Remand, drugs and youth justice change
Given more than $150,000 a year to house a prisoner
And about $9,000 to educate a child for a year
When courts and probation are given more funding
Then remand numbers fall
And drug possession is decriminalised, while supply stays an offence, with a health-led response
And programmes that help prisoners process family-violence harm in their past are expanded
And the youth-court age is raised in line with developmental science
And drug and mental-health court pilots are expanded
Outcome the system spends more on rehabilitation and earlier prevention, and less on warehousing
Activates opportunity-healthy-people
