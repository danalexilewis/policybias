# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kua heke te whakawhirinaki ki te kāwanatanga i te tipu o te whaiwhai, ngā takoha me te mana pokapū. Ka puta te whaiwhai ki te awatea mā tētahi wā whakamātao, he rēhita tūmatanui me tētahi tikanga ā-ture he pānga tūturu. Ka taupanehia ngā takoha, ka herea ki ngā kaipōti takitahi, ā, mā tētahi rōpū aukati pirau motuhake, ngā ture Official Information Act kaha ake, me tētahi wāhanga utu kaupapa here e kaha ake te pono.

System: Clean Up Politics

Scenario: Ka panoni ngā ture whaiwhai, takoha me te pono
Given e utua ana Te Tari Hara Tāware ki te $17 miriona tata ia tau
When ka pā tētahi wā whakamātao tau kotahi i waenga i ngā minita me te whaiwhai
And ka tū he rēhita whaiwhai tūmatanui e taea te rapu, e herea ana
And kei tētahi tikanga whanonga ā-ture mō ngā kaiwhaiwhai te tango rēhita me ngā whaina
And ka taupanehia ngā takoha ia rōpū, ia kaikoha, ia pōtitanga
And ka rāhuitia ngā takoha mai i ngā kamupene me ngā tarahiti, nā reira ko ngā kaipōti o Aotearoa kua rēhitatia anake e taea te takoha
And ka puta ngā takoha i runga ake i te paepae whāki ki ngā tauākī ā-tau
And ka whakatewhatewha, ka ārai tētahi rōpū aukati pirau motuhake i te pirau tōrangapū
And ka rāhuitia ngā pānui pōti hē, tinihanga
And ka whakakaha te Official Information Act, ka tiakina ngā kaipūaho, ā, ka whakatūria he wāhanga utu kaupapa here motuhake
Then ka kaha ake ngā ture pono mō te whaiwhai, ngā takoha me te utu motuhake
Output ngā takoha e taupanehia ana ki te $30,000 ia rōpū, ia kaikoha, ia pōtitanga
And ka puta ngā takoha neke atu i te $1,000 ki ngā tauākī ā-tau
And tata ki te $52 miriona ia tau mō te mōkī
And $40 miriona ia tau mō te rōpū aukati pirau
And $10 miriona anō mō te Electoral Commission
And $2 miriona mō te wāhanga utu i ngā tau pōti
Outcome ko ngā tāngata, ehara ko ngā rōpū whaiwhai, e utu i te tōrangapū
