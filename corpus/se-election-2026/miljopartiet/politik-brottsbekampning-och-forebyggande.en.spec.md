# What the page states

> Shootings, bombings and unsolved crimes create insecurity. New recruitment is to be broken early through school, social services and leisure, while the gangs' money and weapons are stopped. Children who have committed crime are to be cared for in SiS, not sit in prison. Crime victims are to get clearer rights and faster compensation.

System: Prevention and prosecution against gang violence

Scenario: Early intervention and the justice chain are built out together
Given shootings, bombings, violence in close relationships and unsolved crimes create insecurity
And children and young people risk being drawn into crime when support comes too late
When school, social services and police give coordinated support and the justice chain gets more resources
Then recruitment into the gangs is to be stopped earlier
And the authorities strike at the gangs' money, weapons and explosives
And more crimes are investigated and prosecuted with tools that protect due process
And SiS and HVB are reformed so that young people get rehabilitation instead of prison
And a crime-victim law makes the rights clear and the state collects more damages
Output 25 years of age as the upper limit for a statutory right to aftercare
Outcome children are not to sit in prison
And women's shelters, protected housing and care after sexual violence are to be strengthened
