# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka kapi tētahi rohe ōhanga motuhake ki Marsden Point i te whare whakarewa o mua me Northport, e arotahi ana ki te whakaputa pūngao me ngā mahi moana, tae atu ki te whakatika kaipuke. Mā tētahi pae kāwanatanga e whakatau tere ngā ture whakamahere me ngā whakaaetanga RMA, kaua mā ngā umanga maha. Kei te whakaarohia tonu ngā whakatenatena tāke, ngā panoni ki te Overseas Investment Act me ngā whakamāmā reiti, kāore anō kia whakapūmautia. Kāore he reiti, he tahua, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Rohe ōhanga motuhake ki Marsden Point

Scenario: Ka whakatūria he rohe ōhanga motuhake ki Marsden Point
Given ki te kore te rohe, ka mahi ngā ahumahi ki ngā kaunihera ā-rohe me ngā kaunihera ā-rohe nui, ki a Te Papa Atawhai me ētahi atu umanga i raro i te RMA
When ka whakatūria he rohe ōhanga motuhake e kapi ana i te whare whakarewa o mua me Northport
Then ka whakatauhia tere ngā ture whakamahere me ngā whakaaetanga RMA e tētahi pae kāwanatanga i runga i te tupu ōhanga me ngā whāinga o te rohe
And kei te whakaarohia tonu ngā whakatenatena tāke, ngā panoni ki te Overseas Investment Act me ngā whakamāmā reiti, kāore anō kia whakapūmautia
Outcome ka whakaarohia tuatahi te whakaputa me te tohatoha pūngao me ngā mahi moana, tae atu ki te whakatika kaipuke, i te rohe
