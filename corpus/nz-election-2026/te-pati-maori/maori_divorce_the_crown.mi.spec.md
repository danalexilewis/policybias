# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tangohia te kāhui ariki o Ingarangi hei upoko o te motu, ā, ka neke a Aotearoa ki tētahi motu i runga i te Tiriti o Waitangi. Ka whakatūria he Pāremata Māori, ā, ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga katoa a Matike Mai mō te hurihanga tūāpapa ture. Ka whakahoungia nui te tukanga whakataunga Tiriti, ā, ka whakahokia te whenua ki te whānau, ki te hapū, ki te iwi.
