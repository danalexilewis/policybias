# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka hangaia anō, ka whakakahangia hoki he mana hauora e ārahitia ana e te Māori. Ka neke te pūnaha ki te ārai mate me te oranga whānau, ehara i te urupare mōrearea anake. Ka taea te uru ki ngā ratonga, ka haumaru ā-ahurea, ka whakautu ki te Māori.

System: Te whakahounga pūnaha hauora e ārahitia ana e te Māori

Scenario: Ka hangaia anō he mana hauora e ārahitia ana e te Māori
Given he pūnaha hauora kāore e mārama ki te Māori e rahua tonu ana ki te Māori
When ka hangaia anō, ka whakakahangia hoki he mana hauora e ārahitia ana e te Māori

Outcome ka whiwhi te Māori i te taurima e kite ana i te katoa o te tangata, e ārai ana i te tūkino
