# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ko ngā rawa rima tekau tau e whakahaerehia ana i runga i ngā huringa tōrangapū e toru tau, e moumou pūtea ana ki te tiaki kua tārewatia. Ko te National Infrastructure Plan 30 tau a Te Waihanga, i pānuitia i te Hui-tanguru 2026, i whakaaetia i te Pipiri, ka whakatakotoria ki te ture, me ngā kāri kaute me te paepae teitei ki te whakakore kaupapa nui. Ka haere te 60 ōrau o te whakapau ā mua ki te tiaki me te whakahou, e utua ana ētahi mā tētahi Infrastructure Fund me ngā taputapu kaunihera i tua atu i ngā reiti anake.

System: Hanganga whakatipuranga

Scenario: Ka raka, ka tiakina te mahere 30 tau
Given ngā rawa rima tekau tau e whakahaerehia ana i runga i ngā huringa tōrangapū e toru tau
And 12 o ngā 31 tari kāwanatanga kāore e taea te rārangi i ngā rawa e pupuri ana
And te nama tūmatanui tata ki te 51% o te GDP, ki te toharite OECD o te 112%
And kua whakaaetia ngā tūtohutanga katoa 16 a Te Waihanga
When ka whakatakotoria te National Infrastructure Plan 30 tau ki te ture
Then ka tirotiro a Te Waihanga i te whakamahere, te pūtea me te tuku
And me tētahi Pire tūmatanui mā te Pāremata te whakakore i tētahi kaupapa nui
And ka pānuitia ngā kāri kaute i ngā rā 100, ngā marama 12 me ngā tau e toru
And he rēhita, he mahere tiaki, he kaiwhakahaere whakaingoatia mō ia rawa tūmatanui
Output me tētahi Pire tūmatanui mā te Pāremata te whakakore i tētahi kaupapa nui nui ake i te $250 million
And 60% o te whakapau hanganga ā mua ki te tiaki me te whakahou
And kia $60 billion e kohia ana i roto i ngā tau tekau mā tētahi Infrastructure Fund
And ngā utu huamoni i raro i te 1% o te GDP
And tata ki te $167 million ia tau o te whakapau mahi
And $27 million ia tau hei whakakaha i a Te Waihanga
And $150 million ia tau toenga mō te waka tūmatanui kore utu
Outcome ka riro i te whakatipuranga e tū mai nei ngā rawa pai ake te āhua
And ka whiwhi ngā kaunihera i ngā takoha, ngā utu pokapū, ngā tāke manuhiri me te hopu-uara kia kaua ngā reiti e kawe i te kawenga katoa
