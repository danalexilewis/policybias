# What the page states

> Growing older must never mean losing one's freedom. Care is to be marked by choice, respect and human dignity. Every older person should be able to live in community and take decisions about their own daily life.

System: Choice and self-determination in elderly care

And involuntary loneliness is countered
And working conditions and competence in elderly care are improved
