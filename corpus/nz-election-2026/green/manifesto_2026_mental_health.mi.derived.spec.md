# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakawhānui ngā ratonga kore utu ki Piki, Mana Ake, Youth One Stop Shops me ngā kaupapa e pā ana. Ka arotahi Every Life Matters ki te hunga i raro i te 24, ā, ka āta whakamutua te wehe mā te manaaki hapori. Kāore he nama mō ngā katoa pūtea, ngā whāinga kaimahi, ngā whāinga wā tatari rānei.

System: Te urunga kore utu me te putanga i te wehe ki te kaha kāore i whakaingoatia

Scenario: Ka whakawhānui ngā ratonga hauora hinengaro kore utu
When ka whakawhānui ngā ratonga hauora hinengaro kore utu, tae atu ki Piki, Mana Ake, Youth One Stop Shops, Asian Family Services me te tautoko i muri i te whānau
Then ehara te utu i te ārai kua whakaingoatia i te kaupapa here
Outcome ka taea e te tangata i karo i te manaaki nā te utu te haere mai i te kūaha
But kei ngā DHB me ngā NGO tonu te whakatau ko wai e tatari, mō te pēhea te roa, i ia rohe

Scenario: Ka āta whakamutua te wehe, ko te manaaki hapori te whakakapi
When ka āta whakamutua katoatia te wehe
And ka nui ake te wawaotanga wawe kia iti ake te manaaki ā-ture
Then ka puta te noho mokemoke i te tauira manaaki kua whakaingoatia
Outcome e tika ana kia manaakitia ngā tāngata i te mōrearea i te hapori, kaua e wehea
But me whai tonu ngā rōpū hapori i ngā moenga, ngā kaimahi me tētahi wātaka āta haere me utua e tētahi
