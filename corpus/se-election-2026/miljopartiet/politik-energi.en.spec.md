# What the page states

> When industry and transport are electrified the electricity system must be robust and sustainable, while households' electricity costs are kept down. Expansion is to rest on wind, solar and green baseload. New nuclear power is rejected as slow and expensive. A municipal veto over wind power is to remain, but the decision is to come earlier. The target for green baseload is set at 2030.

System: A renewable electricity system with green baseload

Scenario: Wind, solar and green baseload are built out at the same time
Given Sweden needs a renewable energy system to manage the climate transition
And industry and transport are electrified
When offshore wind, solar electricity and energy efficiency are driven on and green baseload is planned
Then the national grid is built out at sea and wind-power permitting becomes more legally secure
And municipalities and neighbours get better compensation and can be offered co-ownership
And support for energy efficiency in multi-dwelling buildings and industries is restored
And the state helps industry and electricity producers to sign electrification agreements so that new electricity is built in step with demand
And electricity-network companies are put under harder pressure against unjustified rises in network charges
And new nuclear power is rejected as slow, expensive and crowding out faster electricity production
Output 10 GW of green baseload
Outcome electricity prices are pressed through more renewable electricity, efficiency and steering
And the municipal veto over wind power is kept, with earlier decisions
