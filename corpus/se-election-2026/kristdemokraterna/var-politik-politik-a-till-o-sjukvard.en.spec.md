# What the page states

> Health care is to offer care that is adapted to need, accessible and efficient, of good quality. To cure, relieve and comfort is the main task. Publicly funded care is to be funded in solidarity and driven by need. State principal responsibility is pursued for more equal and accessible care. Prevention, care on the patient's terms and patient safety are prioritised.

System: State principal responsibility for health care

Scenario: Care is to become more equal across the country
When state principal responsibility for health care is introduced
Then care is to become more equal and accessible
And publicly funded care remains funded in solidarity and driven by need
Outcome everyone is to be able to trust that care meets their needs
