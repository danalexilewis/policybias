# What the page states

> A Māori Kai Sovereignty Board would oversee kai justice. Māori food systems would be protected and community food systems including māra kai invested in. Grocery costs would be reduced by removing GST from kai and regulating supermarket price hikes, with rangatahi supported into food and primary industries. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Kai sovereignty with board oversight and grocery relief

Scenario: A Māori Kai Sovereignty Board oversees kai justice
Given Aotearoa produces more than enough kai but whānau go without
When a Māori Kai Sovereignty Board is established to oversee kai justice
Then Māori food systems are protected, including seed sovereignty, rejecting GE foods and upholding WAI262
And community food systems including māra kai and food farms are invested in
And rangatahi are supported into food and primary industries
And regenerative Māori agriculture is invested in
And corporate control is broken by supporting Māori-owned supermarkets and protecting local kai sellers
And grocery costs are reduced, including removing GST from kai and regulating supermarket price hikes
And access to land for food production is increased
Outcome kai is affordable, healthy and connected to whenua and whakapapa
