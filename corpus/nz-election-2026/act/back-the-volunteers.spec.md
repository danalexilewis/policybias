# What the page states

> DOC cannot maintain its huge hut and track network alone and many volunteer groups work on annual discretionary grants. The policy gives community groups first opportunity and multi-year contracts for backcountry upkeep, opens nature credits to accredited hunting groups, and gives hunting bodies formal representation in predator-removal decisions. No dollar budget or credit-market detail is given.

System: Backcountry upkeep through community groups and hunting conservation

Scenario: DOC funds capable community and hunting groups for backcountry work
Given DOC is responsible for more than 950 huts and 15,000 kilometres of tracks
And DOC faces a 30 per cent maintenance shortfall
And DOC has issued more than 2,300 closure alerts since 2021
And hunting, tramping and conservation groups restore huts, maintain tracks and run trap lines at a fraction of the cost
And many groups survive on annual discretionary grants with no certainty of continued funding
When DOC is required to give community groups the first opportunity for funding when they offer a competitive option for backcountry upkeep
Then volunteer organisations may hold multi-year contracts instead of applying year by year
Output the Voluntary Nature Credits Market opened to accredited hunting groups for verified biodiversity results
And formal representation for the New Zealand Deerstalkers Association and Game Animal Council in predator-removal decisions affecting hunters
Outcome capable community groups deliver backcountry upkeep work
And accredited hunting groups earn nature credits for conservation results that can be independently verified
