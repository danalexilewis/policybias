# Our reading

> This is our systems reading of the source page, not the published claim. The page funds artists at least the living wage on public projects, stabilises major events, ends gambling dependence for long-term arts money, repatriates taonga, and increases Radio New Zealand, Whakaata Māori and community media funding. It states no budget figures or living-wage rate. Those readings are ours. We invent no dollar figures.

System: Arts and public media funding without priced lifts

Scenario: Publicly funded projects must pay artists the living wage
Given creatives working without stable income while local and Indigenous artists struggle
When all publicly funded projects must pay at least the living wage to artists
Then artists on those projects meet a wage floor
Outcome livelihoods on public work become more secure
But the same envelope either costs more or buys fewer hours

Scenario: Long-term arts funding leaves gambling revenue
When sustainable long-term arts funding not reliant on gambling revenue is provided
Then arts budgets no longer turn on gambling proceeds
Outcome funding can be steadier across years
But without a named replacement source, leaving gambling revenue opens a hole

Scenario: RNZ and Whakaata Māori funding increases
Given steep cuts to Māori arts and broadcasting
When funding for Radio New Zealand and Whakaata Māori increases alongside community, Māori and Pacific media
Then non-commercial public media can restore capacity
Outcome local stories and talent have a stronger broadcast base
But the increase is unnamed, so the lift cannot be checked from the page
