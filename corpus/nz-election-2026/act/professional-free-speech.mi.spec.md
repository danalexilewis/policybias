# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kua pā te whiu ki ngā ngaio waeture mō ngā whakaaro ture i kōrerotia ki waho o te mahi. Ka aukati te kaupapa here i ngā kaiwaeture e whiu ana i ngā mema mō te kōrero tūmataiti ture, me noho tautika ngā umanga, ka whāiti te whakangungu here ki te tohungatanga me te haumaru, ā, ka whakakaha te tirohanga ā-kōti i te wā e mau ana ngā mana hē. Kāore he rā tīmata i homai.

System: Tiakanga kōrero mō ngā ngaio waeture

Scenario: Ka whakapuaki tētahi ngaio waeture i tētahi whakaaro ture ki waho o tana mahi
Given neke atu i te 200,000 ngā tāngata o Aotearoa e mahi ana i ngā umanga waeture
And kua pā te mahi whiu, te whakangungu whakaaro here rānei ki ngā kaiako, ngā nēhi, ngā kaihoko whenua me ētahi atu ngaio mō ngā whakaaro i kōrerotia ki waho o tā rātou mahi
When ka whakatikahia te ture kia noho ngā kaiwaeture ki te mahi ngaio me te haumaru tūmatanui
Then kāore e āhei ngā kaiwaeture ngaio ki te whiu i ngā mema mō te kōrero ture ki waho o tā rātou mahi ngaio
Output me noho tautika ngā kaiwaeture ki ngā take tōrangapū me ngā take whakaaro
And ka whāitihia te whakangungu here ki ngā take e hono tika ana ki te tohungatanga ngaio me te haumaru tūmatanui
And ka whakakaha te tirohanga ā-kōti kia taea e ngā kōti te aukati i ngā kaiwaeture e hipa ana i ō rātou mana ture
Outcome ka mau tonu te āheinga o ngā kaiwaeture ki te mahi mō te hē ngaio tūturu, te kino ki ngā kiritaki, ki ngā tūroro rānei, me ngā takahi i ngā kawenga ngaio
And ka taea tonu te whiu i te hē nui i te mahi, engari ehara te kōrero tūmataiti ture i te hara ngaio
