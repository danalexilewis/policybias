# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka noho ngā pae matihiko ki tētahi tikanga whakaiti tūkino me tētahi Digital Duty of Care, te mārama o ngā hātepe, me tētahi kaituku waeture ā-motu motuhake. Ka waeturehia te whakamahi i te atamai hangarau, ka kaha ake te tāke i ngā hua hangarau ā-ao e haere ana ki tāwāhi, ā, ka pā te Māori Data Governance Model me te Māori AI Governance Framework puta noa i te rāngai tūmatanui. Ka kaha ake ngā whiu tūmataiti me te whakamana, ka whānui ake te mātauranga, ā, ka pai ake te whakauru matihiko mā te ipurangi, ngā pūrere, me ngā huarahi tuarongo. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te kawenga hangarau matihiko me te whakauru

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere hangarau matihiko
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere hangarau matihiko
Then ka waeturehia ngā pae pāpāho pāpori mā tētahi Digital Duty of Care me te mārama o ngā hātepe, ngā pūnaha amuamu, me tētahi kaituku waeture ā-motu motuhake
And ka waeturehia te whakamahi i te atamai hangarau, tae atu ki ngā ture kaha ake mō ngā deepfake me ngā rauemi hara tinihanga
And ka kaha te patu i te karo tāke me te kawe hua ki tāwāhi a ngā kamupene hangarau ā-ao pērā i a Meta, Amazon me Microsoft
And ka tango te rāngai tūmatanui i te Māori Data Governance Model me te Māori AI Governance Framework nā Te Kāhui Raraunga
And ka whakakahangia ngā kawenga o te hunga pupuri raraunga ki te tiaki i ngā raraunga kaiwhakamahi me ngā raraunga āhuahanga, me ngā whiu tūmataiti nui ake me ngā mana whakamana a te Privacy Commissioner
And ka whānui ake te mātauranga pāpāho me te mātauranga matihiko
And ka whakaitia ngā aukati ki te whakauru matihiko mā te ipurangi, ngā pūrere, me ngā huarahi tuarongo mō ngā ratonga taketake
Outcome ka uru ngā hapori ki ngā ratonga matihiko me te kore e whakararu i te tūmataiti me te haumaru ipurangi
