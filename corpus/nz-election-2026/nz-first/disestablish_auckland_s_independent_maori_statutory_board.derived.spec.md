# Our understanding

> The policy states $3.5 million last year for IMSB operations, disestablishment of the unelected board, and orderly transition of functions back to Auckland Council without preventing council engagement with Māori. It does not prove ratepayers keep the $3.5 million if the work continues inside council.

System: Board removal as accountability change with a named operations cost

Scenario: The board is disestablished
Given ratepayers pay $3.5 million annually to support IMSB operations with no ability to elect or remove its members
When the Independent Māori Statutory Board is disestablished
Then the board's separate operations line ends
Output $3.5 million in annual IMSB operations no longer paid to that board
Outcome those who influence spending sit inside structures accountable to the public
But the $3.5 million is the board's operations cost, not a guaranteed net saving

Scenario: Functions move back to Auckland Council
Given the policy allows orderly transition of the IMSB's functions, property and obligations back to Auckland Council
When those functions sit with council
Then Māori engagement can continue under democratically accountable structures
Outcome transparency and accountability change even if similar work continues
But the work may continue under different accountability, so cost and influence do not automatically vanish
