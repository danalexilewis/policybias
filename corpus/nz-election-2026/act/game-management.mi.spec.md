# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te whakahaere a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā kararehe tākaro i raro i ngā ture e ahu ana ki te ārai, i te wā ka taea e te Game Animal Council te tohutohu anake. Ka whakawhitia e te kaupapa here te whakahaere ki te Game Animal Council kua pūteatia katoatia, ka whakamanahia ngā momo tākaro hei rawa e whai uara ana, ā, ka tohua ngā Herds of Special Interest, tīmata ki te whitetail o Stewart Island. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Whakahaere kararehe tākaro nā te kaiwhaiwhai i runga i te whenua atawhai

Scenario: Ka nuku te whakahaere kararehe tākaro mai i Te Papa Atawhai ki te Game Animal Council
Given kei te whakahaere a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā kararehe tākaro i runga i te whenua atawhai i raro i ngā ture e tīmata ana i te ārai, te whakamate rānei
And ka taea e te Game Animal Council te tohutohu, i te wā kei a Te Papa Atawhai te mana
When ka whakawhitia te kawenga whakahaere kararehe tākaro i runga i te whenua atawhai ki te Game Animal Council
Then ka pūteatia katoatia te Kaunihera ki te whakatutuki i ōna kawenga o nāianei me ērā e marohitia ana
Output ka whakamanahia ngā momo kararehe tākaro me ngā manu tākaro i ngā ture hāngai hei rawa taiao e whai uara ana, hei whakahaere toitū
And ka tohua ngā kāhui tākaro tino whai uara o Aotearoa hei Herds of Special Interest, tīmata ki te tia whitetail o Stewart Island
Outcome ka whakahaerehia ngā kararehe tākaro i runga i te toitū i roto i ngā tepenga atawhai
And mā ngā kaiwhaiwhai e mōhio ana ki ngā kāhui me te whenua ngā whakataunga whakahaere tākaro, kaua te kōrero tohutohu anake
