# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> E here ana te kete ki ngā pirihimana ārai tuatahi tāpiri i roto i ngā marama tekau mā waru, ka tāruatia a Youth Aid, ā, ka whakaurua ngā whiu iti rawa mō ngā whakaeke ki ngā pirihimana me ngā kaiurupare tuatahi. Mā tētahi whare herehere kēne anake e whakamahi te rawa e tū ana, ka tohua ngā kēne hei rōpū whakatuma, ā, mā tētahi tikanga taumata kōhuru e whakamahi te herehere mō ake tonu mō te kōhuru tuatahi. Ka tangohia ngā whiu hapori me ngā whakamāmā mō ngā tāngata hara tūkino e kiia ana he whakatuma. Kāore he utu tāra i homai.
