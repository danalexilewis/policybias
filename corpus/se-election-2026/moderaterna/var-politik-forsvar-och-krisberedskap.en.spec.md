# What the page states

> Sweden is to be able to deter an armed attack and meet hybrid threats together with Nato. The armed forces are being rearmed quickly. Ukraine is to receive long-term military support. Total defence is to be strengthened in parallel, and financing is in time to be carried in balanced public finances.

System: Rearmament, Nato and support for Ukraine

Scenario: The defence appropriation is to reach Nato's military target
Given the appropriation has been raised from 75 billion kronor in 2022 to 175 billion kronor in 2026
And that is 100 billion kronor more a year
And Sweden is expected to reach 2.8 percent of GDP in 2026
And the government could reach that target in 2025
When rearmament continues towards Nato's target
Then direct military spending is to reach the alliance's target earlier than other allies
Output 3.5 percent of GDP to direct military spending as early as 2030
And 1.5 percent of GDP to security- and defence-related investment
And parts of the build-up are loan-financed now and are to be carried permanently so that public finances are in balance by 2035 at the latest
Outcome Sweden is to deter Russia together with allies in northern Europe

Scenario: Ukraine needs aircraft and long-term support
Given Sweden has supported Ukraine militarily with 128 billion kronor since 2022
When the support continues
Then donated aircraft are replaced with new Gripen E/F for the Swedish air force
Output 20 Gripen E/F that Ukraine is preparing to buy
And 16 JAS 39 Gripen C/D from its own fleet
And 40 billion kronor in support in 2026
And as much planned for 2027
Outcome Ukraine's defence is also an investment in Swedish security

Scenario: Conscripts and officers are to stay in the system
Given the daily allowance for conscripts and civil conscripts is raised from 146 kronor to 200 kronor
And officer cadets are raised from 175 to 240 kronor
When the number of conscripts increases in the coming years
Then conditions and training are also to be good enough on the civilian labour market
Output 25 billion kronor extra on matériel beyond other appropriations
Outcome personnel supply is to carry a larger war organisation
