# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka taea te ārai ā-whakaaro i ngā kaiwhaiwhai ture i te rārangi pū, te whakawhiu rānei mā te rererangi i ngā patu manu hīra, me te iti o te rongoā ture motuhake. Ka waihanga te kaupapa here i ngā hara infringement mō ēnei mahi e rua, ā, ka noho tonu te tāngata ki te whakahē i te whaiwhai. Kāore he nui whaina, he rā whakatinana rānei i homai.

System: Tiakina te whaiwhai ture i te pokanoa ā-whakaaro

Scenario: Ka ārai ā-whakaaro tētahi i tētahi kaiwhaiwhai ture, i tētahi patu manu hīra rānei
Given ka taea te ārai ā-whakaaro i ngā kaiwhaiwhai e mahi haumaru ana, e noho ana ki te ture, e ngā tāngata ka tū ki te rārangi pū, kāore he hara infringement motuhake
And ka taea te whakamahi i ngā drone, i ngā waka rererangi hurihuri rānei ki te whakawhiu i ngā kaipatu manu hīra mā te whakamarara, te whakateretere rānei i ngā manu, me te iti o te rongoā ture
When ka waihangatia ngā hara infringement motuhake mō ēnei mahi
Then ko te tū ā-whakaaro ki te rārangi pū a tētahi kaiwhaiwhai ture he hara infringement
Output he hara infringement mō te whakamahi i ngā drone, i ngā waka rererangi hurihuri rānei ki te whakawhiu i ngā kaipatu manu hīra
Outcome ka noho tonu te tāngata ki te whakahē i te whaiwhai, engari kaua e pokanoa i ngā huarahi e mōrearea ai ētahi atu
And kāore e whakakāhoretia ngā kaiwhaiwhai me ngā kaipatu e tika ana kia ū ki ngā ture haumaru mārō e te ārai ā-whakaaro
