# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka mātakitaki tētahi Commissioner for Animals me tētahi manatū motuhake i ngā tikanga kararehe ahuwhenua. Ka whakamutua āhua te ahuwhenua wheketere. Me ōrite ngā kawemai ki ngā paerewa o konei. Ka kawe ngā kaipāmu me ngā kaihoko-mai i te huringa; ka kawe te Karauna i ngā tari hōu.

System: Ngā tari oranga kararehe me ngā whakamutunga āhua, kāore he rā

Scenario: Ka whakatūria tētahi Commissioner for Animals me tētahi manatū motuhake
Given e mamae ana ngā kararehe ahuwhenua nā te mea kāore e ūtia ngā paerewa i whakaarohia hei tiaki i a rātou
When ka aroturuki motuhake, ka tautoko hoki tētahi Commissioner for Animals, ā, ka whakatūria tētahi manatū motuhake
Then he kaitautoko motuhake me tētahi tari mō te oranga kararehe, i waho i te hopu ahumahi
Outcome he kāinga ā-tari mārama ake mō ngā kerēme nanakia
But me utu tonu te Karauna i ngā kaimahi me ngā mana i mua i te mahi a ngā tari

Scenario: Ka whakamutua āhua te ahuwhenua wheketere me ngā tikanga e pā ana
When ka whakamutua āhua te tapahi ngutu, te kai pāhikahika kaha o te takurua, te ahuwhenua wheketere, te ahuwhenua wheke, ngā pouaka whānau poaka, me ngā rōtio
Then ka mutu aua tikanga hei huarahi whakaputa tika ā-ture i roto i te wā
Outcome ka wehe ngā kararehe i ngā pūnaha e kīia ana e te kaupapa here he kino
But ka whakahaere tonu ngā kaipāmu i aua pūnaha kia whakatauhia te rā mutunga e tētahi whakatau o muri

Scenario: Me tutuki ngā kawemai i ngā paerewa oranga kararehe o Aotearoa
When me tutuki ngā hua kawemai, tae atu ki ngā rongoā ātaahua, i ngā paerewa oranga kararehe o Aotearoa
Then ka aukatia, ka herea rānei kia whakapikia ngā taonga e kore e eke ki te pae o konei
Outcome kāore e tapahia ngā paerewa o te kāinga e ngā kawemai ngoikore ake
But ka tau te utu whai i te ture i te tapa ki ngā kaihoko-mai, kātahi ki ngā kaihoko
