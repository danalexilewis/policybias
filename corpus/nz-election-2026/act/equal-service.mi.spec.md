# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> I hanga ngā kāwanatanga o mua i ngā ara ratonga tūmatanui wehe, ā, kei te toe mai ngā ture maha i runga i te iwi. Ka whakakorea e te kaupapa here aua ture, ka whakature i te tuku ratonga i runga i te hiahia, ka whakamutua ngā tikanga wairua e pūteatia ana e te tūmatanui i te mahi, ka tangohia ngā herenga ahurea kāore e hāngai ki te tūranga, ā, ka tuhia anō te Public Service Act. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Kotahi te ratonga tūmatanui mō ngā tāngata katoa o Aotearoa

Scenario: Ka tangohia ngā ara ratonga tūmatanui wehe, ka whakatauria tētahi paerewa kotahi
Given i hanga ngā kāwanatanga o mua i ngā ara wehe mō ngā rōpū rerekē, ā, kei te toe mai te nuinga o aua ture
And kua tohutohungia kē ngā tari ki te tuku ratonga i runga i te hiahia, kaua i te tuakiri
And e here ana te Public Service Act i ngā kaimahi tūmatanui kia tōpū tōrangapū-kore, kia kōwhiria i runga i te pūkenga
When ka tangohia ngā ture i runga i te iwi, ka tuhia anō te Public Service Act
Then ka whakakorea te section 73 o te Public Service Act me te section 118 o te Crown Entities Act
Output ka tohatohaina ngā ratonga tūmatanui ki te hunga e hiahia ana, ahakoa te tuakiri
And ka whakaturehia te ture i runga i te hiahia kia taea e te tangata te wero i ngā whakataunga e takahi ana i te kōti
And ka mutu te whakamahi i te wā tūmatanui me te pūtea tūmatanui mō ngā mahi wairua, haahi rānei i te mahi
Outcome ka riro ngā tūranga mahi kāwanatanga i te kaitono pai rawa i runga i te pūkenga
And ka tangohia e ngā tari ngā herenga ahurea i ngā pānui mahi me ngā whakaahuatanga tūranga, ki te kore rawa e hiahiatia taua mātauranga mō te tūranga
And ka noho tōrangapū-kore te ratonga tūmatanui, ka kōwhiri i runga i te pūkenga, ka āta taurite te atawhai i te tangata, ā, ka whakawāia ki te pātai mehemea ka tae ngā ratonga ki te hunga e hiahia ana
