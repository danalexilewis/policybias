### What we’ll do

*   Support Māori-led disability services and decision-making.
*   Make support easier to access and less degrading.
*   Fund support that fits whānau realities, not bureaucratic categories.

### Why it matters

Disabled Māori deserve support that honours their mana, their whānau, and their right to live fully. No one should have to fight a degrading system just to access the care, dignity, and support they are entitled to.
