# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tiakina te whenua, te moana me ngā momo taonga i te tāmio ā-kamupene mā te whakahou ture atawhai me te rāhui keri kōwhai i runga i te whenua atawhai. Ka whānui ake te Jobs for Nature, ka tiakina ngā repo māori e toe ana, ka whakaorangia te wāhi repo kua tūkino, ā, ka piki te pūtea atawhai tūmatanui, tae atu ki te Predator Free 2050. Ka pūteatia te Action for Nature me ngā ārahi kanorau koiora taketake.

System: Te tiaki atawhai me te kanorau koiora

Scenario: Ka whakamanahia te mahere atawhai
When ka whakamanahia e te kāwanatanga te mahere atawhai
Then ka whakahoungia ngā ture atawhai me ngā ture kararehe māori kia whakamanahia Te Tiriti, kia whakamutua, kia hurihia hoki te ngaronga o te taiao
And ka rāhuitia te keri kōwhai hou i runga i te whenua atawhai, tae atu ki te whenua kaitiaki (stewardship land)
And ka whānui ake te pūtea mō Jobs for Nature – Mahi mō te Taiao
And ka tiakina ngā repo māori katoa e toe ana
And ka pūteatia te Action for Nature me ngā ārahi kanorau koiora taketake, tae atu ki ngā momo taonga
And ka piki te pūtea atawhai tūmatanui whānui, tae atu ki te Predator Free 2050
Output ka tiakina te 30% o te whenua me ngā moana
And ka whakaorangia i te iti rawa te 30% o te wāhi repo kua tūkino
Outcome ka tiakina ngā momo taketake me ngā pūnaha hauropi i te keri kōwhai i runga i te whenua atawhai
