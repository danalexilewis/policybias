# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te ngākau o te hononga ture a Te Tiriti mā te whakahou Ture Tiriti me te whakatau pono puta noa i te kāwanatanga. Ka whakahoutia ngā whakataunga Tiriti kia tango i ngā rara Full and Final, kia whakamana i te rangatiratanga hapū, ā, ka piki ngā rawa o Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi me ngā tūtohutanga here mō te whakahoki whenua. Ka whakahokia te whenua i murua hē, tae atu ki te tūhura i te pae tuatahi mō te whenua tūmataiti i te wā hoko. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Whakahou ture me ngā whakataunga mō Te Tiriti o Waitangi

Scenario: Ka whakatinanatia te mahere Te Tiriti
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere Te Tiriti
Then ka whakaae te Treaty of Waitangi Act ko Te Tiriti te kupu mana e mahi tahi ana me He Whakaputanga
And ka whakatinanatia te whakatau i runga i Te Tiriti puta noa i te kāwanatanga, me te whai pūtea a ngā iwi me ngā hapū hei hoa
And ka āhei te whakahou Treaty Settlements i ngā whiriwhiri tōkeke, kāore he rara Full and Final, ka mutu te large natural grouping, ā, ka whakamanatia te rangatiratanga hapū
And ka piki ngā rawa o Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi me ngā tūtohutanga here mō te whakahoki whenua a te Karauna me te whenua tūmataiti
And ka whakahokia te whenua i murua hē, tae atu ki te tūhura i te pae-tuatahi-ki-te-hoko mō te whenua tūmataiti i te wā hoko
Outcome ka whakamanatia te rangatiratanga hapū i ngā whakataunga me te whakahoki whenua
