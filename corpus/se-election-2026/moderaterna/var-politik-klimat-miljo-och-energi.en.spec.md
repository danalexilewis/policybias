# What the page states

> Sweden has the EU's lowest emissions per person and is to reach net zero by 2045 at the latest. Dispatchable electricity is to be rebuilt in the south so that households do not have to live by the electricity price. Climate money is to go where emissions fall most per krona. The Baltic Sea is to be protected against bottom trawling and eutrophication.

System: Dispatchable electricity, cost-effective emissions and protected seas

Scenario: Southern Sweden lacks dispatchable electricity
Given six reactors have been closed early
And southern Sweden lost 3 800 MW of dispatchable electricity production
When new nuclear power supplies southern Sweden
Then hydropower can be used for heavy industry in the north
And the state is to reduce risk through rules, financing and state ownership
And the electricity price, grid charges and energy taxes are to be kept as low as possible together
And capacity charges are to be recast so that ordinary families need not plan their lives around the power system
And dispatchable technologies are to be paid for their availability
Outcome electricity is to be there where it is needed when it is needed

Scenario: Sweden is to reach net zero without raising the petrol price
Given Sweden accounts for about 0.1 percent of global emissions
And China emits more carbon dioxide than the EU and the US together
When Klimatklivet and Industriklivet reward innovation
Then the climate premium for light lorries is extended
And Klimatklivet is strengthened among other things for charging infrastructure in homes and blocks of flats
And ineffective environmental assessments are to be simplified
Output 1.5 million tonnes lower emissions by 2030 according to the budget
Outcome emissions are to come down where each krona gives most, not through a higher petrol price

Scenario: The Baltic Sea is eutrophic and trawled
Given more than half of the world's dead bottoms are in the Baltic Sea
When bottom trawling is banned in Sweden's marine protected areas
Then co-operation against eutrophication, plastics and hazardous substances is to be deepened
And large-scale industrial trawling for fishmeal is to be stopped
Outcome inshore fishing and healthier seas are to be able to be secured
