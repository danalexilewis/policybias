# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka ū ki te huringa tūāpapa ture i runga i te Tiriti. Ka whakakahangia te mana tōrangapū Māori me ngā hanganga whakahaere. Ka kaunuitia te whakahoki whenua me ngā oati rangatiratanga i raro i te Tiriti o Waitangi.

System: Te mana motuhake me te hurihanga i runga i te Tiriti

Scenario: Ka ū ki te huringa tūāpapa ture i runga i te Tiriti
Given ka taea te whakakore i ia painga mō te Māori mēnā kāore he hurihanga tūāpapa ture
When ka ū ki te huringa tūāpapa ture i runga i te Tiriti
Then ka whakakahangia te mana tōrangapū Māori me ngā hanganga whakahaere
And ka kaunuitia te whakahoki whenua me ngā oati rangatiratanga i raro i te Tiriti o Waitangi
Outcome ka hoki te mana ki te tangata whenua, ki te hapū, ki te iwi, ki te whānau, ki te Māori hoki
