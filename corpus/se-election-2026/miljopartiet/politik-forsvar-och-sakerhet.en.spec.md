# What the page states

> The security situation is serious. Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine shows that Europe must defend Ukraine's freedom and joint total defence. Security is both military defence and protection for care, electricity, food, water and communications in a crisis. Rearmament is not to crowd out welfare.

System: Total defence without nuclear weapons on Swedish soil

Scenario: Total defence is rebuilt and nuclear weapons on Swedish soil are banned
Given Sweden is in a serious security-policy situation
And Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine
When military and civil defence are strengthened together
Then healthcare, railways, electricity supply, food, drinking water and communications are to function even in a crisis
And rearmament is funded among other things with a preparedness tax on the highest incomes so that welfare is not crowded out
And civil conscription is built out as a complement to military conscription
And stocks of food, medicines and necessary goods are secured
And air defence is to protect the civilian population
And Sweden supports Ukraine's freedom for as long as it is needed
And membership of NATO is respected, with demands for openness, democratic conditions and the principle of no first use
And nuclear weapons on Swedish soil are banned in peace and war
And anti-personnel mines are to remain banned and the Ottawa Convention is not to be left
Outcome resilience rises against war, cyber attacks and natural disasters at the same time
And an independent foreign policy is to be possible even as a NATO member
