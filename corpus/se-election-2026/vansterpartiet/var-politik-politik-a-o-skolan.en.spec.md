# What the page states

> Sweden is alone in unlimited profit on school activity; the school voucher ends up in groups and tax havens and the market gives grade inflation, segregation and inequality. The possibility of profit is to be abolished, school given more resources, and the state take back main responsibility from the municipalities so that the nearest school is a good school. Upper-secondary pupils are to have a right to school transport where there are no buses, and municipalities' right to charge for school food in upper secondary is to go.

System: A profit ban and state principalship for school

Scenario: School groups may no longer take out profit
Given Sweden as the only country in the world allows unlimited profit on school activity
And the school voucher goes to profits in school groups and tax havens
And the market-adapted school leads to grade inflation, segregation and greater inequality
And money for school has fallen over the past decades
When schools' possibility of making a profit is abolished
Then more support can be given to those who come from worse conditions
And efforts reach teaching, pupil health, special-needs teachers, after-school activity and social support
And teachers' workload eases through more colleagues, less administration and fewer national tests
Outcome a compensatory school that meets children on the basis of shifting needs
And school food that is vegetarian or organic, with low emissions and good animal protection

Scenario: The state takes the schools back and upper secondary becomes free to eat in
Given free school choice gathers pupils with different backgrounds in different schools
And municipalities may today charge a fee for school food in upper secondary
When the state takes back main responsibility for the schools from the municipalities
Then money can be distributed more according to the schools' and pupils' needs
And the nearest school is to be trusted as a good school
And after-school centres are made available to all pupils as an integrated part of the school day
And vocational programmes and academic-preparatory programmes give at least basic eligibility for higher education
Outcome upper-secondary pupils have a right to school transport where there are no buses
And the fee for school food in upper secondary is abolished
