# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakapūmautia ngā mōtika me te mana Māori ki te wai māori. E kore e whakatauhia ngā whakatau kāore he whakaaetanga Māori. Ka tiakina ngā rawa, ā, ka tuaritia tika ngā hua.

System: Te mana Māori ki te wai māori

Scenario: Ka whakapūmautia ngā mōtika me te mana Māori ki te wai māori
Given kei te wai te whakapapa, ā, nāna e whāngai te whenua me ngā whakatupuranga
When ka whakapūmautia ngā mōtika me te mana Māori ki te wai māori
Then e kore e whakatauhia ngā whakatau kāore he whakaaetanga Māori
And ka tiakina ngā rawa, ā, ka tuaritia tika ngā hua
Outcome kei a rātou ngā whakatau mō te wai, nā rātou nei i kawe te kawenga nō tua whakarere
