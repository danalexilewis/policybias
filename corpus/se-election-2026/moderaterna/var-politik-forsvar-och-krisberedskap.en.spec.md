# What the page states

> Sweden should be able to deter an armed attack and meet hybrid threats together with NATO. Rearmament should reach NATO's target earlier than the alliance's date, Ukraine should receive long-term support and the defence industry should deliver faster.

System: NATO target, Ukraine support and defence industry

Scenario: The defence appropriation is to reach NATO's military target
Given Russia is the defining threat to Sweden, Europe and NATO
And Sweden is expected to reach 2.8 percent of GDP in defence spending in 2026
When rearmament continues towards NATO's target
Then direct military spending should reach the alliance's target earlier than other allies
And the defence industry should contribute to expanded production capacity and faster delivery rates
And parts of the build-up are loan-financed now and should be carried permanently so that public finances are in balance by 2035 at the latest
Output 3.5 percent of GDP to direct military spending as early as 2030
And 1.5 percent of GDP to security- and defence-related investment
Outcome Sweden should deter Russia together with allies in northern Europe

Scenario: Ukraine needs aircraft and long-term support
Given Ukraine is fighting for its freedom and defending Sweden and Europe against Russian aggression
When support continues over the long term
Then donated aircraft are replaced with new Gripen E/F for the Swedish air force
And support should cover advanced materiel, training, ammunition and spare parts
Output 20 Gripen E/F that Ukraine is preparing to buy
And up to 16 JAS 39 Gripen C/D from its own fleet
And 40 billion kronor in support in 2026
And as much planned for 2027
Outcome Ukraine's defence is also an investment in Swedish security
