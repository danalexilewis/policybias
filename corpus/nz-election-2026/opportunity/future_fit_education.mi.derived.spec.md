# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Te ōrite utu ECE ki ngā kura, he rahi pokapū me ngā ōwehenga kaha ake, te mātauranga tōrangapū Tau 6, me te iti ake o te wā mata — engari kāore i utuhia te pire utu, ā, kāore he wāhi kua tapaina mā ngā whānau i ngā pokapū nui e whakaheke ana.

System: He ōrite utu kāore he pire

Scenario: Ka hiahiatia te ōrite utu ECE
When me utu ngā kaiako ECE ki ngā reiti kura tuatahi me te kura tuarua
Then ka piki te pire utu mō ngā pokapū
Outcome ko ngā pokapū hapori e utu kino ake nei ngā mea e hiahia ana te kaupapa here ki te tautoko
But kei te nama tonu ngā pokapū me ngā kaipānga tāke i te pire utu kāore he ara pūtea

Scenario: Ka taupanehia te rahi pokapū
When ka ngau ngā ture rahi me te ōwehenga
Then kāore ētahi wāhi o nāianei e ū
Outcome ko ngā pokapū nā te mātua, nā te hapori ngā whakakapi kua tapaina
But ka ngaro pea te wāhi a ngā whānau e whakamahi ana i ngā pokapū nui i mua i te tū o te whakakapi
