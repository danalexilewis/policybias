# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka noho ngā pāremata e rua me te mana inaki: ka tango te kāwanatanga tārewa i te Karauna hei upoko o te motu, ā, ka hono tētahi Pāremata Māori ki te Whare o nāianei. Ki te kore he ture papā, kāore tētahi whare e mōhio ko tā wai ture ka toa ina tautohe rāua—ā, me whai raupapa me tētahi ture utu te whakatinana i a Matike Mai me te whakahou whakataunga i mua i te neke o te taitara.

System: Te mana takirua, kāore he ture mātāmua

Scenario: Ka noho tētahi Pāremata Māori ki te taha o te Whare o nāianei
When ka whakatūria he Pāremata Māori
Then ka taea e ngā whare e rua te kī he mana tō rāua ki ngā wāhanga kotahi
Outcome ko te tino rangatiratanga te whāinga kua kīia
But ki te kore he ture papātanga, kāore tētahi whare e mōhio ko tā wai ture ka toa

Scenario: Ka ū ngātahi a Matike Mai me te whakahou whakataunga
When ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga katoa a Matike Mai, ā, ka whakahouhia te tukanga whakataunga Tiriti
Then me whai hinonga tango me tētahi ture utu anō te whakahoki whenua ki ngā whānau, hapū, iwi
Outcome kāore e kiia ngā whakataunga "full and final" he mea kua katia
But kāore e taea e ngā whānau, hapū, iwi te tango taitara kia tū rā anō he hinonga tango me tētahi ture utu
