# Our understanding

> The state takes risk in new nuclear power so that households need not steer their lives by the electricity price, but tearing down went quickly and building takes years. The budget's emissions cut is not enough for the ESR in 2030. The trawling ban applies to protected areas first.

System: State risk in baseload against households' capacity charges

Scenario: The capacity shortage in the south is met with reactors that do not yet exist
Given southern Sweden lost 3 800 MW of dispatchable electricity production
When the state steps in with financing, rules and ownership
Then new nuclear power is in time to feed the south
Outcome households are promised they need not plan around capacity charges
But the years to breaking ground are borne as German prices in bidding zones three and four

Scenario: The 2030 emissions target is not met with fuel tax
Given Sweden accounts for about 0.1 percent of global emissions
When the budget cuts emissions without raising the petrol price
Then the ESR commitment to 2030 becomes hard to reach
Output 1.5 million tonnes lower emissions by 2030
Outcome the remaining cut is pushed onto more expensive measures or later years
But households with a car miss the price signal that would have carried the ESR
