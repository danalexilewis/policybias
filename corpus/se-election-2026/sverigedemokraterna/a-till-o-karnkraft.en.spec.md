# What the page states

> Energy policy has been recast to restore the stability, price level and robustness that the electricity system had before half of nuclear power was shut down. The closures hit households and firms. It is quick to dismantle, but takes time to restore. New nuclear power is to be able to deliver large amounts of secure electricity and capacity for a long time. Earlier obstacles to investment have been removed and extensive state risk-sharing is offered.

System: New nuclear power with state risk-sharing

Scenario: The electricity system lacks the nuclear power that was shut down
Given half of Sweden's nuclear power was shut down
And the closures hit both households and firms hard and constituted a large economic loss
When new nuclear power is made possible by clearing earlier obstacles to investment and offering extensive state risk-sharing
Then new nuclear power is to deliver large amounts of secure electricity and capacity for a long time
And a robust energy system is to be rebuilt
Outcome the electricity system is to regain the stability, price level and robustness it had before the closures
