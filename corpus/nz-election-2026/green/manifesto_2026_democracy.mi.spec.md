# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ko te manapori te kanohi o te iwi, kaua ko ngā pānga kamupene taonga. Mā tētahi kōrerorero ā-motu mō te panoni ture taketake e whakamahi i a Matike Mai Aotearoa, ka horahia ngā mōtika pōti ki ngā tāngata 16 tau, ki te hunga i te hereherenga hoki, ā, ka tāmua ngā tūru Māori me ngā wā hurihanga rārangi e roa ake ana. Ka tepea ngā takoha ki ngā kaipōti kua rēhitatia, ka nui ake te pūtea tūmatanui mō ngā kauhau pōti, ā, ka noho te whaiwhai ki tētahi rēhita me tētahi tikanga mahi. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te kanohi manapori me te whakahou pūtea tōrangapū

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere manapori
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere manapori
Then ka ū tētahi kōrerorero ā-motu mō te panoni ture taketake ki a He Whakaputanga me Te Tiriti mā te whakamahi i a Matike Mai Aotearoa
And ka horahia te pakeke pōti ki te 16, me ngā mōtika pōti mō te hunga katoa i te hereherenga, ā, ka whakahokia te rēhita tae noa ki te rā pōti
And ka tāmua ngā tūru pōti Māori, ā, ka roa ake te wā hurihanga rārangi Māori
And ka herea ngā takoha ki ngā kaipōti kua rēhitatia, ka nui ake te pūtea tūmatanui mō ngā kauhau pōti, ā, ka tepea te awe o te kaitakoha
And ka whakakaha tētahi rēhita kaiwhaiwhai me tētahi tikanga mahi i te mārama o te whaiwhai
Outcome ka ū Te Tiriti me te hononga pono ki te whakatau
