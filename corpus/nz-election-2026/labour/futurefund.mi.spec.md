# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka haumi tētahi Future Fund e kāwanatia motuhake ana ki te hanganga me ngā pakihi Kiwi auaha, ka ruatia ki ngā rawa nā te Karauna me tētahi takoha pūtea tuatahi. E noho ana i te taha, wehe anō i te NZ Super Fund. Ka tiakina ā-ture ngā rawa kua ruatia kia kore e hokona.
