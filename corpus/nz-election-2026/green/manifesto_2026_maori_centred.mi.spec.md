# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka hoki te mana me ngā rawa ki te tangata whenua hei kaiwhakatau mō ō rātou whānau, hapū, iwi hoki. Ka whai pūtea te Marae Resilience Fund i ngā kaupapa e ārahi ana te marae, me te pūtea ohorere ā-iwi e haere tonu ana. Ka mutu ngā rīhi mutunga-kore i runga i te whenua Māori, ā, ka tiakina e te Public Works Act te whenua e tata ana ki ngā marae, ngā urupā, ngā wāhi tapu hoki. Ka whakawhānuihia te ako me te whakamahi i te reo Māori ki te motu katoa. Kua whakaingoatia te Marae Resilience Fund, kāore he nui tāra. Kāore he wātaka i homai.

System: Te kaupapa here e arotahi ana ki te Māori, me te pakari o te marae

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere e arotahi ana ki te Māori

And ka whakaiti te haumi i ngā rerekētanga e pāngia ana te tangata whenua i ngā putanga pāpori, ōhanga hoki

And ka whakawhānuihia te ako, te kōrero, te whakatairanga hoki i te reo Māori i ngā wāhi ōkawa, ōpaki hoki, ki te motu katoa
