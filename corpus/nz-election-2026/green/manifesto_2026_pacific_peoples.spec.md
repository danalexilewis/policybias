# What the page states

> Pacific partnership deepens through visa-free travel, restored climate aid and fair seasonal work replacing the Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme. Tangata moana climate leadership is resourced within Aotearoa while independence for West Papua and French-occupied Polynesia is supported. Marine protection funding increases for the South Pacific Regional Environment Programme and Pacific protected areas under the High Seas Treaty. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Pacific peoples policy and regional solidarity

Scenario: The Pacific peoples plan is put into effect
When the government puts the Pacific peoples plan into effect
Then visa-free travel to Aotearoa is extended to visitors from Pacific Islands
And international climate aid funding is restored and increased with support for Pacific climate negotiations
And tangata moana climate leadership within Aotearoa is resourced
And the Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme is replaced with fair wages, housing, open work rights and residency pathways
And independence of West Papua and French-occupied Polynesia is supported with an Indigenous-led human rights investigation
And the South Pacific Regional Environment Programme and Pacific marine protected areas under the High Seas Treaty receive increased funding support
Outcome seasonal workers are not tied to a single employer
