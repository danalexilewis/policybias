# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kāore e taea te kaukau i te haurua o ngā awa, ā, kei te mōrearea te kanorau koiora ahurei. Ka piki te pūtea tiaki taiao me te haumaru koiora mō ngā repo me te whakatō ngahere Māori, ka whai te kounga wai i te tirohanga motuhake, ā, ka āwhinatia ngā kaipupuri whenua ki te huri ki ngā whakamahinga nui te uara, iti te pānga. Ka utu tētahi mākete taiao mō te kanorau koiora i te taha o te ngahere Māori hipoki-tonu me tētahi ōhanga kirihou porohita e hāngai ana ki te mahere ara ahumahi. Kei te whakarewa tonu te kaupapa here taipitopito.

System: Whenua hauora

Scenario: Ka huri ngā ture whenua, wai me te taiao
Given kāore e taea te kaukau i te haurua o ngā awa, ā, kei te mōrearea te kanorau koiora ahurei
When ka piki te pūtea tiaki taiao me te haumaru koiora, me te arotahi ki ngā repo, te whakatō ngahere Māori, me ētahi atu kaupapa nui te kanorau koiora
Then ka pai ake te kounga wai mā te waeture kaha me te tirohanga motuhake
And ka tautokona ngā kaipupuri whenua ki te huri ki ngā whakamahinga nui te uara, iti te pānga, mā te whakamahere mokowā, ngā tikanga pai ki te taiao, me te hangarau e whakaheke ana i te pēhitihaiti me te whakamōmona hanga
And ka whakangao ki te ngahere Māori hipoki-tonu me tētahi mākete taiao e utu ana mō te kanorau koiora
And ka hoahoatia tētahi ōhanga kirihou porohita ki te New Plastics Economy Roadmap
Outcome ka taea e ngā pakihi aratakina e te kaupapa te whakatupu rawa mā te whakaora i te taiao
