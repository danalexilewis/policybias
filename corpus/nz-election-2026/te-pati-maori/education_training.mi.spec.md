# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka hangaia te mana mātauranga aratakina e te Māori me ngā otinga aratakina e te Māori. Ka tāmua tika te mātauranga Māori puta noa i te ako. Ka wewetea ngā aukati e arai ana i ngā whānau i te mātauranga me te whakangungu, ā, ka tautokona te mana whakahaere Māori me te whakatau Māori i te mātauranga.

System: Mātauranga me te whakangungu aratakina e te Māori

Scenario: Ka hangaia te mana mātauranga aratakina e te Māori me ngā otinga aratakina e te Māori
Given me whakakaha te mātauranga i te tuakiri, ā, me whakarite te Māori kia ārahi hei Māori
When ka hangaia te mana mātauranga aratakina e te Māori me ngā otinga aratakina e te Māori
Then ka tāmua tika te mātauranga Māori puta noa i te ako
And ka wewetea ngā aukati e arai ana i ngā whānau i te mātauranga me te whakangungu
And ka tautokona te mana whakahaere Māori me te whakatau Māori i te mātauranga
Outcome ka tupu te māia, ngā ara, me te hautūtanga o ngā tamariki, me te kore e waiho i a rātou anō i te kūaha
