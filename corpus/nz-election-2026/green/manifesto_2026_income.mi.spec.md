# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakapūmautia ngā moni whiwhi e ora ai te tangata mā te whakahou i te toko i te ora me tētahi pūnaha tāke tika. Ka piki ngā tautoko pūtea ki ngā taumata e ora ai te tangata, ka whakakorehia ngā whiu, ka whakawhānuihia Te Kaporeihana Āwhina Hunga Whara ki tua atu i te whara, ā, ka kaha ake te Working for Families i te taha o te tāke moni whiwhi ā-taumata. Ka utua te kōpaki e tētahi tāke rawa i te hunga tino taonga. Kāore he wātaka i homai.

System: Ngā moni whiwhi e ora ai te tangata, me ngā tautuhinga tāke

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere moni whiwhi
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere moni whiwhi
Then ka whakahouhia te Social Security Act, me tētahi tauira whakapono nui i Work and Income
And ka piki ngā tautoko pūtea ki ngā taumata e ora ai te tangata, me ngā tika takitahi, ā, ka whakakorehia ngā whiu
And ka kapi i Te Kaporeihana Āwhina Hunga Whara te tautoko moni whiwhi me te maimoatanga, ahakoa te pūtake o te whara, te hauātanga, te māuiui rānei
And ka piki ngā utu Working for Families, ka iti ake ngā whakaheke, ā, ka toro ngā whiwhinga tāke ki ngā whānau kāore i te mahi
And ka ā-taumata te tāke moni whiwhi, me tētahi paepae kore tāke, he reiti iti ake mō te hunga iti me te hunga waenga te moni whiwhi, me tētahi reiti o runga teitei ake
And ka noho ngā āwhina whakahoki hei takuhe, ka murua ngā nama a Te Manatū Whakahiato Ora, ā, ka rāhuitia ngā ota piri ki te hunga tautoko pūtea
Output he tāke 2.5% i te rawa toenga nui ake i te $10 million
Outcome ka iti ake te pōharatanga mā ngā huringa toko i te ora me ngā huringa tāke
