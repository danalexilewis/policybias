# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kua tae kē mai te huringa āhuarangi, ā, me mahi te whakaheke me te urutaunga. Ka whakakaha te Emissions Trading Scheme mā te whakauru i ngā tukunga pāmu, te aukati i ngā ngahere tauiwi hou, me te whakahoki i ngā tahua a te Climate Change Commission. Ka tautokona ngā kaunihera mō ngā otinga waipuke me te wera ā-taiao, ka pai ake te kounga hau mā ngā whakatenatena ōhanga, ā, ka utua te hopu waro i te oneone me ngā repo, ehara i ngā maara paina. Kei te tīmata tonu te kaupapa here taipitopito.
