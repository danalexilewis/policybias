# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka tuku tētahi rohe ōhanga motuhake ki Marsden Point me Northport i ngā whakaaetanga whakamahere me te RMA ki tētahi pae kāwanatanga, me ngā whakatenatena tāke kei te whakaaroarohia anake. Ko te tere whakamahere te whakamāmā kua tāpaetia; kāore he reiti tāke, he tahua, he rā rānei i whakatauria.

System: Te rohe ōhanga motuhake hei whakamāmā whakamahere i mua i ngā whakatenatena tāke

Scenario: Ka whakatūria te rohe ki Marsden Point
Given ka mahi kē ngā ahumahi i te rohe ki ngā kaunihera ā-rohe me ngā kaunihera ā-rohe nui, Te Papa Atawhai me ētahi atu tari i raro i te RMA
When ka whakatūria tētahi rohe ōhanga motuhake e kapi ana i te whare tinei hinu o mua me Northport
Then ka whakatauhia wawe ngā ture whakamahere me ngā whakaaetanga RMA e tētahi pae kāwanatanga
Outcome ka whakaarohia te whakaputa pūngao me ngā mahi moana, tae atu ki te whakatika kaipuke, i te rohe
But ko te whakamāmā matua kua tāpaetia ko te tere whakamahere, ehara i tētahi tapahi tāke kua whakaingoatia

Scenario: Ka tae mai tētahi whakaaetanga kua tukuna kē ki ngā kaiwhakatau ā-rohe
Given ka pupuri kē ngā kaunihera ā-rohe me ngā kaunihera ā-rohe nui me ētahi atu tari i ngā tūranga RMA me te whakamahere
When ka whakatau te pae kāwanatanga i te whakaaetanga i runga i te tipu ōhanga me ngā whāinga o te rohe
Then ka tangohia te pāpā ā-rohe ki tērā mahi
Outcome ka huri ngā whakaaetanga ki ngā whakamātautau tipu me te rohe a te pae
But kāore e pupuri ngā kaiputu reiti me ngā kaunihera i te kupu whakamahere whakamutunga i roto i te rohe

Scenario: Ka paunatia ngā whakatenatena tāke me te haumi i muri i te tū o te rohe
When ka whakaarohia ngā whakatenatena tāke, ngā huringa Overseas Investment Act me ngā whakamāmā reiti
Then ka noho aua kōwhiringa ki te whakaaroaro, kāore i tāpaetia
Outcome ka taea tonu te rohe te mahi i runga i te whakamāmā whakamahere anake
But ehara anō tētahi tukunga tāke i te oati e taea e tētahi pakihi te whakawhirinaki
