# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka kaha ake te tiaki taiao mā ngā tepenga hauora pūnaha hauropi e whai taunakitanga ana, ko te tangata whenua he hoa whakatau. Ka whakakorea te Fast-track Approvals Act, ka whakakorea hoki ngā whakaaetanga keri hōrapa, ka hoki mai he Manatū Mō Te Taiao motuhake me te nui ake o ngā rauemi mō Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te uruhi, ā, ka hāngai ngā paerewa kounga hau ki ngā tiakanga hauora ā-ao. Ka whāia kia whakakorea ngā urunga koiora, tae atu ki ngā paina porokati, te Caulerpa me ngā pī waiū waewae kōwhai, ā, ka whakatūria anō te Community Environment Fund. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

And ka whakahokia he Manatū Mō Te Taiao motuhake me te nui ake o ngā rauemi mō Te Mana Rauhī Taiao me te uruhi

Outcome ka tiakina, ka whakaorangia te taiao hei tūāpapa mō ngā hapori hauora
