# What the page states

> Immigration is to be tightened and control retaken over who is in the country, so that integration can succeed. Asylum-related immigration is to come down towards the EU-law minimum. Highly qualified labour is to be given priority. Those who commit crime or lack a permit are to leave.

System: Tightened asylum, a higher wage requirement and more returns

Scenario: Asylum-related immigration is to come down to the EU floor
Given about 1.2 million foreign citizens immigrated in the twelve years before the change of government
And over 700 000 Swedish citizenships were granted in the same period
And 320 000 asylum seekers were received in eight years without a plan
And the number of asylum seekers has fallen by 60 percent since 2022
When asylum legislation is aligned with the EU-law minimum
Then requirements of Swedish, civic knowledge, self-support and longer residence are to apply for citizenship
And those sentenced to a penalty stricter than a fine are as a rule to be expelled
Outcome asylum-related residence permits are to lie at the lowest level since 1985

Scenario: An employer wants to recruit from a country outside the EU
Given around 350 000 people are registered with the Public Employment Service
And first-time permits granted in 2023 amounted to less than 0.5 percent of the labour force
And processing times for the highly qualified have fallen from over 100 days to under 20 days
When the support requirement is raised
Then exemptions from the wage requirement are to be able to apply to certain occupations
Output just over 33 000 kronor
And 90 percent of median pay
And 80 percent of median pay, which the requirement was raised to in November 2023
Outcome low-paid labour immigration from third countries is to be replaced by people already in the country

Scenario: A person is in Sweden without a valid permit
Given the Tax Agency estimates that between 110 000 and 185 000 are in the country without a valid permit
And the number of returns has risen by 60 percent since 2022
When a census, reception centres and more inland immigration controls are carried out
Then EBO is sharply restricted
And a duty to inform is to cover the Public Employment Service, the Social Insurance Agency, the Prison and Probation Service, the Enforcement Authority, the Pensions Agency and the Tax Agency
Output 350 000 kronor in re-migration grant for adults
And 25 000 kronor for children
And at most 500 000 kronor for spouses or cohabitants
And at most 600 000 kronor per family
Outcome more people who have no right to be in the country are to leave
