# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakakorea te Independent Māori Statutory Board kāore i pōtitia mā tētahi pire mema, ā, ka hoki ōna mahi, ōna rawa me ōna here ki te Auckland Council. Ko te hunga e whakaawe i te whakapau pūtea tūmatanui ka noho haepapa tōtika ki ngā kaipānga, me te kore aukati i te whai wāhi a te kaunihera ki a Māori. Kāore he wātaka whakakore i homai.

System: Te whakakore i te Independent Māori Statutory Board o Tāmaki Makaurau

Scenario: Ka whakakorea te Independent Māori Statutory Board
Given kua nui te whakaawe a te Independent Māori Statutory Board i ngā whakatau a te kaunihera mai i te 2010
And e utu ana ngā kaipānga i te $3.5 million ia tau hei tautoko i ngā mahi IMSB, kāore he āheinga ki te pōti, te tango rānei i ōna mema
When ka whakakorea te Independent Māori Statutory Board
Then ko te hunga e whakaawe i te whakapau pūtea tūmatanui me te whakatau ka noho haepapa tōtika ki te iwi
And ka hoki ngā mahi, ngā rawa me ngā here a te IMSB ki te Auckland Council
