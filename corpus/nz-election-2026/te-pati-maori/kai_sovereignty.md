### What we’ll do

*   Protect Māori food systems (including **seed sovereignty**, rejecting GE foods, and upholding **WAI262**).
*   Establish a **Māori Kai Sovereignty Board** to oversee kai justice.
*   Invest in community food systems (including **māra kai** and food farms).
*   Support rangatahi into food and primary industries.
*   Invest in **regenerative Māori agriculture**.
*   Break corporate control by supporting Māori-owned supermarkets and protecting local kai sellers.
*   Reduce grocery costs (including removing **GST from kai** and regulating supermarket price hikes).
*   Increase access to land for food production.

### Why it matters

Aotearoa produces more than enough kai, but too many whānau go without. Food insecurity is a system failure — kai should be affordable, healthy, and connected to whenua and whakapapa.
