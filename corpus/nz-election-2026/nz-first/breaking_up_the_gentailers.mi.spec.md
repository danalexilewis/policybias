# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka wehewehea ngā kamupene hiko nui hei kaiwhakaputa me ngā kaihoko motuhake, i te taha o tētahi rautaki pūngao ā-motu. Mā ngā ture utu e aukati ngā kamupene kia pupuri i te tuku kia piki ai ngā utu, ā, ka whiwhi te whakaputa hou i ngā kirimana utu-pūmau wā roa me te tuku tuatahi. Ka taea e ngā kāinga e whakaputa ana i te hiko rā te hoko hiko ki te matiti i te utu hokohoko e utua ana e rātou. Kāore he utu kirimana, he wātaka rānei i homai.

System: Te wehenga mākete hiko me te rautaki pūngao ā-motu

Scenario: Ka wehewehea ngā gentailers, ka whakahoungia te utu hiko
Given e whakahaere ana ngā kamupene hiko nui e whā i te 90% tata o te whakaputa hiko, ka hoko anō ki a rātou anō
And mā te kaiwhakaputa utu nui rawa e whakatau te utu mō te hiko katoa i raro i te pūnaha o nāianei
When ka wehewehea ngā kamupene hiko nui hei kaiwhakaputa me ngā kaihoko
Then kāore e taea e ngā kamupene te pupuri i te tuku kia piki noa ai ngā utu
And ka whakamanahia ngā kirimana utu-pūmau wā roa mō ngā kaupapa whakaputa hanga hou, ā, ka whakamahia tō rātou hiko tuatahi
And ka taea e ngā kāinga e whakaputa ana i te hiko rā te hoko hiko ki te matiti i te utu kotahi e utua ana e rātou mōna
Outcome he teihana hiko nui ake, he pūngao whakahou, he whakataetae me te manawaroa
