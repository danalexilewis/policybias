### What we’ll do

*   End the pipeline that removes Māori children from their people.
*   Restore **whānau authority** and invest in whānau-led care.
*   Make the state accountable for harm caused through uplifts and placements.

### Why it matters

Our tamariki are not state problems to be managed. They are whakapapa, promise, and future leaders born to thrive. Protecting mokopuna Māori means restoring whānau authority and ending the systems that separate our children from their people.
