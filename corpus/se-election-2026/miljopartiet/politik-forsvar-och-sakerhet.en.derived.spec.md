# Our understanding

> Rearmament is to be paid at the top of the income scale so hospitals and schools do not become the residual. NATO is accepted, not rolled back. The nuclear-weapons ban and the mine ban are legal fences inside that membership. Ukraine support is open-ended in time and unpriced in kronor.

System: Financed total defence inside a fenced alliance

Scenario: Welfare is ring-fenced by a top preparedness tax
When a preparedness tax on the highest incomes is levied to pay for rearmament
Then the military and civil build-up should not raid the welfare budget
Outcome hospitals, railways and food stores can in principle rise together
But if the tax does not yield enough, the crowding-out the policy wants to avoid returns through the ordinary budget

Scenario: Nuclear weapons are banned on Swedish soil while NATO membership stands
When a law forbids nuclear weapons on Swedish territory in peace and in war
Then no allied warhead is to be based or parked here
Outcome deterrence for the alliance still rests on nuclear weapons held somewhere else
But transit, consultation and wartime basing are alliance practice the statute would need to override
