# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka utua e te hunga tino whai rawa he wāhi nui ake. Ka tukuna te pūtea i kohia ki te whare noho, te hauora, te mātauranga me te tautoko whānau. Kāore he tūāpapa tāke, he pāpātanga, he paepae, he whakatau tata pūtea rānei i homai.

System: Te pūtea mai i te hunga tino whai rawa, ka tukuna ki ngā whakapaunga pāpori

Scenario: Ka utua e te hunga tino whai rawa he wāhi nui ake
Given he pūnaha tāke e whakapiki ana i te hunga runga, e tāmi ana i ētahi atu katoa
When ka utua e te hunga tino whai rawa he wāhi nui ake
Then ka tukuna te pūtea i kohia ki te whare noho, te hauora, te mātauranga me te tautoko whānau
