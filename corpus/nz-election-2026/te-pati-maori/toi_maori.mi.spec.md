# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka haumitia te toi Māori me ngā ahumahi auaha Māori. Ka tiakina ngā mōtika ahurea Māori me ngā whakapuakitanga. Ka tautokona te tūāhanga toi, te pūtea, me ngā ara nā te Māori e ārahi ana.

System: Te toi Māori me ngā ahumahi auaha Māori

Scenario: Ka haumitia te toi Māori me ngā ahumahi auaha Māori
Given ko te toi Māori te kōrero pono, te ātete, te whakaora, te tuakiri, me te taonga tuku iho
When ka haumitia te toi Māori me ngā ahumahi auaha Māori
Then ka tiakina ngā mōtika ahurea Māori me ngā whakapuakitanga
And ka tautokona te tūāhanga toi, te pūtea, me ngā ara nā te Māori e ārahi ana
Outcome ka kawe te toi Māori i ngā kōrero i whakamūmūtia, ā, ka whiu i te pohewa ki te anamata
