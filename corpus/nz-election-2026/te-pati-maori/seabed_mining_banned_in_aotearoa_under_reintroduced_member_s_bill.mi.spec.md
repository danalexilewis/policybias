# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā te Seabed Mining Prohibition Act e kati ai ngā huarahi ture katoa mō te keri takere moana, tae atu ki ngā whakaaetanga fast-track, puta noa i te rohe moana takutai, te rohe ōhanga motuhake me te paparahi whenua. E karanga ana tētahi petihana kia rāhuitia pūmau ki ngā New Zealand waters. Kua rite te Pire mō te pōti Pire Mema. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te rāhui pūmau i te keri takere moana, tae atu ki te fast-track

Scenario: Ka whakaaroaro te Pāremata i tētahi Pire Mema i whakaurua anō hei aukati i te keri takere moana
Given kua kitea ka puta i te keri takere moana he mamae kāore e tukua, ahakoa i raro i te ture fast-track
When ka whakaaroarohia te Seabed Mining Prohibition Act
Then ka katia ngā huarahi ture katoa mō te keri takere moana, tae atu ki ngā whakaaetanga fast-track
And ka pā te rāhui puta noa i te rohe moana takutai, te rohe ōhanga motuhake, me te paparahi whenua
Outcome ka tiakina te moana kia mōhio pū ki te keri takere moana

Scenario: E tautoko ana tētahi petihana i te rāhui pūmau i roto i ngā New Zealand waters
Given kua 14 tau ngā iwi, ngā hapū me ngā kaitiaki e whawhai ana ki te keri takere moana
And ka puta i te keri takere moana he pānga nui, e kore e taea te hoki, ki ngā pūnaha hauropi moana me te tikanga Māori
When ka whakamahia tētahi petihana hei whakaatu i te tautoko mō te Seabed Mining Prohibition Act
Then ka peia kia puta he rāhui ture pūmau i te keri takere moana i roto i ngā New Zealand waters
Outcome ka tiakina te moana kia mōhio pū ki te keri takere moana
