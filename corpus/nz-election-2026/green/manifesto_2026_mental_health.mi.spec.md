# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakawhānuihia ngā ratonga hauora hinengaro kore utu kia kore rawa te utu e aukati, tae atu ki ngā hōtaka mō te rangatahi me ērā e hāngai ana ki te ahurea. Ka whakatinanahia te rautaki ārai whakamomori Every Life Matters me ngā mahi kaupapa Māori e arotahi ana ki te hunga i raro i te 24. Ka āta whakakorehia te noho wehe i te mea ka whakaheke te wawao wawe i te taurima here, ā, ka hono ngā huarahi tuku tūroro i ngā ratonga tuatahi me ngā ratonga tuarua. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te urunga ki te hauora hinengaro me te ārai whakamomori

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere hauora hinengaro
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere hauora hinengaro
Then ka whakawhānuihia ngā ratonga hauora hinengaro kore utu, tae atu ki a Piki, a Mana Ake, ngā Youth One Stop Shops, a Asian Family Services me te tautoko i muri i te whānau tamariki
And ka whakatinanahia te rautaki ārai whakamomori Every Life Matters me ngā mahi kaupapa Māori e arotahi ana ki te hunga i raro i te 24
And ka āta whakakorehia te noho wehe, ā, ka nui ake te wawao wawe e whakaheke ana i te taurima here
And ka pūteatia ngā hōtaka hauora hinengaro me te waranga kaupapa Māori, ā, ka whai wāhi te reo rangatahi Māori ki te kaupapa here
And ka honoa ngā ratonga hauora hinengaro tuatahi me ngā tuarua ki ngā huarahi tuku tūroro mārama i ia rohe hauora
Outcome ka tuku te noho wehe ā-whare ki ngā tauira taurima hapori
