# What the page states

> Seniors and kaumātua thrive through a long-term ageing strategy, expanded community spaces and suitable intergenerational housing. Papakāinga building expands with kaumātua prioritised on social housing waitlists and home care workers paid equitably including travel time. Māori, Pacific and migrant organisations are resourced for culturally aligned kaumātua care. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Support for seniors and kaumātua

Scenario: The seniors plan is put into effect
When the government puts the seniors plan into effect
Then a long-term ageing strategy covers kaumātua wellbeing, elder abuse prevention, equitable income and a state-owned not-for-profit retirement village scheme
And funding for community spaces, facilities, programmes and volunteering accessible to seniors increases
And more papakāinga are built, kaumātua are prioritised on social housing waitlists and housing is single-level, warm and intergenerational
And home and community care workers are well trained, supported and paid equitably including travel time
And Māori, Pacific and migrant organisations are resourced for kaumātua care aligned with tikanga
Outcome seniors can access culturally grounded care and suitable housing
