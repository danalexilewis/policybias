# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> He nui ngā hoa tūkino e whara ana i ngā kararehe hoa, ā, he nui ngā pāmamae ka whakaroa te wehe kia tiakina ngā mōkai. Ka hara te kaupapa here i te whakamahi i te tūkino kararehe hoa hei aukati i te whānau, ka whakakaha i ngā Ōta Tiaki, ka tono i Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa kia tuhi ngā mōkai ki ngā pūrongo wharanga ā-whānau, ā, ka hoatu te mana mārama ki te tango kararehe ki te wāhi haumaru. Kāore he wātaka whakatinana i homai.

System: Te tiaki kararehe hoa i te aukati tūkino ā-whānau

Scenario: Ka urupare Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa ki te tūkino ā-whānau e whakamahia ana te mōkai hei aukati
Given i tata ki te 11 ngā wā te nui ake o te tūponotanga ka āta whara te hoa tūkino i tētahi kararehe i te hoa kāore e tūkino ana
And neke atu i te haurua o ngā pāmamae i whakaroa te wehe i te hononga tūkino nā te mea i pīrangi rātou ki te haumaru o ā rātou mōkai
And tērā pea ka oma tētahi pāmamae i tētahi karanga, ka mahue te mōkai ā-whānau ki te taha o te kaipatu e whakapaehia ana
When ka whakatikahia te Crimes Act kia hara te whakamahi i te tūkino kararehe hoa hei aukati, hei whakahaere rānei i tētahi hononga tūkino ā-whānau
Then tērā pea ka eke te whiu ki te whitu tau i te whare herehere
Output ka whakakahangia ngā Ōta Tiaki kia kore e taea e te kaipatu te pupuri, te hoko, te tuku, te whakakore rānei i te kararehe hoa o te tangata e tiakina ana
And kāore ngā kararehe hoa e mahue ki tētahi kaipatu mōrearea ina tukuna tētahi Ōta Tiaki
And me tuhi Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa i ngā mōkai ki ngā pūrongo wharanga ā-whānau
And ka hoatu ki Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa te mana ture mārama me te aratohu mahi mārama ki te tango kararehe hoa ki tētahi wāhi haumaru pērā i a Pet Refuge, i te SPCA rānei, ina mōrearea te kararehe, ina whakamahia rānei hei aukati
Outcome ka taea e ngā pāmamae te wehe i te tūkino me te kore e mahue tētahi wāhanga o tō rātou whānau
And kāore e taea e ngā kaipatu te whakamahi mōkai hei patu aukati
