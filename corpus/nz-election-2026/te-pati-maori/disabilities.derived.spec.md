# Our understanding

> Māori-led disability services need a receiving entity with decision rights, not just a preference for Māori providers. Easier, less degrading access means changing assessments, waiting rules or appeal paths—and funding that fits whānau realities is a redesign of eligibility categories plus a Budget line.

System: Māori-led support without the funding design

Scenario: Māori-led services are backed
Given disabled Māori face a degrading system to access care and dignity
When Māori-led disability services and decision-making are supported
Then a receiving entity and a decision right must still be named
Outcome support is meant to honour mana and whānau
But without that entity, officials still hold the service

Scenario: Access and funding are to change together
When support is made easier to access and less degrading
And funding is meant to fit whānau realities rather than bureaucratic categories
Then eligibility categories and a Budget line must be chosen
Outcome access should cost less dignity to use
But until funding and category reform are named, the degrading path remains the default
