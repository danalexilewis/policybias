# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka here te kaupapa here i te pakihi ā-rohe ki te Buy Kiwi Made, te hoko a ngā tari, ngā karāti ICT, te whakawhanaketanga ā-rohe, me te whakahou whakataetae. Kāore he hea hoko, kāore he rahi karāti, kāore he utu pikinga tari i tāpirihia.

System: Te mātāmua ā-rohe me te whakataetae, kāore he papa here

Scenario: Ka kīia ngā tari kia nui ake te hoko i ngā pakihi o Aotearoa
Given e kōpeketia ana ngā kaiwhakarato ā-rohe i te wā e tuku ana ngā kamupene nui i ngā hua ki tāwāhi
When ka oati ngā tari kāwanatanga kia nui ake te hoko taonga me ngā ratonga i ngā pakihi o Aotearoa
Then ka nui ake te hea o ngā pakihi ā-rohe i te hiahia tūmatanui mēnā ka here te oati i ngā kaihoko
Outcome ka taea te noho o te rawa ki ngā hapori ā-rohe ina tino neke te hoko
But ka taea e ngā kaihoko te noho ki te hoko-nui ake hei ārahitanga ki te kore tētahi hea, ture rānei e here i a rātou

Scenario: Ka toro te Buy Kiwi Made me ngā tapanga whenua-taketake
When ka whakaorangia te Buy Kiwi Made, ā, ka toro ngā tapanga whenua-taketake
Then ka māmā ake te wehewehe a ngā kaihoko i ngā taonga ā-rohe
Outcome ka mārama ake te kōwhiringa papa mō ngā kaihoko e hiahia ana ki te tautoko i ngā pakihi ā-rohe
But kāore te tapanga anake e huri i te utu, nā reira ka taea te haere tonu o te kōpeke i ngā kaiwhakarato ā-rohe

Scenario: Ka nui ake ngā mana o ngā tari whakataetae ki te tūhura
When ka whakahouria ngā ture whakataetae, ā, ka riro i ngā tari waeture ngā rauemi me ngā mana ki ngā mahi ātete-whakataetae
Then ka taea e ngā pakihi iti me ngā kaihoko te kawe i ngā kēhi kaha ake ki ngā kaitākaro kua whakaemi, pērā i te duopoly hokomaha
Outcome ka taea te tītaha o te papa tākaro ki ngā pakihi iti ake mēnā ka tūturu ngā tūhuratanga
But me whakakī kaimahi, me utu hoki te Commerce Commission i ia tūhuratanga e whakahaerehia ana
