# Te reo Māori glossary for NZ 2026 Gurki specs

Shared vocabulary for `.mi.spec.md` siblings. Institution names below are **official** bilingual names from [Te Kawa Mataaho / Public Service Commission — Central government organisations](https://www.publicservice.govt.nz/system/central-government-organisations), or a **previous official** name where the agency no longer publishes a Māori name. Do not invent ministry or agency names. If an organisation is not in this list, keep the English name.

Gurki keywords stay English: `System`, `Scenario`, `Given`, `When`, `Then`, `And`, `But`, `Output`, `Outcome`, `Activates`.

Leave te reo already in the source (whānau, hapū, iwi, Te Tiriti, mana motuhake, mātauranga Māori, mokopuna, takatāpui, toi Māori, whenua, moana, kai) as it stands when it already reads well.

## Previous official names (agency gone or Māori name dropped)

Use these when the spec is talking about that body, not a made-up replacement.

| English | Previous official te reo |
| --- | --- |
| Māori Health Authority | Te Aka Whai Ora |
| Ministry for the Environment | Manatū Mō Te Taiao |
| Ministry of Transport | Te Manatū Waka |
| Ministry of Housing and Urban Development | Te Tūāpapa Kura Kāinga |
| Office for Māori Crown Relations | Te Arawhiti |

Do not invent a Māori name for a body that Te Kawa Mataaho lists in English only.

The current Ministry for Cities, Environment, Regions and Transport has **no** Māori name on that list. Do not coin one. If the spec is talking about a predecessor (environment, transport, or housing), use that predecessor's last official name above. If it means the merged ministry as a whole, keep the English name.

Proposed ministries that do not exist yet (for example Ministry for Energy) stay in English.

Immigration New Zealand has no standalone official Māori name on that list. Keep **Immigration New Zealand**.

## Party names

Keep **Te Pāti Māori**. Keep ACT, Labour, National, Green, NZ First, and TOP as in the source unless the source already uses a Māori name. Do not invent party names.

## Numbers and proper nouns

Leave figures, dates, dollar amounts, URLs, tags, cluster ids, party ids, and `id` untouched. Official English names of bills and funds (NZ Super Fund, Future Fund) stay English unless this glossary gives an official Māori name.
