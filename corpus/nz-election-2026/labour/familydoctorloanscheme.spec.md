# What the page states

> Owner-operated general practices can borrow to buy into a practice through a low-interest loan scheme from 1 July 2027. Corporate-owned practices are excluded. The scheme aims to free up GP appointments alongside broader primary care changes.

System: Family Doctor Loan Scheme

Scenario: A doctor buys into an owner-operated general practice
Given owner-operated general practices face funding pressure
When the Family Doctor Loan Scheme is delivered
Then owner-operated practices can borrow to buy in
Output up to 90% of practice purchase cost lent
And loan capped at $500,000
And interest-free for the first two years
And 3% annual interest on the outstanding balance after that
And 10 years to repay in total
And up to 50 loans available every year
And $25 million a year lent if fully subscribed
And less than $8 million a year forgone interest if fully subscribed
Outcome more than 4.5 million GP appointments freed up a year nationwide
