# What the page states

> The gaps between schools are too large. Large children's groups, staff shortages and stress are to be met with a law on smaller groups, more state funding according to need and a stop to profit-seeking. School choice is to remain, but tax money is to go to teaching, support and pupil health.

System: An equivalent school with smaller groups

Scenario: Groups are reduced and the school voucher stays in the service
Given the gaps between schools are too large
And large children's groups, staff shortages and stress worsen conditions
When smaller children's groups are legislated and profit-seeking in school is stopped
Then preschool and after-school care get more adults according to the National Agency for Education's benchmarks
And the state takes greater responsibility for equivalent funding according to need
And limited companies in school are stopped while it is still possible to choose a school
And pupil health and special-educational support are to exist in every school
Output 15 to 30 hours of general preschool a week
And 20 hours a week as a first step
Outcome pupils' chances are not to be decided by postcode or the municipality's finances
And grades in the middle years are rejected as a tool for catching needs
