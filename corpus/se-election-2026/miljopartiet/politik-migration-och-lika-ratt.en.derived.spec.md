# Our understanding

> Resettlement is a counted intake. Asylum stays open as a right. The harder edge is who already here cannot be removed: settled young people, workers who switch track, and holders of permanent permits that cannot be pulled back.

System: Counted quota and a harder right to remain

Scenario: Settled young people are taken off the removal list
When particularly distressing circumstances return and young people can stay as family members into early adulthood
Then people who grew up in the country are meant to keep their permits
Outcome removal at majority is closed for that group
But the Migration Agency still has to decide each case, and no extra caseworker count is given

Scenario: Permanent status becomes the default and cannot be withdrawn later
When permanent residence is the main rule and cannot be revoked after the fact
Then status no longer expires into a temporary cycle
Outcome people can plan work, language and family life on a standing permit
But a later security or fraud finding has no withdrawal path the policy names
