# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka taea e te kōrero Matike Mai te whakatipu kōwhiringa. Ehara i te tuhi anō i te pukapuka ture. Me tū tonu te ture pōti mō te pōti i te 16 me te herehere. Me tū tonu tētahi taupane tāra mō te whakahou koha i mua i te taea o ngā rōpū me ngā kaihomai te whakamahere.

System: He kōrero me te tika pōti kāore he kaupapa ture kua oti, he rahi pūtea rānei

Scenario: Ka tīmata te kōrero kaupapa ture ā-motu
When ka whakangaotia tētahi kōrero ā-motu mō te panoni kaupapa ture mā Matike Mai Aotearoa
Then ka kōrero ngā hapori mō He Whakaputanga me Te Tiriti hei tuhinga tīmatanga
Outcome ka taea te whakatipu māramatanga me ngā kōwhiringa mā te kōrero e ārahi ana
But kāore te pukapuka ture e huri kia huri rā anō ngā pōti o muri i te kōrero hei ture

Scenario: Ka whānui te tika pōti ki te 16 me te hunga i te herehere
When ka whakawhānuitia te tau pōti ki te 16
And ka toro te tika pōti ki te hunga katoa i te herehere
And ka whakahokia te rēhita tae noa ki te rā pōti
Then ka nui ake pea te hunga e rēhita, e pōti
Outcome ka whānui te tika pōti i ēnei ahunga e toru
But me paahi tonu te ture pōti mō ia panoni i mua i te whakamahi a te pōtitanga e tū mai ana

Scenario: Ka haere te whakahou koha kāore he taupane i tāia
When ka herea ngā koha ki ngā kaipōti kua rēhitatia
And ka nui ake te pūtea tūmatanui mō ngā kōkiri
And ka herea ngā koha a tētahi kaihomai ki tētahi taumata kāore e mōrearea te awe o te kaihomai
Then me whakaheke ngā huarahi kamupene me ngā tarahiti e whāia ana e te kaupapa here
Outcome me whakaheke ngā huarahi koha kamupene me ngā tarahiti e whāia ana e te kaupapa here
But kāore anō ngā rōpū me ngā kaihomai kia mōhio ki te taupane, nā reira kāore e taea te whakamahere ki tērā
