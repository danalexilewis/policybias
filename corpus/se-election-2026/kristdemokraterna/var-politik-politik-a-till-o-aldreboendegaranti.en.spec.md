# What the page states

> A care-home guarantee is to give people over 85 a statutory right to move into sheltered or residential care when they themselves judge that the need is there. No one should have to wait until their health has deteriorated sharply or go through extensive needs assessments. The aim is stronger self-determination, less loneliness and a more secure old age. More care-home places are needed, and municipalities are to stimulate sheltered housing and make it easier for those who want to live at home.
