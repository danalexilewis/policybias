# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tū motuhake te kaupapa here aorere mō te rongomau, te tika me ngā tika tangata, ehara i te whakakaha i ngā hononga hōia ki te United States. Ka arotahi te waonga ki te urupare āhuarangi, te mahi atawhai me te aroturuki taiao, me te whakakore i AUKUS me te wehe i Five Eyes. Ka whakamanahia a Palestine, ka whainatia a Israel, ka tautokona ngā kaupapa wetewete kōrāhia, ā, mā te Trade For All e uruhi Te Tiriti, ngā tika kaimahi, ngā tika tangata me ngā here āhuarangi i te hokohoko. Mā te takawaenga āhuarangi e whāia ngā tiaki patent ngoikore ake mō ngā hangarau kākāriki me ngā whakatikatika raina āhuarangi. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Ngā take aorere motuhake me te hokohoko

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere take ao me te hokohoko
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere take ao me te hokohoko
Then ka tū he kaupapa here aorere motuhake ki te tūkino tika tangata, ā, ka tautoko i te rongomau me te tika
And ka arotahi te kaupapa here waonga ki ngā urupare āhuarangi, te urupare atawhai me te aroturuki taiao
And ka wehe ngā pou tarāwaho hōia me te haumarutanga i te United States, tae atu ki te whakakore i AUKUS me te wehe i Five Eyes
And ka tautokona te mana motuhake me te rangatiratanga o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa, ā, ka ātetehia te hōia o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
And ka whakamanahia a Palestine hei whenua, ā, ka whainatia a Israel
And ka tautokona ngā kaupapa wetewete kōrāhia me te manapori huri noa i te ao, inā ko ngā iwi taketake o te rohe Āhia–Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
And mā te Trade For All e uruhi Te Tiriti, ngā tika kaimahi, ngā tika tangata me ngā here āhuarangi i ngā whakaaetanga hokohoko
And ka ahu whakamua ngā kaupapa āhuarangi mā ngā hinonga ā-ao, tae atu ki te whakaheke i ngā tiaki patent mō ngā hangarau kākāriki me ngā whakatikatika raina āhuarangi
Outcome ehara a Aotearoa i te mema o AUKUS, o Five Eyes rānei
