# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka neke te hauora āniwaniwa ki ngā ratonga e ū ana ki te ahurea, me te nui ake o te pūtea mō ngā rōpū e ārahitia ana e te Māori me te Pasifika. Ko te taurima whakamana ira kore utu i runga i te whakaae māramahia, tae atu ki te whakahoki i te aukati puberty blocker. Ka whakamāramatia e te ture tika tangata ngā pūtake whakatoihara, ā, ka aukatia ngā pokanga intersex kāore he whakaae. Ka whakahouhia te ture heke mai kia taea te whakamana me te hononga anō o te whānau mō ngā kaikimi piki, rerenga me ngā manene āniwaniwa. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Ngā tika o ngā hapori āniwaniwa me te urunga ki te hauora

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere hapori āniwaniwa
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere hapori āniwaniwa
Then ka whakawhanakehia ngā ratonga me ngā rautaki taurima hauora e tika ana mō te āniwaniwa, me ngā tauira ā-ahurea, ā-katoa
And ka piki te pūtea mō ngā rōpū āniwaniwa, tae atu ki ngā rōpū e ārahitia ana e te Māori me te Pasifika
And ka homai te taurima hauora whakamana ira kore utu, kore whakatoihara i runga i te whakaae māramahia, tae atu ki te whakahoki i te aukati puberty blocker
And ka whakamāramatia e te Human Rights Act ngā pūtake whakatoihara mō te aronga tāne-wahine, te tuakiri ira me ngā āhuatanga ira, ā, ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga o te pūrongo Ia Tangata
And ka aukatia ngā pokanga kāore he whakaae, kāore e tika ā-rongoā ki ngā tāngata intersex
And ka taea e ngā ture me ngā kaupapa here te whakamana ira ā-ture, te hononga hoa, te hononga anō o te whānau me te noho anō mō ngā kaikimi piki, rerenga me ngā manene āniwaniwa
Outcome ka taea e ngā hapori takatāpui me ngā hapori āniwaniwa te uru ki te taurima me te whakamana ā-ture
