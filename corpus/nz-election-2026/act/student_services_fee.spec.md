# What the page states

> The up to $1,253 compulsory fee, more than $4,000 over a three-year degree, taxpayer subsidy through student loans, menu-based voluntary selection, and user-pays for unchosen services are all on the source page. No regulatory citation or rollout date is given.

System: Voluntary Student Services Fees at universities

Scenario: A domestic university student selects student services each year
Given every domestic university student currently pays a compulsory Student Services Fee of up to $1,253 a year on top of tuition
And across a three-year degree this can add more than $4,000 to a student's loan
And student loans are subsidised by the taxpayer
When the regulations are amended so universities must offer student services as a menu
Then students select the services they want and pay only for those, from fitness facilities to students unions and student media
Output services not chosen still available on a transparent user-pays basis
And up to around $4,000 saved on a student loan over a three-year degree
Outcome students pay only for services they choose
And the taxpayer saves money because student debt is subsidised
And students are not forced to fund unions, advocacy or student media they do not support
