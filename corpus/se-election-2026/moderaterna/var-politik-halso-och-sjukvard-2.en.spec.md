# What the page states

> Care should be accessible, safe and coordinated so that the right care arrives in time. The care guarantee should be tightened and regions should become liable when it is not met. A national care brokerage should make it easier to receive care in another region. Mental ill health, involuntary childlessness and women's diseases should be prioritised.

System: Tighter care guarantee and national care brokerage

Scenario: A patient waits longer than the care guarantee
Given too many patients do not receive their treatment within the care guarantee's 90-day limit
And waiting times in child and adolescent psychiatry are far too long
And a household with a police officer and a nurse pays around 130,000 kronor in regional tax each year
When the care guarantee is tightened and regions become liable when it is not met
Then it should become easier to receive care in another region through a national care brokerage
And a statutory care guarantee should apply to appointment time and treatment in child and adolescent psychiatry
And first-line psychiatry should be strengthened and relieve pressure on child and adolescent psychiatry
And the number of subsidised IVF attempts should be doubled and sibling attempts included
And the age limit for child and adolescent psychiatry should be raised and young people with severe mental illness should receive a fixed care contact
Output 21 years as the new age limit for child and adolescent psychiatry instead of 18
Outcome more people should receive care in time without regional tax being raised
