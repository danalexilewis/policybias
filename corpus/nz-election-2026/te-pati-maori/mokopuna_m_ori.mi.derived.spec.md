# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Mā te whakamutu i te pīpanga tango tamariki ka whāiti ake ko wai ka tango i ngā mokopuna, ā, ko te tiaki nā te whānau e ārahi ana te mea e pupuri ai ngā tamariki ki ō rātou tāngata—engari kāore i āta whakamāramatia te huringa ture ki ngā mana tango, ā, kāore he pūtea, he hoahoa rānei mō te kaha tiaki. Ko te haepapa o te kāwanatanga mō te hara o ngā tango tamariki tērā pea he whakapāha, he utu, he uiui, he haepapa hara rānei; kia kōwhiria rā anō tētahi, kāore e taea e ngā whānau kua tūkino te whai muru.

System: Te tiaki a te whānau, kāore he ture haepapa kua whakaingoatia

Scenario: Ka whakamutua te pīpanga tango tamariki
Given ngā tamariki e kīia ana he whakapapa, me tupu tahi me ō rātou tāngata
And kāore he huringa kua whakaingoatia ki ngā mana tango
When ka whakamutua te pīpanga e tango ana i ngā tamariki Māori
Then me whāiti, me whakakapi, me whakakore rānei ngā pūtake ā-ture mō te tango tamariki
Outcome ka iti iho ngā mokopuna ka wehe i ō rātou tāngata mehemea ka huri te ture
But kia whakaingoatia rā anō te huringa mana, he whakaaro noa te mutunga o te pīpanga

Scenario: Ka whakahokia te mana whānau me te tiaki nā te whānau e ārahi ana
Given he haumi ki te tiaki nā te whānau e ārahi ana hei whakakapinga
And kāore he taumata pūtea, he hoahoa hōtaka rānei
When kua tata te tamaiti kia tangohia
Then me whai kaha, tautoko, huarahi whakatau hoki te tiaki nā te whānau e ārahi ana
Outcome ka taea e ngā mokopuna te noho honohono mehemea kei reira tērā kaha
But kāore ngā whānau e mōhio e hia ngā mokopuna ka taea e te pūnaha tiaki te pupuri

Scenario: Ka whakahaepapatia te kāwanatanga mō te hara o ngā tango tamariki
Given he whakaaro kia haepapa te kāwanatanga mō te hara o ngā tango tamariki me ngā whakanoho
And kāore he whakapāha, he utu, he taraipiunara, he taputapu haepapa kē rānei
When ka whakapaehia he hara
Then me kōwhiri anō te āhua o te haepapa
Outcome ko te haepapa te kawenga kua whakaingoatia
But kāore e taea e ngā whānau kua tūkino te whai muru, ā, kāore e taea e Te Tai Ōhanga te ine i te mōrearea o te kāwanatanga
