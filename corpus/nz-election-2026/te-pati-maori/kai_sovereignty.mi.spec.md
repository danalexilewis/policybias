# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā te Māori Kai Sovereignty Board e tirotiro te tika kai. Ka tiakina ngā pūnaha kai Māori, ā, ka whakangaohia ngā pūnaha kai ā-hapori, tae atu ki ngā māra kai. Ka whakahekea ngā utu hokomaha mā te tango i te tāke hokohoko (GST) i te kai me te waeture i ngā pikinga utu hokomaha, ā, ka tautokona te rangatahi ki ngā ahumahi kai me ngā ahumahi tuatahi. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te mana motuhake kai me te tirotiro a te poari, me te whakaheke utu hokomaha

Scenario: Mā te Māori Kai Sovereignty Board e tirotiro te tika kai
Given he nui rawa te kai e whakaputaina ana e Aotearoa, engari ka noho te whānau i te kore
When ka whakatūria he Māori Kai Sovereignty Board hei tirotiro i te tika kai
Then ka tiakina ngā pūnaha kai Māori, tae atu ki te mana kākano, te whakahē i ngā kai GE, me te hāpai i a WAI262
And ka whakangaohia ngā pūnaha kai ā-hapori, tae atu ki ngā māra kai me ngā pāmu kai
And ka tautokona te rangatahi ki ngā ahumahi kai me ngā ahumahi tuatahi
And ka whakangaohia te ahuwhenua Māori whakaora
And ka whatiia te mana kaporeihana mā te tautoko i ngā hokomaha nā te Māori, me te tiaki i ngā kaihoko kai ā-rohe
And ka whakahekea ngā utu hokomaha, tae atu ki te tango i te tāke hokohoko (GST) i te kai me te waeture i ngā pikinga utu hokomaha
And ka whakapikia te urunga ki te whenua hei whakatipu kai
Outcome he utu taea, he hauora te kai, ā, e hono ana ki te whenua me te whakapapa

Scenario: Ka tiakina ngā pūnaha kai Māori
Given he nui te kai e whakatipuria ana e Aotearoa, engari e hiakai tonu ana te whānau
When ka tiakina ngā pūnaha kai Māori, tae atu ki te mana kākano, te whakahē i ngā kai GE, me te hāpai i a WAI262
Then ka whakangaohia ngā pūnaha kai ā-hapori me te ahuwhenua Māori whakaora
And ka whatiia te mana kaporeihana mā te tautoko i ngā hokomaha nā te Māori, me te tiaki i ngā kaihoko kai ā-rohe
And ka whakapikia te urunga ki te whenua hei whakatipu kai
Outcome he kai hauora, utu taea, e hono ana ki te whenua, te whakapapa, me te mana Māori
