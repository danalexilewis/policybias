# What the page states

> Democracy would represent people rather than wealthy corporate interests. A national conversation on constitutional transformation would use Matike Mai Aotearoa, voting rights would extend to 16-year-olds and people in custody, and Māori seats would be entrenched with longer roll-switch windows. Donations would be capped for registered voters with more public campaign financing, and lobbying would face a register and code of conduct. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Democratic representation and political finance reform

Scenario: The democracy plan is put into effect
When the government puts the democracy plan into effect
Then a national conversation for constitutional transformation upholds He Whakaputanga and Te Tiriti using Matike Mai Aotearoa
And the voting age is extended to 16 with voting rights for all people in custody and enrolment restored up to election day
And the Māori electoral seats are entrenched and Māori roll switch time is extended
And donations are limited to registered voters with increased public campaign financing and capped donor influence
And a lobbyist register and code of conduct strengthen lobbying transparency
Outcome Te Tiriti and genuine partnership are upheld in decision-making
