# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā te pūnaha hiko e whakamua te oranga tūmatanui i mua i te hua kamupene mā te ture hou me tētahi tari hanganga pūngao whakahou. Ka horahia te rāwhiti tohatoha me ngā pūhiko ki ngā kāinga, ngā marae me ngā whare tūmatanui, me te whakamahere pūngao e ārahi ana te hapori. Ka rāhuitia te tango wahie para hou, ka mutua ngā tautoko wahie para, ka whakakorea te tauranga LNG, ā, mā tētahi kaituku waeture pūngao kotahi me te Ministry for Energy e tirotiro ngā anga mana Māori me te mana hapori e tū ana i Te Tiriti. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te whakahou pūnaha pūngao whakahou e utu ngāwari ana

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere pūngao

And ka whakarite tētahi tari hanganga pūngao whakahou i te haumaru tuku me ngā utu iti ake
And ka taea te rāwhiti tohatoha me ngā pūhiko mō ngā kāinga, ngā marae me ngā whare tūmatanui, me te whakamahere pūngao e ārahi ana te hapori
And ka rāhuitia te tango wahie para hou, ka mutua ngā tautoko wahie para, ā, ka whakakorea te tauranga LNG

And ka whakaheke waro te ahumahi, ā, ka ākina te urupare hiahia me te pai o te pūngao
Outcome he pūngao whakahou utu ngāwari, haumaru, mā hoki tā ngā hapori
