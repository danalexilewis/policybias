# What the page states

> Health and medical care is underfunded. Queues, staff shortages and health gaps are to be met with long-term funding, stronger primary care and a working-environment lift. Mental ill-health is to be met earlier. Dental care is to become more equal. Public money is to go to care, not profits.

System: Equal care with shorter queues

Scenario: Care gets long-term money and staff can bear to stay
Given Swedish health and medical care is underfunded
And queues, staff shortages and large health gaps
And the staff shortage is one of the most important causes of care queues
When long-term funding and a working-environment lift are carried through
Then more people want to stay in care and more care places can be opened
And primary care is strengthened as the hub with a named care contact
And mental ill-health is met earlier without a requirement for a diagnosis
And public money goes to care according to need, not profits or fast-track queues
Output 23 years of age for free dental care
Outcome care is steered more by need than by the wallet
And health gaps and unequal care are to shrink
