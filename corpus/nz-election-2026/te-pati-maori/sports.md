### What we’ll do

*   Invest in sport and community initiatives that build belonging, wellbeing, and opportunity.
*   Support pathways for rangatahi through local clubs, programmes, and facilities.

### Why it matters

Sport is more than a game. It builds belonging, discipline, leadership, confidence, and connection. Strong clubs and community pathways give our rangatahi places to stand, people to stand with, and futures to run toward.
