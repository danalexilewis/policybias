# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka arotahi te haumi ki ngā waka, ngā hokohoko ki tāwāhi, te pūngao, me ngā ratonga o mua o Te Waipounamu. Kei ngā hononga rori me te rerewē te pokapū momo-maha o Rolleston me te whā-ara o Queenstown, me ngā moenga hōhipera me ngā ruma ako ka tāpirihia. Ka ōrite te kaupapa here pūngao i te hiahia ahumahi hou ki te whakaputa hou, tae atu ki te tuku ū mō ngā pokapū raraunga.

System: Mahere tipu Te Waipounamu

Scenario: Ka haumitia ngā hanganga me ngā hokohoko ki tāwāhi o Te Waipounamu
Given e tata ana ki te $100 billion ā-tau te ōhanga o Te Waipounamu
And e tata ana te 22 ōrau o te ōhanga ā-motu te ōhanga o Te Waipounamu
And $18.1 billion te whakapaunga a ngā manuhiri tāwāhi i te tau ki te March 2025
And e tautoko ana te tāpoi i tētahi o ia iwa mahi i Aotearoa
When ka haere te mahere tipu o Te Waipounamu
Then ka haere ngā kaupapa rori, rerewē, hōhipera, me ngā ruma ako
Output $50 million mō ngā hononga o te pokapū utanga momo-maha o Rolleston
And $25 million ki te whā-ara i Queenstown i tua atu i Hansen Road ki te porowhita o Five Mile
And 96 ngā moenga tāpiri i Christchurch Hospital Tower Three
And e tata ana ki te 100 ngā ruma ako anō puta noa i Te Waipounamu
Outcome e kawe ana ngā tauranga o Te Waipounamu i te tata ki te hautoru o ngā hokohoko taonga ki tāwāhi a Aotearoa
