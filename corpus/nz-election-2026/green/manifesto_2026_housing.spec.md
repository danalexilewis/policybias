# What the page states

> Warm, affordable housing would be treated as a basic right rather than an investment asset. Tens of thousands of new public homes and more papakāinga on whenua Māori would be built, annual rent increases would be capped, and new tenancy rents would link to what previous tenants paid. Healthy Homes Standards would strengthen under an independently certified Rental Warrant of Fitness, Housing First and supported housing would expand with same-day homelessness placement, and landlord tax benefits would reverse.

System: Warm, affordable housing for all

Scenario: The housing plan is put into effect
When the government puts the housing plan into effect
Then tens of thousands of new public homes are built with more accessible public housing and papakāinga on whenua Māori
And new tenancy rents are linked to what previous tenants paid
And Healthy Homes Standards are strengthened and enforced with an independently certified Rental Warrant of Fitness
And Housing First, transitional and supported housing expand with same-day accommodation for people experiencing homelessness
And councils collaborate with mana whenua on walkable mixed-use areas and affordable infill
And generous tax benefits for landlords and property speculators are reversed
Output annual rent increases limited to no more than 2%
Outcome housing is treated as a basic right rather than an investment asset
