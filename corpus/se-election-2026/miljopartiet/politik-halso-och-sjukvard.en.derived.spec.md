# Our understanding

> Queues are treated as a staffing problem. Grants and a workplace lift are meant to keep people in the job so beds can reopen. Dental care is pulled toward the same needs rule as the rest of medicine. Regions still have to find the staff, and adults still face an unpriced dental shield.

System: Staff hours as the queue instrument

Scenario: A workplace lift is meant to reopen beds
When hours are cut, sick-day wait is removed and specialist training is paid
Then more people are expected to stay and more beds to open
Outcome queues fall if the extra labour actually arrives
But roster cover and training time must be paid from grants the policy does not size

Scenario: Public money is fenced off from profit and fast-track insurance
When surplus must be reinvested and private insurance cannot buy a jump in public care
Then publicly funded slots are meant to follow need
Outcome a private fast track loses its public foothold
But privately insured patients lose that exit and wait in the same queue
