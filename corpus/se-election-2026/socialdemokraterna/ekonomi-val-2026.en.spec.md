# What the page states

> When the economy grows it is to show in the wallet through raised child and study allowances, cheaper medicines, free dental care for the young and protection against high electricity prices.
> The qualifying day is to go for those who cannot work from home, because both pay and pension are hit.
> A jobs ladder to self-support, job contracts for the young and an activity duty are to press down unemployment. The election is on 13 September.
