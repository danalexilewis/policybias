# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka utu te $1 piriona i tētahi rangahau i raro i te whenua puta noa i te motu e tuku ana i te raraunga me tētahi anga, ehara i te tuku hokohoko kua whakamanatia. Ko ngā tūponotanga kitenga nui rawa atu i te iwa tekau ōrau me tētahi huarahi e ono tau ki te rangatiratanga ā-whenua e kīia ana kāore he tikanga kua inehia, he ture kua whakamanahia rānei. Ka whai ngā raihana nui rawa atu i te 50% i te wā ka pakeke te whakaputa, roa i muri i te whakapaunga rangahau.

System: Te whakapaunga rangahau hei raraunga i mua i te tuku

Scenario: Ka utua te rangahau puta noa i te motu
Given kāore anō ngā pākawa o waho o Aotearoa kia āta rangahaua ki ngā taputapu o nāianei
When ka utua tētahi rangahau whakawhanake i raro i te whenua puta noa i te motu i te wā o te kāwanatanga
Then ka hangaia tētahi huinga raraunga motu kotahi me tētahi anga whakawhanake
Output $1 billion invested over the term of government into a nationwide subsurface development survey
Outcome e tūmanakohia ana ngā hua tuatahi i roto i ngā marama tekau mā rua, ā, ngā pākawa kua tohua rawa i roto i ngā marama e rua tekau mā whā
But ka hoko te whakapaunga i te raraunga me tētahi anga, ehara i te tuku hokohoko kua whakamanatia

Scenario: Ka kitea ngā tūponotanga kitenga hei tūponotanga kua inehia
Given e kī ana te kaupapa here ko ngā tūponotanga o tētahi kitenga hokohoko nui nui rawa atu i te iwa tekau ōrau
When ka pānuitia tērā nama hei tūponotanga
Then ka noho he kerēme kāore he tikanga kua whakapuakina
Outcome e tautohetia ana tonu te rangahau he mea tika kia mahia
But ka utu ngā kaiputu tāke i te rangahau i runga i ngā tūponotanga kāore e taea te whakamana

Scenario: Ka pakeke te whakaputa i muri i tētahi kitenga
Given tētahi raihana nui rawa atu i te 50% ki ngā tango i te wā ka pakeke te whakaputa
And tētahi huarahi pea i roto i ngā tau e ono ki te pupuri me te whakahaere i te tuku pūngao ā-whenua
When ka whakamana ngā kaipōkai i ngā papa, ā, ka tīmata te whakaputa
Then ka piki te wāhi raihana a te Karauna i te wā ka pakeke te whakaputa
Output a royalty well above 50% on extractions as production matures
Outcome ko te rangatiratanga ā-whenua te ūnga kua whakaingoatia
But me whakamana tonu te Pāremata i te huarahi i mua i te tūturu o te rangatiratanga a te Karauna
