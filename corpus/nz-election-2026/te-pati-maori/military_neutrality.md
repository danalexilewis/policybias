### **What we’ll do**

*   Declare Aotearoa a **militarily neutral nation**.
*   Reset foreign policy to be **independent**, grounded in Māori values, and centred on Pacific and Indigenous relationships.
*   Review and withdraw from military alignments that undermine sovereignty.
*   Refocus the Defence Force on protection, disaster response, and Pacific support.
*   Uphold international human rights and hold war criminals to account.
