# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka tiakina e te kaupapa here te 30% neke atu o te rohe moana, ka aukati i ngā tikanga hī ika whakakino me te keri takere moana, ā, ka arotake i te Quota Management System. Kāore e tiakina te uara quota ina kati ngā wai.

System: Ngā wai kua kati me te urunga quota kua ngaro

Scenario: Ka tiakina te rohe moana, ka mutu te hī ika i roto
When ka whakahouhia te ture wāhi rāhui moana ki te whāinga tiaki moana
Then ka mutu te hī ika, ka tino herea rānei, i ngā wai kua tiakina
Output at least 30% of New Zealand's ocean territory protected
Outcome ko te oranga moana me ngā taputapu e ārahi ana te tikanga ngā whāinga kua whakaingoatia
But ka ngaro i ngā kaipupuri quota i hī i aua wai te urunga kua whakamanatia i reira

Scenario: Ka aukatia ngā tikanga whakakino
When ka aukatia te kūpenetanga i raro i te moana ki ngā maunga moana, me te kupenga here me te keri
Then kāore e taea e ngā waka i whakawhirinaki ki aua tikanga te hī ika i te ara ōrite
Outcome he iti ake te taputapu whakakino e pā ana ki ngā maunga moana me ngā nohoanga takutai
But ka ngaro i ngā kaiwhakahaere me te quota e here ana ki aua tikanga te urunga, me whakahou rānei ngā taputapu i tā rātou ake utu

Scenario: Ka neke te Quota Management System ki te whakahaere rauropi
When ka arotakehia te Quota Management System ki te whakahaere hī ika i runga i te pūnaha rauropi
Then ka taea te tapahi, te nuku, te whakahou rānei i te hopu kua whakamanatia ahakoa ka noho ngā whakataunga ki te anga
Outcome ka arotahi te whakahaere ki te oranga rauropi, kaua ki te tango momo kotahi anake
But ka tango ngā kaipupuri quota whakataunga me ētahi atu i te ngaronga moni hopu, kāore he rārangi utu
