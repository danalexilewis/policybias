# Our understanding

> Takatāpui wellbeing, safety and dignity are named aims, and public services are to be inclusive, accessible and accountable—but no law, standard, complaint path or sanction binds agencies yet. Without those instruments, inclusion stays a preference rather than a requirement someone can enforce.

System: Wellbeing without the enforcement instrument

Scenario: Wellbeing, safety and dignity are to be protected
Given takatāpui Māori deserve safety, dignity, joy and full belonging
When the wellbeing, safety and dignity of takatāpui Māori are protected
Then a legal or programme instrument must still be chosen
Outcome living fully without discrimination is the aim
But without that instrument, agencies are not bound to a new duty

Scenario: Public services are to be inclusive and accountable
When public services are made inclusive, accessible and accountable
Then standards and an accountability path must still be named
Outcome services are meant to stop pushing people to the margins
But until those rules exist, inclusion stays a preference, not a requirement
