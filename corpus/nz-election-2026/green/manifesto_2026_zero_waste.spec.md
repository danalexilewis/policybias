# What the page states

> The source page names plan items on stewardship, levies, waste-to-energy, targets, soil diversion and community recovery. Levy increase is described as matching international best practice without a rate. No timetable is given.

System: Zero waste and circular economy

Scenario: The zero waste plan is put into effect
When the government puts the zero waste plan into effect
Then mandatory product stewardship, a beverage container return scheme, product phase-outs and right-to-repair are established
And waste disposal levy revenue funds waste minimisation with the levy increased to international best practice and a consistent rate across landfill classes
And a moratorium applies to waste-to-energy plants with biogas from organic waste explored
And binding measurable waste reduction targets are set with Te Tiriti in the Waste Minimisation Act and duty-of-care for all waste streams
And discarded soil is diverted from landfills where safe
And local, mana whenua and community-driven resource recovery and organic waste processing are prioritised
Outcome producers take responsibility for products through their lifecycle
