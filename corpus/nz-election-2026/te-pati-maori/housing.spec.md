> More public and affordable homes would be built and supported. It would be easier to build homes on Māori and ancestral land. Speculation and land-banking would be stopped, including action on vacant properties.

System: Housing for whānau on Māori land

Scenario: More public and affordable homes are built
Given whānau face waiting lists, overcrowding and speculation instead of secure homes
When more public and affordable homes are built and supported
Then it is easier to build homes on Māori and ancestral land
And speculation and land-banking are stopped including action on vacant properties
Outcome whānau have warm, safe, secure homes as the ground for health, education and stability
