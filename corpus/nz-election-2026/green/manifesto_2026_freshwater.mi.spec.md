# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakaorangia te hauora wai māori mā ngā tepenga ā-motu kaha, tae atu ki te tepenga nitrate iti ake mō te wai inu, me te whakahoki i Te Mana o Te Wai. Ka mutu ngā takuhe mō ngā kaupapa irikuhanga me te pāpuni nui hōu, ka kaha ake ngā ture tuku para wai kia kauhoe ai ngā awa me ngā takutai, ā, ka pūteatia te kaiwhakahaere wai inu mō te aroturuki me te uruhi. Ka piki te pūtea manahau waipuke, ka whakakorea āta te wairākau hauota hanga, ā, ka heke ngā pāpātanga kararehe mā te riu. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te hauora wai māori me te haumaru wai inu

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere wai māori
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere wai māori
Then ka whakatinanahia ngā tepenga ā-motu kaha mō te wai māori, tae atu ki te whakaheke i te tepenga nitrate mō te wai inu
And ka whakahokia Te Mana o Te Wai kia whakaarotau te hauora wai māori, kia whakatinana hoki i Te Tiriti o Waitangi
And ka mutu ngā takuhe me te pūtea tūmatanui mō ngā kaupapa irikuhanga me te pāpuni nui hōu, ā, ka arotakehia ngā whakaaetanga ki ngā wāhi kua heke rawa te taumata wai
And ka whakakahangia ngā ture tuku para wai kia kauhoe ai ngā roto, ngā awa, ngā takutai me ngā takutai moana
And ka pūteatia te kaiwhakahaere wai inu mō te aroturuki, te pūrongo me te uruhi
And ka piki te pūtea manahau waipuke, me te wāhi mō ngā awa me ngā pūnaha repo māori
And ka whakakorea āta te wairākau hauota hanga, ā, ka whakahekehia ngā pāpātanga kararehe mā te riu
