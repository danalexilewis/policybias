# Our understanding

> Continuity is the test: same worker, more time, no stopwatch. That is a staffing-density problem. Profit recovery and an ombudsman sit downstream of the schedule actually being filled.

System: Continuity as a staffing-density problem

Scenario: Minute control ends
When visit time is no longer sliced per minute and staff may use judgement
Then visits can last as long as the work requires
Outcome older people should meet fewer new faces
But without a published staff-per-home number the extra minutes come from the existing team or from overtime

Scenario: An ombudsman guards rights after harm
When a national elderly ombudsman is appointed
Then neglect, violence and abuse have a named watchdog
Outcome families have a rights route outside the municipality
But the ombudsman does not staff the evening shift
