# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka kitea te mātauranga hei painga tūmatanui e tū ana i Te Tiriti o Waitangi me ngā tika tangata o te ao. Ka utu-kore te mātauranga kōhungahunga, ka whakamutua ngā pokapū e whai hua ana, ka tautuhia anō te marau ki te ahunga taketake o Te Mātaiaho, ā, ka whakawhānuihia te tautoko ako mā tētahi tari whakarite kotahi. Ka tupu a Ka Ora, Ka Ako ki ngā tina kura mō te katoa, mā te Māori Education Authority e whakahaere te mātauranga reo Māori, ā, ka waihangatia ngātahi ngā utu tika me ngā taumata kaimahi me te rāngai. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Mātauranga tūmatanui e whakauru ana

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere mātauranga
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere mātauranga
Then ka utu-kore te mātauranga kōhungahunga, ā, ka tukuna hei painga tūmatanui, ka whakamutua ngā pokapū e whai hua ana
And ka tautuhia anō ngā whakahou marau me te aromatawai ki te ahunga taketake o Te Mātaiaho
And ka whakawhānuihia ngā paearu tautoko ako me tētahi tari whakarite kotahi, ā, ka whakatinanahia ngā inenga Framework for Action katoa
And ka whakawhānuihia a Ka Ora, Ka Ako ki tētahi tina kura mahana, kai pai, hangaia ā-rohe mō te katoa
And mā te Māori Education Authority e whakahaere, e pūtea hoki te mātauranga reo Māori me te mātauranga kaupapa Māori
And ka waihangatia ngātahi ngā utu tika, ngā taumata kaimahi me tētahi mahere ohumahi roa me te rāngai
Outcome kei te pūtake o te pūnaha te tino rangatiratanga, te reo Māori me te mātauranga Māori
