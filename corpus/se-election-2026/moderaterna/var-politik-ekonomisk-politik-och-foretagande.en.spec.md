# What the page states

> Sweden is to have the EU's highest living standard within ten years. Those who work are to keep more of their pay. Firms are to be able to grow without unnecessary hassle. Work should always pay better than benefits.

System: Lower tax on work and simpler business

Scenario: A household in ordinary occupations passes the threshold
Given Sweden has the lowest tax take in 50 years but still belongs among the ten countries with the highest tax take in the OECD
And 1.3 million people are not self-supporting
And those who work shifts or take extra hours can pay more than 50 percent tax on every hundred kronor above the national income threshold
And an ordinary family has had 5 000 kronor more left since 2022
When tax on work, food and electricity is cut
Then more people in ordinary occupations are to keep at least half of earned pay
Output 1 800 kronor more in the wallet each month for an ordinary family
And 3 100 kronor more a month in 2026 compared with 2022 for a single care assistant with two children
Outcome work is to pay better than benefits

Scenario: A firm wants to grow and hire
Given exports amount to almost 52 percent of GDP
And 1.4 million jobs are tied to exports
When corporation tax and employers' social contributions are cut
Then the Simplification Council and the Implementation Council are to reduce red tape and gold-plating of EU rules
And receipt hassle and food requirements have been abolished
And new 3:12 rules reduce the administrative burden by a third
And the Environmental Code is to become more flexible and a central environmental permitting authority is to take over permits from county administrative boards and the land and environment courts
Outcome more firms are to be able to grow and hire
