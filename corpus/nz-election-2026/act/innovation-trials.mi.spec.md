# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka taea e ngā ture tawhito te aukati i ngā waka motuhake, ngā drone ahuwhenua me te hangarau hauora mō ngā tau, i a ētahi whenua e neke whakamua ana. Ka whakaputa te kaupapa here i tētahi tukanga Whakamātautau Auaha tūmau hei whakatārewa i tētahi waeture motuhake mō tētahi whakamātautau iti i tētahi wāhi, me ngā herenga haumaru me ngā hua ka inehia. Kāore he tepenga roa, he paearu whakaae rānei i homai.

System: Ngā Whakamātautau Auaha mō ngā hangarau e aukatihia ana e ngā ture tawhito

Scenario: Ka tono tētahi kamupene ki te whakahaere Whakamātautau Auaha i Aotearoa
Given ka taea e ngā drone ahuwhenua nui ake i te 25 kilograms te utu tae atu ki te $2,000 mō te whakamana, ā, nui ake i te tau te roa i Aotearoa
And ka taea e ngā kaipāmu o Ahitereiria te whakamahi drone tae atu ki te 150 kilograms i raro i tētahi raihana māmā
And kua whakatakotoria e ngā āpiha o Aotearoa te take ki tētahi hōtaka mahi e rua tau te roa
When ka whakaputaina tētahi tukanga tono tūmau mō ngā Whakamātautau Auaha
Then ka taea te whakatārewa i tētahi waeture motuhake mō tētahi whakamātautau iti i tētahi wāhi
Output ngā herenga haumaru me tētahi ara ine i te hua i mua i te tīmatanga o tētahi whakamātautau
And ka āwhina Te Manatū Waeture i ngā tari ki te tautuhi i ngā mana whakawātea o nāianei me te whakahaere whakamātautau ōrite
Outcome ka taea e tētahi kamupene te tono mā tētahi tukanga tūmatanui kotahi, kaua ki te kimi i ngā āpiha tika
And ka whakamutua te whakamātautau e ngā hua kino, ā, ka homai e ngā hua pai he taunakitanga hei huri i te ture
And ka pōwhiritia ngā kamupene e mahi ana i ngā waka motuhake, ngā drone ahuwhenua me te hangarau hauora ki te whakahaere whakamātautau i Aotearoa
