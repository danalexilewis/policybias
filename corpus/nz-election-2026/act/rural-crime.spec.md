# What the page states

> Rural crime is rising and many farmers no longer report incidents because prosecution is rare. The policy funds additional rural patrol hours, establishes a dedicated rural crime unit, extends Text 111 to areas with unreliable mobile coverage, and strengthens penalties for poaching and rustling. No budget or penalty detail is given.

System: Rural crime crackdown

Scenario: Police and rural communities respond to growing rural crime
Given Federated Farmers' latest Rural Crime Survey found 67% of farmers experienced crime, up from 41% in 2016
And nearly half of farmers do not bother reporting incidents to police
And of those who do report, only 15% see the offender investigated and prosecuted
When Police are funded to hire more officers for rural areas
Then a target of an additional 100,000 extra patrol hours each year is set
Output a dedicated rural crime unit established
And eligibility for Text 111 services extended to people in rural areas with unreliable mobile coverage
Outcome increased penalties and strengthened offences for armed poachers and livestock rustling
And strengthened tools used between agencies for rural enforcement coordination
And rural communities gain more police presence, connectivity and harsher deterrents
