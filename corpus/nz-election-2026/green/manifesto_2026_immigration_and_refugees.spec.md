# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover residence pathways, health requirements, employer switching, family reunification, Te Tiriti-based immigration and refugee resettlement. The refugee quota rises to 5,000 by 2029. The 12-month visa stand-down is removed.

System: Fair immigration and refugee resettlement

Scenario: The immigration and refugee plan is put into effect
When the government puts the immigration and refugee plan into effect
Then residence pathways are created for long-term migrants including people whose visas have expired
And Acceptable Standards of Health requirements are abolished
And migrant work visa holders may switch employers and the 12-month stand-down between visas is removed
And refugee family reunification backlogs clear, partner work rights expand, birthright citizenship is restored and parent sponsorship income barriers are removed
And immigration is based on Te Tiriti with devolved decision-making to hapū and iwi and Te Tiriti education for migrants and refugees
And the refugee quota progressively increases to 5,000 by 2029 with more resettlement funding and a humanitarian visa for New Zealanders' families in conflict zones
And Immigration New Zealand is resourced to clear tier 1 and tier 2 family reunification and process asylum quickly while stopping detention and electronic monitoring of asylum seekers
Outcome fair pathways to residency replace exploitative rules
