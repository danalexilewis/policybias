# Our understanding

> Declaring neutrality and refocusing Defence on disaster response and Pacific support would change posture—but commanders still lack a list of which treaties end and what replaces alliance tasks. A legal definition of neutrality and prohibited alignment must arrive before the declaration binds anyone.

System: Neutrality without the exit list

Scenario: Neutrality is declared
Given foreign policy should not be dictated by powerful nations, alliances or wars that are not ours
When Aotearoa is declared a militarily neutral nation
Then a legal definition of neutrality and of prohibited alignment must still be chosen
Outcome the posture is independence grounded in Māori values and Pacific relationships
But without that law, the declaration is a statement of intent

Scenario: Alignments are withdrawn and Defence is refocused
When military alignments that undermine sovereignty are reviewed and withdrawn
And the Defence Force refocuses on protection, disaster response and Pacific support
Then which treaties end, and what force structure replaces alliance tasks, must be listed
Outcome Defence is aimed at home protection and Pacific support
But until the exit list and the new force design exist, commanders keep both old and new duties
