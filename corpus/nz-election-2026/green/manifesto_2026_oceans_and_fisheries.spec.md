# What the page states

> Ocean protection puts mauri and kaitiakitanga ahead of short-term commercial extraction. Marine protected areas reform toward protecting at least 30% of ocean territory by 2030 while destructive fishing methods and seabed mining are banned. Cameras roll out on the entire fishing fleet and the Quota Management System moves toward ecosystem-based management. No budget figures are given.

System: Oceans and fisheries protection

Scenario: The oceans and fisheries plan is put into effect
When the government puts the oceans and fisheries plan into effect
Then marine protected areas legislation is reformed
And bottom trawling on seamounts is banned along with set netting and dredging
And seabed mining is banned in the coastal marine area and exclusive economic zone
And kaitiakitanga is embedded and iwi and hapū are resourced to lead ocean protection
And cameras roll out on the entire fishing fleet with fisheries-related mortality limits for protected species
And the Agreement on Marine Biological Diversity of Areas Beyond National Jurisdiction is ratified with support for high seas and Pacific marine protection
And the Quota Management System is reviewed toward ecosystem-based fisheries management
Output at least 30% of New Zealand's ocean territory protected
Outcome tangata whenua lead mātauranga- and tikanga-based ocean conservation
