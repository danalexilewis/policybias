# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> He uaua te tae ki te nui o te whenua tūmatanui, ā, ka noho kati ngā rohe nui nā ngā rārangi kaua-e-rere, kāore he take tiaki taiao mārama, i a Te Papa Atawhai e pehi ana i te CAA mō ngā momo topa. Mā te kaupapa here ka āhei te Game Animal Council ki te tohu wāhi taunga, me ngā whakamātautau uru mārama a Te Papa Atawhai, ka whakaarohia tuatahitia te uru hopu, ā, ka waiho te haumaru waka rererangi ki te Civil Aviation Authority. Kāore he nama, he rā whakatinana rānei e kitea ana.

System: Te uru tuawhenua mō te hopu me te kohikohi mio

Scenario: Ka whakatauhia ngā whakatau uru mō te whenua tiaki tūmatanui i ngā whenua taratara
Given he uaua te tae ki te nui o te whenua tūmatanui o Aotearoa, ā, i ngā whenua taratara pērā i a Fiordland ko te topa anake te huarahi whaihua
And ka noho kati ngā rohe nui nā ngā rārangi kaua-e-rere, kaua-e-taunga, kāore he take tiaki taiao, haumaru rānei e mārama ana
And ka taea e Te Papa Atawhai te whakahau i te momo topa e whakamahia ana e ngā kaiwhakahaere kohikohi mio arumoni, ahakoa nā te Civil Aviation Authority ngā waeture haumaru waka rererangi
When ka whakahoungia ngā ture uru tuawhenua
Then ka taea e te Game Animal Council te tohu wāhi taunga topa whaihua, e taea te tae
Output me whakamahi a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā whakamātautau mārama, tūmatanui, ōrite hoki ina whakatau kei hea ka taunga ngā waka rererangi
And ka tohutohungia te Outdoor Access Commission kia whakaarohia tuatahitia te uru hopu i tana hōtaka mahi
And ka aukatihia a Te Papa Atawhai ki te whakahau ēhea topa ka whakamahia e ngā kaiwhakahaere kohikohi mio arumoni, ā, ka waiho te haumaru waka rererangi ki te Civil Aviation Authority
Outcome ka taea e te Game Animal Council te herenga tango arumoni ā-ira kararehe ina hiahiatia, kia tōkeke te tiritiri a ngā kaipupuri hopu ngahau me ngā kaiwhakahaere kohikohi i tētahi rawa e pai te whakahaere
And me mātua take tiaki taiao, haumaru rānei ngā herenga uru, kaua ngā rārangi mahere tawhito anake
