# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whāia ngā whakaaetanga hokohoko hou ki ngā mākete e tupu tere ana i Ūropi, Amerika ki te Tonga, Āhia me Āwherika. Ka whakahekehia ngā ārai, ka whakamāmāhia ngā tukanga rohe, ā, mā ngā taputapu matihiko e āwhina ngā kaihoko ki tāwāhi kia māmā ake te tae ki ngā kiritaki. Ko te whāinga, kia hono ngā pakihi ki tata ki te kotahi piriona kiritaki hou e taea ana, ā, kia mau tonu te whāinga kia tāruatia te uara kaweake ā te 2034.

System: Te mahere hokohoko mō te piriona kiritaki e whai ake a Aotearoa

Scenario: Ka whāia te rārangi take hokohoko
Given i ēnei rā e tautoko ana te hokohoko i te kotahi i roto i te whā o ngā mahi o Aotearoa
When ka honoa ngā pakihi o Aotearoa ki tata ki te kotahi piriona kiritaki hou e taea ana
Then ka whāia ngā whakaaetanga hokohoko hou ki ngā mākete ā-ao e tupu tere ana
Output tata ki te kotahi piriona kiritaki hou e taea ana i ngā mākete e arotahia ana
Outcome ka mau tonu te whāinga kia tāruatia te uara o ngā kaweake a Aotearoa ā te 2034
