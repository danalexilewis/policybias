# What the page states

> Regulated professionals have faced discipline over lawful opinions expressed outside work. The policy bans regulators disciplining members for lawful private expression, requires institutional neutrality, limits mandatory training to competence and safety, and strengthens judicial oversight while preserving misconduct powers. No commencement date is given.

System: Free speech protections for regulated professionals

Scenario: A regulated professional expresses a lawful opinion outside their work
Given more than 200,000 New Zealanders work in regulated professions
And teachers, nurses, real estate agents and other professionals have faced disciplinary action or compulsory ideological training over opinions expressed outside their work
When the law is amended to keep regulators focused on professional performance and public safety
Then professional regulators may not discipline members for lawful expression outside their professional practice
Output regulators required to remain institutionally neutral on political and ideological issues
And mandatory training restricted to matters directly connected to professional competence and public safety
And strengthened judicial oversight so courts can rein in regulators that go beyond their legal powers
Outcome regulators retain ability to act against genuine professional misconduct, harm to clients or patients, and breaches of professional duties
And serious misconduct at work remains punishable while lawful private expression is not a professional offence
