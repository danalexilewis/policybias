# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka tū tētahi tari pūngao tūmatanui hei haumaru i te tuku, hei whakaheke i ngā utu. Ka mutu te keri hinu hou, ka whakakorea te tauranga LNG. Ka honohono te waeture ki tētahi rōpū kotahi. Ka amo ngā kāinga me te ahumahi i te utu mēnā ka tōmuri te āheinga whakakapi.

System: Te hanga pūngao tūmatanui me te wehe hinu

Scenario: Ka tū tētahi tari pūngao whakahou
When ka whakatūria tētahi tari hanganga pūngao whakahou hei haumaru i te tuku, hei whakaheke i ngā utu
Then ka pupuri tētahi rōpū tūmatanui i ngā kawenga e kīia ana kua rahua e te mākete anake
Outcome me pā ki ngā kāinga me te ahumahi he hiko haumaru ake, māmā ake te utu
But me utu e ngā Tahua o muri te rārangi hanga a te tari, nā te mea kāore tētahi i tāpirihia
Activates green-power-for-all-of-us

Scenario: Ka mutu te keri hinu hou, ka whakakorea te tauranga LNG
When ka aukatia te keri hinu hou, ka mutu ngā tautoko pūtea hinu, ka whakakorea te tauranga LNG
Then ka wehe aua kōwhiringa tuku i te mahere
Outcome me nui ake te pūnaha ki ngā pūngao whakahou, te rā tohatoha, me te urupare tono
But ka amo ngā kāinga me te ahumahi i te utu mēnā ka tōmuri te āheinga whakakapi

Scenario: Ka kohia te waeture ki tētahi kaiwhakahaere kotahi me tētahi Ministry for Energy
When ka whakatūria tētahi kaiwhakahaere pūngao kotahi me tētahi Ministry for Energy
Then ka noho te tirotiro me te kaupapa here ki ētahi rōpū Karauna iti ake, me ngā pou tarāwaho mana hapori me te Māori e takea ana i Te Tiriti
Outcome ka noho te uru tōkeke me te hautū Māori hei whāinga hoahoa ā-ture
But ka amo ngā kaiwhakahaere o nāianei me ā rātou kaimahi i te utu whakaheke kia oti rā anō te hononga
