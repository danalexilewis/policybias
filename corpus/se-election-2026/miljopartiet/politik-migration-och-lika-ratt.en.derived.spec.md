# Our understanding

> Resettlement is a counted intake. Asylum stays open as a right. The harder edge is who is already here and cannot be deported: established youth, workers changing track, and holders of permanent permits who cannot be withdrawn.

System: Counted quota and a harder right to stay

Scenario: Established youth are taken off the deportation list
When exceptionally compassionate circumstances return and young people may stay as family members into early adulthood
Then people who grew up in the country should keep their permits
Outcome deportation at majority closes for that group
But the Migration Agency must still decide each case, and no extra caseworker number is stated

Scenario: Permanent residence becomes the default and cannot be withdrawn later
When permanent residence is the default and cannot be revoked afterwards
Then status no longer runs out in a temporary cycle
Outcome people can plan work, language and family life on a standing permit
But a later security or fraud finding has no revocation route the policy names
