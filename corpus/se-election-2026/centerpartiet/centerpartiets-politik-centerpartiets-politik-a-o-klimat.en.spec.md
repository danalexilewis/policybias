# What the page states

> Climate change threatens the future, nature and the economy, while the transition is often complicated and unfair. Anyone who wants to do the right thing meets high costs, slow permit processes and uncertain rules of the game. Policy is to be ambitious, market-driven and long-term: it should cost to emit and be profitable to invest in green solutions. Net zero in Sweden and the EU by 2040 at the latest.

System: A price on emissions, fossil-free power and net zero

Scenario: A firm weighs a fossil investment against a green one
Given climate change threatens our future, nature and the economy
And the transition is often complicated and unfair, with high costs, slow permit processes and uncertain rules of the game
When the climate target is tightened to net-zero emissions in Sweden and the EU and fossil subsidies are stopped
Then emissions are to fall quickly
And all fossil-free power is expanded, and new nuclear is to be able to be built on commercial terms without subsidies
And negative emissions through bio-CCS are to capture and store carbon dioxide
And permit processes for electricity grids, green industry and climate-smart investment are to be sped up
Outcome the transition is to create jobs, strengthen firms and give people the freedom to choose sustainable solutions in town and country
