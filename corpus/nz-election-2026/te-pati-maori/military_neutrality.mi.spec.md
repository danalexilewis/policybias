# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakapuakina a Aotearoa hei whenua kūwaho ā-hoia. Ka whakahoungia te kaupapa here tāwāhi kia motuhake, kia ū ki ngā uara Māori, kia arotahi ki ngā hononga ki Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa me ngā iwi taketake. Ka arotakehia, ka unuhia hoki ngā hononga hoia e whakaiti ana i te mana motuhake, ā, ka arotahi anō Te Ope Kātua o Aotearoa ki te tiaki, ki te urupare aituā, ki te tautoko i Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa. Ka ūtia ngā motika tangata o te ao, ā, ka kawea ngā kaihara pakanga ki te kawenga.

System: Te noho kūwaho ā-hoia me te kaupapa here tāwāhi motuhake

Scenario: Ka whakapuakina a Aotearoa hei whenua kūwaho ā-hoia
Given e kore e tika kia whakahaua te kaupapa here tāwāhi e ngā iwi kaha, e ngā hononga, e ngā pakanga rānei ehara nō tātou
