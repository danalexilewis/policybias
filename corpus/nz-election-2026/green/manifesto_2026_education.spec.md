# What the page states

> Education would be treated as a public good grounded in Te Tiriti o Waitangi and universal human rights. Early childhood education would become free with for-profit centres stopped, curriculum would reset to the original Te Mātaiaho direction, and learning support would expand through a single coordination agency. Ka Ora, Ka Ako would grow toward universal school lunches, a Māori Education Authority would govern Māori medium education, and fair wages and staffing would be co-developed with the sector. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Inclusive public education

Scenario: The education plan is put into effect
When the government puts the education plan into effect
Then early childhood education is made free and provided as a public good with for-profit centres stopped
And curriculum and assessment reforms are reset to the original Te Mātaiaho direction
And learning support criteria are expanded with a single coordination agency and all Framework for Action measures implemented
And Ka Ora, Ka Ako is expanded toward a universal warm nutritious locally made school lunch
And a Māori Education Authority governs and funds Māori medium and Kaupapa Māori education
And fair wages, staffing levels and a long-term workforce plan are co-developed with the sector
Outcome tino rangatiratanga, te reo Māori and mātauranga Māori are central to the system
