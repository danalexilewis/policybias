# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā ngā mīhini tohu e waitohu ētahi mahi hanga me te kore tirotiro a te kaunihera, mā te whakature i ngā producer statements i te Building Act. Mā tētahi Building Consent Authority mātanga e whakaae ngā whare arumoni nui mā tētahi tukanga ā-motu kotahi. Ko te whāinga, kia tere ake ngā whakaaetanga mō ngā kaupapa nui, kia iti ake te tāruarua, kia wātea ngā kaunihera ki te arotahi ki ngā mahi noho.
