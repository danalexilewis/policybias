# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakakorea āta te pāhūpāhū hauota hanga, ā, ka heke ngā reiti kāhui ā-rōpū-awa. Ka ngaro i ngā pāmu tētahi rawa iti te utu, ā, ka iti ake ngā kararehe. Ka whai ngā awa me te wai inu i te āwhina. Ka tae mai te wātaka me te nama pāhūpāhū i muri.

System: Ngā pae raro wai māori hei aukati rawa pāmu

Scenario: Ka whakakorea āta te pāhūpāhū hauota hanga
When ka whakakorea āta te pāhūpāhū hauota hanga
Then ka ngaro i ngā pāmu tētahi rawa nui e whakanui ana ināianei i te tupu o te pātītī
Outcome ka iti ake te pēhanga hauota ki ngā awa me te wai inu
But ka ngaro i ngā pāmu tētahi rawa iti te utu i mua i te taenga mai o tētahi moni whakawhiti

Scenario: Ka heke ngā reiti kāhui ā-rōpū-awa
When ka whakahekea ngā reiti kāhui ā-rōpū-awa
Then ka iti ake ngā kararehe o ngā pāmu e pā ana i tēnei rā
Outcome ka taea te pai ake o te kounga wai o te rōpū-awa
But ka iti ake ngā kararehe a ngā kaipāmu e pā ana i mua i tō rātou mōhio ko ēhea rōpū-awa ka neke tuatahi

Scenario: Ka here ngā pae raro me Te Mana o Te Wai i te whakamahi whenua ki runga ake
When ka whakatinanahia ngā pae raro kaha ā-motu, tae atu ki tētahi here pāhūpāhū iti ake mō te wai inu
And ka whakahokia Te Mana o Te Wai kia whakamāmā i te hauora wai māori
Then me tutuki te whakamahi whenua ki runga ake o te wai inu me ngā awa i te ture kaha ake
Outcome ka taea te huri i te heke o te wai māori mēnā ka here te ture
But me tutuki ngā pāmu ki runga ake o ngā tomokanga wai inu i mua i te tānga o te nama pāhūpāhū
