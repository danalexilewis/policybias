# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka tohu te Game Animal Council i ngā wāhi taunga waka topatopa, me whakamahi a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā whakamātautau uru mārama, ā, ka noho te haumaru waka rererangi ki te Civil Aviation Authority. Ko te whakamātautau tūmatanui, ēhea wāhi tuatahi, me te pehea e whakamanahia ai ngā here ira ki te tango arumoni, kāore i āta tohua.

System: Ngā wāhi taunga, ngā whakamātautau uru, me tētahi wehenga haumaru

Scenario: Ka tohu te Game Animal Council i ngā wāhi taunga waka topatopa
Given he uaua te tae ki te nuinga o ngā whenua tūmatanui o Aotearoa, ā, i te whenua taratara pērā i Te Rua-o-te-moko ko te waka topatopa anake pea te huarahi mahi ki roto
And ka noho kati ngā wāhi nui i ngā rārangi kaua-e-rere me ngā rārangi kaua-e-tau, kāore he take tiaki taiao, haumaru rānei e mārama ana
When ka taea e te Game Animal Council te tohu i ngā wāhi taunga waka topatopa e tika ana, e taea te tae
Then ka whiwhi ngā kaiwhai me ngā kaiwhakahaere tango i ngā tomokanga mahi kāore i a rātou i mua
Outcome me whai take tiaki taiao, haumaru rānei e mārama ana ngā here uru, kaua ko ngā rārangi mahere tawhito anake
But ko ngā taunga nui ake kāore he aroturuki, he mahere kāhui, he ture taupatupatu rānei ka whakatuwhera i te whenua tere ake i te whakahaere

Scenario: Me whakamārama a Te Papa Atawhai ki hea ka taea te tau o ngā waka rererangi
When e herea ana a Te Papa Atawhai ki te whakamahi i ngā whakamātautau mārama, tūmatanui, ōrite hoki ina whakatau ki hea ka taea te tau o ngā waka rererangi
Then ka ngaro te tū o tētahi katinga kāore e taea te tutuki i te whakamātautau
Outcome ka taea te whakamārama i ngā whakatau uru ki te iwi
But kāore e mōhio ngā kaiwhakahaere me ngā kaiwhai ēhea rārangi katinga ka tū, kia whakaputaina rā anō te whakamātautau

Scenario: Ka noho te haumaru waka rererangi ki te Civil Aviation Authority
Given ka taea e Te Papa Atawhai te whakahau i te momo waka topatopa e whakamahia ana e ngā kaiwhakahaere tango hiroa arumoni ahakoa te waeture haumaru waka rererangi a te Civil Aviation Authority
When ka aukatia a Te Papa Atawhai ki te whakahau ēhea waka topatopa e whakamahia ana e ngā kaiwhakahaere tango hiroa arumoni, ā, ka waiho te haumaru waka rererangi ki te Civil Aviation Authority
Then ka tūtaki ngā kaiwhakahaere ki ngā ture rererangi mō te tika-rere me ngā ture a Te Papa Atawhai mō ngā pānga tiaki taiao hei pene wehe
Outcome ka taka ngā here turbine i takai i te haumaru ki te kākahu tiaki taiao
But ka noho ngā here tiaki taiao e here tonu ana i te pehea me te wāhi o te rere tango hei taputapu a Te Papa Atawhai
