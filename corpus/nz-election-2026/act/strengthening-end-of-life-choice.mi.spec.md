# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> I tūtohua e te arotake ā-ture o te End of Life Choice Act ngā panonitanga e 25, ā, ka aukati te ture matapae e ono marama i ētahi kaitono e mate ana. Ka whakatinana te Pire whakatikatika i ngā tūtohutanga e 25 katoa, ka whakakapi i te whakamātautau matapae ki te mate mate-ā-kiko e kaha haere ana, e mate ana, ā, ka tuku i tētahi whakakore iti o te whakaae whakamutunga. Ka mana te Pire i te rā i muri i te Royal assent.

System: Ngā whakatikatika o te End of Life Choice Act

Scenario: Ka whakaae te Pāremata i te End of Life Choice Amendment Bill
Given kua homai e te End of Life Choice Act tēnei kōwhiringa ki ngā tāngata o Aotearoa mai i te 2021
And i tūtohua e te arotake ā-ture tuatahi a Manatū Hauora ngā panonitanga e 25 ki te ture
And tata ki te 1,700 ngā tāngata o Aotearoa kua kōwhiri i te mate āwhina
And neke atu i te 300 ngā kaitono i kitea kāore e āhei i tērā tau, ā, 42 ngā tāngata i unu
When ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga e 25 katoa o te arotake ā-ture a Manatū Hauora
Then ka whakakapia te whakaritenga matapae e ono marama ki te whakaritenga he mate e kaha haere ana, e tūmanakohia ana ka mate te tangata
Output ka mau tonu ērā atu whakamātautau āhei, tae atu ki te 18 tau neke atu, te heke kāore e hoki, te mamae kāore e taea te whakangāwari tika, me te āhei ki te whakatau māramatanga
And tētahi whakakore iti o te whakaae whakamutunga ina kua aromatawaitia te tangata he āhei, i kōwhiri i te rā me te wā i a ia e mārama ana, engari ka ngaro tōna āhei i mua i tērā rā
Outcome me whakangungu ngā kaimahi hauora, me te tirotiro kaha ake a te Kairēhita, me te kawenga ki te tirotiro kāore ngā tono i te pehanga
And ka mana te Pire i te rā i muri i te Royal assent
And kāore e aukatihia ngā tāngata e mate ana, e heke ana kāore e hoki, e mamae ana kāore e taea te whakangāwari, nā te mea kāore e taea e te tākuta te kī ka mate i roto i ngā marama e ono
