# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka pā te tāhae whare ki ngā kāinga maha, ā, ko te nuinga o ngā kaitāhae kua tukuna ka hara anō wawe. Ka waihanga te kaupapa here i tētahi tikanga e toru ngā patu e here ana i te whiu whare herehere iti rawa e toru tau, kāore he parōle, i muri i te whakawhiu tuatoru mō te tāhae whare, ā, ka wātea tonu ki ngā kaiwhakawā ki te mōrahi tekau tau. Kāore he utu, he tatau kaitāhae rānei i homai.

System: E toru ngā patu mō te tāhae whare

Scenario: Ka tae tētahi kaitāhae whare auau ki te whakawhiu tuatoru mō te tāhae whare
Given 184,000 ngā tāngata o Aotearoa i pā ki te tāhae whare i te 2025
And ko te nuinga o te hunga kua tukuna i te whare herehere mō te tāhae whare ka whakahokia ki te whare herehere i roto i ngā tau e rua
And tata ki te toru hauwhā o ngā kaitāhae kua tukuna ka whiu anō
And tata ki te kotahi i roto i te whā o ngā tāngata i pā ki te tāhae whare kua tāhaetia tō rātou whare i mua
When ka whakawhiua tētahi mō te tāhae whare e toru, ka whakawhiua rānei kotahi mō ngā tāhae whare e toru, neke atu rānei
Then ka whiwhi rātou i te whiu iti rawa e toru tau ki te whare herehere, kāore he parōle, kāore he mauhere kāinga, kāore he tuku wawe
Output e toru tau te whiu whare herehere iti rawa, kāore he parōle
And ka taea e te kaiwhakawā te whiu i waenga i te iti rawa e toru tau me te mōrahi 10 tau
Outcome ka hereherea ngā kaitāhae whare auau, e kore e taea e rātou te tāhae whare i te whare herehere
And ka whakaoho tētahi tāhae whare kua whakakino i tētahi patu i tēnei tikanga me te tikanga Three Strikes o nāianei mō te hara tūkino
