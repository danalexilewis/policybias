# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te hora ngā rākau paina mōhiti me ngā pīkaunga otaota kāore i whakahaerehia i runga i te whenua atawhai, i te wā ka pā ngā aukati ki ngā kaiahuwhenua e rapu ana i ngā whakaaetanga whāngai. Ka whakatikatikahia e te kaupapa here te Conservation Act kia tukuna e Te Papa Atawhai ngā raihana whāngai kararehe, me ngā anga rauropi, ngā here mahi, me te whakamana i te taiepa mariko. Kāore he penapena tāra, he rā whakatinana rānei i homai.

System: Whāngai kararehe kua tohua i runga i te whenua atawhai

Scenario: Ka tuku a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā raihana whāngai kararehe i runga i te whenua atawhai mō te whakahaere otaota
Given e 1.8 miriona heketea whenua e katihia ana e ngā rākau paina mōhiti
And kei te whakahaere a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā wāhi nui o te whenua tuwhera, whenua pāmu o mua, me ētahi atu whenua atawhai kāore he ngahere, ka taea te whāngai haumaru i raro i ngā here
And he maha ngā wā ka tūtaki ngā kaiahuwhenua e rapu ana i ngā whakaaetanga whāngai ki te ātete, ki ngā aukati kāore e tika ana rānei
And kei te utu ngā kaipupuri tāke i te rehu rangi me te tapahi ā-ringa nui te utu i raro i ngā ture o nāianei
When ka whakatikatikahia te Conservation Act 1987 kia homai ki Te Papa Atawhai te mana mārama me te ahunga ki te tuku raihana whāngai kararehe mō ngā wāhi kua tohua ina tautoko te whāngai i ngā whāinga whakahaere otaota
Then ka tautuhi tētahi anga rauropi mārama i hea e tika ana te whāngai ki te pehi i ngā pihinga rākau paina me ētahi atu otaota tauhou, me te whakaiti i ngā pīkaunga wahie ahi ngahere
Output ngā raihana whāngai kararehe e tohu ana i ngā momo kararehe, ngā reiti me te roanga e tika ana
And ka whakamanahia ngā pūnaha taiepa mariko hei tikanga whakaae mō te aukati kararehe i runga i te whenua o Te Papa Atawhai
Outcome ka pehia ngā otaota tauhou me ngā riha mā te whāngai kua tohua, kaua mā te rehu wakatōpū anake
And ka whakaitihia ngā pīkaunga wahie e whai wāhi ana ki ngā ahi ngahere kino
