# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whiwhi ngā hapū me ngā iwi i tētahi pūtea urutaunga motuhake. Ka noho ētahi kāinga anō ki roto i te kapi Natural Hazards i muri i ngā āwhā. Ka nui ake te āwhina ki ngā hoa o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa. He pire tū tonu ā-Karauna tētahi, tētahi o ēnei.

System: Ngā pūtea manawaroa kua whakaingoatia, kāore he rahi i tāia

Scenario: Ka tū te Māori Climate Resilience Fund, kāore he rahi
When ka utua tētahi Māori Climate Resilience Fund
Then ka taea e ngā hapū me ngā iwi te tango i tētahi puna urutaunga motuhake
Outcome ka taea e ngā hapū me ngā iwi te tango i tētahi puna urutaunga motuhake mō te whenua, te wai me ngā taonga
But kāore e taea e ngā hapū me ngā iwi te whakamahere mahi kia whakatauria rā anō e te Rūnanga Minita tā te pūtea e taea te whakapau

Scenario: Ka whānui te kapi Natural Hazards ki ngā aituā āhuarangi me te whakataunga
When ka whakawhānui te Natural Hazards Commission ki te kapi i ngā aituā huarere āhuarangi me te whakataunga e ārahi ana te hapori
Then ka noho ētahi kāinga me ngā hapori anō ki roto i te kapi
Outcome ka noho ētahi kāinga me ngā hapori anō ki roto i te kapi i muri i ngā āwhā me ngā waipuke
But ka piki ngā utu inihua, ka nui ake rānei te tautoko ā-Karauna — ā, kāore te kaupapa here e kōwhiri i tētahi huarahi

Scenario: Ka nui ake te tautoko o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa, kāore he nama whakawhiti
When ka whakanuia te tautoko kia urutau ngā hoa o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
Then ka nui ake te āwhina urutaunga e wehe i a Aotearoa ki ngā whenua tata
Outcome ka nui ake te āwhina urutaunga e wehe i a Aotearoa ki ngā whenua tata
But me kimi e ngā Tahua o muri te moni mō tērā āwhina, nā te mea kāore he taupane
