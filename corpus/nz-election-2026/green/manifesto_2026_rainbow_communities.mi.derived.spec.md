# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka noho te manaaki whakaae-ira i runga i te whakaae mōhio, tae atu ki te whakahoki i te aukati puberty blocker, hei tuku tūmatanui kore utu. Kei te taha ngā pānga Human Rights Act, tētahi aukati poka intersex, me te whakamana manene. Me tuku tonu ngā whare haumanu i te manaaki.

System: Te hauora me ngā mōtika āniwaniwa, kāore he nama kaha

Scenario: Ka tukuna te manaaki whakaae-ira kore utu i runga i te whakaae mōhio
When ka whai pūtea ngā huarahi mārama mō te hauora whakaae-ira wawe, kore utu, kore whakahāwea i runga i te whakaae mōhio, tae atu ki te whakahoki i te aukati puberty blocker
Then kāore te hunga e rapu ana i tērā manaaki e utu, ā, ka hoki mai te huarahi blocker
Outcome ka huri te urunga ki te whakaae me te tino rangatiratanga, kaua ki tētahi aukati
But ka mau i ngā whare haumanu me ngā hōhipera te pire ina kaha ake te hiahia i ngā moenga me ngā kaimahi kei a rātou

Scenario: Ka whakamāramatia ngā pānga Human Rights Act
When ka whakamārama te Human Rights Act ko te aronga hōkakatanga, te tuakiri ira, me ngā rerekētanga āhuatanga ira ngā pānga aukati o te whakahāwea, ā, ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga Ia Tangata
Then ka taea e ngā kerēme whakahāwea te tohu mārama i aua pānga
Outcome he pākaha ake te whakangungu ā-ture a ngā takatāpui me ngā tāngata āniwaniwa
But me tango e te Human Rights Commission me ngā rārangi kōti te katoa o ngā kerēme hou

Scenario: Ka aukatia ngā poka intersex kāore i whakaaetia
When ka aukatia ngā poka, ngā wawaotanga rongoā rānei kāore i whakaaetia, kāore hoki e hiahiatia ā-rongoā, ki ngā tāngata intersex
Then ka mutu aua wawaotanga hei mahi ā-ture
Outcome ka tiakina te tapu tinana intersex e tētahi aukati pākaha
But me whai tonu ngā whare haumanu i ngā kawa me ngā kaimahi tirotiro kia tūturu te aukati
