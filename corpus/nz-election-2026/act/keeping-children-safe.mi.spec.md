# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka taea e ngā tirotiro haumaru te tārewa tae atu ki te toru tau ahakoa ka puta mai he mōhiohio hōu a Ngā Pirihimana. Ka waihanga te kaupapa here i tētahi Kāri Mahi me ngā Tamariki ā-motu kotahi mō ngā kaimahi utu me ngā kaiāwhina, e tirotirohia pūmautia ana ki ngā mōhiohio a Ngā Pirihimana, me te whakatārewatanga tōmua ina whakapae hara taumaha, me te whakangungu tiaki tamariki i te whakahounga. Kāore he rārangi hara, he rā tīmata rānei i homai.

System: Kāri Mahi me ngā Tamariki mō ngā tūranga kua kapi

Scenario: Ka whai tētahi kaimahi utu, kaiāwhina rānei ki te mahi ki ngā tamariki
Given ka taea e te pūnaha o nāianei te waiho e toru tau i waenga i ngā tirotiro haumaru ahakoa ka puta mai he mōhiohio hōu āwangawanga a Ngā Pirihimana
And ka mahia ngā tirotiro haumaru e ia kaiwhakawhiwhi mahi, ā, i te nuinga o te wā ka tirotirohia anō i ia toru tau anake
When ka waihangatia tētahi kāri ā-motu kotahi e tika ana mā ngā kaimahi utu me ngā kaiāwhina ki te mahi ki ngā tamariki
Then nō te kaipupuri te kāri, ā, e toru tau te roa
Output ka tirotirohia pūmautia ngā kaipupuri kāri ki ngā mōhiohio hōu a Ngā Pirihimana
And ka whakatārewatia te kāri ina mōhio Ngā Pirihimana tērā pea he morearea tētahi, ka mutu te mahi ki ngā tamariki, ā, ka whakamōhiotia ia whakahaere kua honoa
And ka whakahoungia te whakangungu tiaki tamariki whaihua ina whakahoungia te kāri
And ka whakawāteatia, ka whakaheke utu rānei ngā tono mō ngā kaiāwhina me ngā ākonga
And tata ki te $108 ka utua e ngā kaimahi utu, he maha ngā wā ka utua e ngā kaiwhakawhiwhi mahi
And $12–20 million te utu whakatū kotahi
And tata ki te $50 million i te tau te utu whakahaere
And tata ki te $37.5 million i te tau ka whakahokia mā ngā utu tono
And tata ki te $12.5 million i te tau te utu toenga ki ngā kaipākeha tāke
Outcome ka aukatihia te hunga kua whakatauhia mō ngā hara taumaha kua tohua ki ngā tamariki i ngā tūranga kua kapi
And ka whiwhi tōmua ngā kaiwhakawhiwhi mahi i te pānui ina me mutu te mahi a tētahi kaipupuri kāri ki ngā tamariki i te wā o te aromatawai
