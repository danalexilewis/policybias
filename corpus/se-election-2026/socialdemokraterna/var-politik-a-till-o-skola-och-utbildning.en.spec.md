# What the page states

> The school's resources are to go to teaching, teachers and pupils, not to profit extraction.
> The school voucher is recast so that independent schools are not overcompensated, and religious independent schools are banned.
> Binding rules are to give more teachers in the early years so that every pupil receives support and class sizes fall in the early years.

System: A profit ban and a recast school voucher

Scenario: Tax money is steered from profit to teaching
Given a chase for profit, disruption and large classes lead to more people than ever being failed by school
And large differences between schools and pupils who do not receive the support they need
When profit extraction is banned and the school voucher is recast
Then tax money goes to teaching, teachers and pupils, and independent schools are not overcompensated
Outcome the school is steered by pupils' needs, religious independent schools are banned, and more teachers are to give study calm in the early years
