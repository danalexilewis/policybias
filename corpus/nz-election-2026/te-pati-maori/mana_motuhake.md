### **What we’ll do**

*   Commit to Te Tiriti-based constitutional change.
*   Strengthen Māori political authority and governance structures.
*   Progress land back and rangatiratanga commitments under Te Tiriti o Waitangi.

### **Why it matters**

Without constitutional transformation, every gain our people make can be undone by the next government. Mana motuhake means power must return to where it belongs: with tangata whenua, hapū, iwi, whānau, and our people.
