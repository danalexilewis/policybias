# What the page states

> Major power companies would be split into separate generators and retailers, alongside a nationwide energy strategy. Pricing rules would stop companies holding back supply to drive prices higher, and new generation would get long-term fixed-price contracts with priority dispatch. Households generating solar could sell power back to the grid at the retail price they pay. No contract prices or timetables are given.

System: Electricity market split and nationwide energy strategy

Scenario: Gentailers are split and electricity pricing is reformed
Given the big four power companies control almost 90% of electricity generation and sell it back to themselves
And the most expensive generator sets the price for all electricity under the current system
When the big power companies are split into generators and retailers
Then companies cannot hold back supply just to drive prices higher
And long-term fixed-price contracts are guaranteed for new-build generation projects with their power first to be used
And households generating solar can sell power back to the grid at the same price they pay for it
Outcome more power stations, renewable energy, competition and resilience
