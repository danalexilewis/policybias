# What the page states

> Anyone who is not well is forced into unfreedom. Every patient is to receive good care in time, with a named GP contact, whether the illness is common, rare or chronic. Staff are to have time for patients and relatives, and continuing professional development throughout working life.

System: Named GP contact and time for the patient

Scenario: A patient with a chronic or rare disease seeks care
Given Swedish healthcare is in many respects excellent but large problems exist and must be closed
And not everyone yet has a named family doctor in primary care and specialist care
And rare diseases are treated too poorly
And relatives need more support
When a named doctor for everyone is introduced and working conditions in care are improved
Then every individual gets help, comfort and support in time
And there is time for patients and for relatives
And continuing professional development is an obvious right throughout working life
And access to knowledgeable care and to effective medicines improves for people with rare conditions
And respite for relatives becomes a right
Outcome the patient meets a named doctor instead of a new face each time
And the professions in care get time for research and development of healthcare
