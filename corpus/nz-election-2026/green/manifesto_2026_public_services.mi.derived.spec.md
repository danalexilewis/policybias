# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka rere Te Tiriti i roto i te ratonga tūmatanui, ka noho ngā pupuri a te kāwanatanga ki ngā ringa tūmatanui, ka arotahi te whakahou pūtea ki te toiora, ka hoki mai te tirotiro a te Children's Commissioner, ka ōrite te utu me te whakangungu, ā, ka utua katoatia te rāngai hapori. Kāore he nama tāra i tētahi o aua rārangi.

System: Te pūtea kāore i inea me tētahi kaitirotiro ā-hinonga

Scenario: Me tika te pūtea o ngā ratonga tūmatanui, me pupuri hei mea tūmatanui
When ka utua ngā ratonga tūmatanui me ngā hinonga a te kāwanatanga kia noho ki ngā ringa tūmatanui
Then ka noho te rangatiratanga ki te Karauna, kaua ki te hoko
Outcome kāore te hokonga tūmataiti o aua pupuri i runga i tēnei mahere
But ka mau i ngā kaipākeha tāke te tikanga o te "tika" ina utua ngā ratonga

Scenario: Ka whakahouhia te pūtea tūmatanui mō te toiora me te manawaroa
When ka whakahouhia te pūnaha pūtea tūmatanui kia tautoko te whakahaere tahua i te manawaroa roa, te kaha whakaputa, me te toiora
Then ka whakawāhia ngā kōwhiringa Tahua ki aua whāinga, me ngā tapahi poto
Outcome ka neke te paearu mō te whakahaere tahua
But kāore te arotahi toiora e tāpiri moni kia whakatauria rā anō e te rā Tahua te kōpaki o ia tari

Scenario: Ka whiwhi anō te Children's Commissioner i te mahi tirotiro
When ka whakahokia te mahi tirotiro a te Children's Commissioner i te taha o te kaitaunaki
Then ka kaitaunaki, ka tirotiro anō tētahi kōmihana motuhake
Outcome he kaitirotiro kua whakaingoatia tā ngā tamariki i te hanganga kōmihana
But me whai tonu te rōpū tirotiro a te Commissioner i ngā utu me te tahua me tuku e tētahi
