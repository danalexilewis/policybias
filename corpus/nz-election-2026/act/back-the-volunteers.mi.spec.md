# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> E kore e taea e Te Papa Atawhai anake te tiaki i tana whatunga whare me ngā ara nui, ā, he maha ngā rōpū tūao e mahi ana i runga i ngā pūtea ā-tau ka taea te tuku. Ka hoatu te kaupapa here ki ngā rōpū hapori te wā tuatahi me ngā kirimana tau-maha mō te tiaki tuawhenua, ka whakatuwhera i ngā ira taiao ki ngā rōpū whakangau whai tohu, ā, ka hoatu māngai ōkawa ki ngā rōpū whakangau i ngā whakatau tango konihi e pā ana ki ngā kaiwhakangau. Kāore he tahua tāra, he taipitopito mākete ira rānei i homai.

System: Te tiaki tuawhenua mā ngā rōpū hapori me te tiaki taiao ā-whakangau

Scenario: Ka pūtea a Te Papa Atawhai i ngā rōpū hapori me ngā rōpū whakangau e kaha ana mō ngā mahi tuawhenua
Given kei a Te Papa Atawhai te kawenga mō ngā whare neke atu i te 950 me ngā ara 15,000 kiromita
And e 30 ōrau te korenga tiaki e pā ana ki a Te Papa Atawhai
And neke atu i te 2,300 ngā whakamōhiotanga kati kua tukuna e Te Papa Atawhai mai i te 2021
And ka whakahou ngā rōpū whakangau, hikoi me te tiaki taiao i ngā whare, ka tiaki i ngā ara, ka whakahaere i ngā rārangi tāwai, he wāhanga iti noa o te utu
And he maha ngā rōpū e ora ana i ngā pūtea ā-tau ka taea te tuku, kāore he mōhio ka haere tonu te pūtea
When me hoatu e Te Papa Atawhai ki ngā rōpū hapori te wā tuatahi ki te pūtea ina tuku rātou i tētahi kōwhiringa whakataetae mō te tiaki tuawhenua
Then ka taea e ngā rōpū tūao te pupuri kirimana tau-maha, kaua ko te tono ia tau
Output ka whakatuwheratia te Voluntary Nature Credits Market ki ngā rōpū whakangau whai tohu mō ngā hua kanorau koiora kua whakamanahia
And he māngai ōkawa mō te New Zealand Deerstalkers Association me te Game Animal Council i ngā whakatau tango konihi e pā ana ki ngā kaiwhakangau
Outcome ka mahi ngā rōpū hapori e kaha ana i ngā mahi tiaki tuawhenua
And ka whiwhi ngā rōpū whakangau whai tohu i ngā ira taiao mō ngā hua tiaki taiao ka taea te whakamana motuhake
