# What the page states

> The task of taxes is to fund welfare, even out incomes and steer climate and environmental policy. Since the 1990s reform — lower tax on work, tighter capital taxation, less tax planning — the system has become a jumble of deductions and reductions. The proposals are an oligopoly tax on the banks' excess profits, equal tax on equal income, a tax reduction for union fees, a green ROT deduction for energy efficiency, and higher vehicle tax on large energy-hungry cars.
