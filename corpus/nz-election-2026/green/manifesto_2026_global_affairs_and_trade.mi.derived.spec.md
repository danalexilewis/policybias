# Tō mātou māramatanga

> E kīia ana te kaupapa here tāwāhi he motuhake: ka huri te waonga ki te mahi āhuarangi me te mahi atawhai, ka whakakāhoretia a AUKUS, ka wehea a Five Eyes, ka whakamanatia a Palestine, ā, ka tāpiri Trade For All i ngā whakamātautau tiriti, mahi, tika tangata me te āhuarangi ki ngā kirimana hokohoko. Kāore he nama tāra, he kaitiaki kua whakaingoatia rānei mō te huri waonga me te whakamana Trade For All.

System: Te wehe hononga me ngā paerewa hokohoko kāore i whakamanahia

Scenario: Ka mutu ngā whakaritenga mātauranga me te hononga
When ka wehe ngā anga hōia me te haumaru, tae atu ki te whakakāhore i a AUKUS me te unu i Five Eyes
Then kāore aua whakaritenga e pā anō
Outcome ka noho a Aotearoa ki waho o AUKUS me Five Eyes
But ka hoki te tiritiri mātauranga ki ngā kirimana takirua e whakaritea ana e ngā kaipupuri hononga i muri

Scenario: Ka tautohua anō te waonga, kāore he tahua i tāia
When ka arotahi te kaupapa here waonga ki ngā urupare āhuarangi, te urupare atawhai me te aroturuki taiao
Then e whakaarohia ana ka whai te hoahoa ope i aua mahi
Outcome ka huri te whakamahere waonga ki ngā mahi āhuarangi me te mahi atawhai
But me puta tonu ngā kaimahi, ngā kaipuke me te moni whakahaere i ngā tahua waonga o nāianei

Scenario: Me tutuki ngā whakaaetanga hokohoko i ētahi whakamātautau anō
When ka whakamana te kaupapa Trade For All i Te Tiriti, ngā tika kaimahi, ngā tika tangata me ngā here āhuarangi i ngā whakaaetanga hokohoko
Then e whakaarohia ana kāore tētahi kirimana e rahua aua whakamātautau e haere
Outcome e whakaarohia ana ka tutuki ngā kirimana ā muri ake i ngā whakamātautau tiriti, mahi, tika me te āhuarangi
But ka whakatau tonu ngā kaiwhiriwhiri āhea ka rahua tētahi kirimana i aua whakamātautau, ki te kore te Pāremata e whakaingoa i tētahi kaiwhakamana
