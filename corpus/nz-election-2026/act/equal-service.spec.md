# What the page states

> The repeal of sections 73 and 118, need-based service law, end to public-funded spiritual practices, removal of cultural job tests, and Public Service Act rewrite are all on the source page. The page carries no dollar figures and no detailed compliance mechanism.

System: One public service for all New Zealanders

Scenario: Separate public service tracks are removed and one standard is set
Given previous governments built separate tracks for different groups and many of those rules remain
And agencies have already been directed to deliver services based on need, not identity
And the Public Service Act requires politically neutral public servants and merit-based appointments
When race-based rules are removed and the Public Service Act is rewritten
Then section 73 of the Public Service Act and section 118 of the Crown Entities Act are repealed
Output public services allocated to people who need them regardless of identity
And the need-based rule put in law so people can challenge decisions that break it in court
And an end to use of public time and public funds for spiritual or religious activities at work
Outcome government jobs go to the best candidate on merit
And agencies remove cultural requirements from job ads and position descriptions unless the knowledge is actually needed for the job
And the public service stays politically neutral, hires on merit, treats people equally and is judged on whether services reach the people who need them
