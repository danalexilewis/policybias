# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whiwhi te tokoora kararehe i tētahi Kaikōmihana motuhake me tētahi manatū, me ngā tikanga pāmu kaha ake mai i tētahi National Animal Welfare Advisory Committee kua whai pūtea. Ka whakakorea ngā mahi kino tae atu ki te ahuwhenua wheketere, te rōdeo me ngā pouaka whānau poaka, ā, me tutuki ngā hua kawemai ki ngā paerewa tokoora o konei. Ka whakaroa te aukati kaweake ora mā te moana ki ngā kaweake ā-rangi me ngā kararehe hopu-mohoao pērā i te tuna heke. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te ture me ngā paerewa tokoora kararehe

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere tokoora kararehe
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere tokoora kararehe
Then ka whakatūria he Kaikōmihana mō ngā Kararehe me tētahi manatū motuhake
And ka whai pūtea te National Animal Welfare Advisory Committee me ngā tikanga kararehe-pāmu kaha ake
And ka whakakorea te tapahi ngutu, te tāiwi takurua kaha, te ahuwhenua wheketere, te ahuwhenua wheke, ngā pouaka whānau poaka me te rōdeo
And me tutuki ngā hua kawemai ki ngā paerewa tokoora kararehe o Aotearoa, tae atu ki ngā rongoā ātaahua
And ka pā ngā paerewa whakamana ki ngā kerēme kōhanga-wātea, kore-nanakia me te vegan
And ka pūmautia te aukati kaweake ora mā te moana, ā, ka whakaroa ki ngā kaweake ā-rangi me ngā kararehe hopu-mohoao pērā i te tuna heke
Outcome ka mutu ngā mahi ahuwhenua me te kaweake kino
