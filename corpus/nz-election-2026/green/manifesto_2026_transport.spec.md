# What the page states

> The source page extends Community Connect free fares to everyone under 18. A fossil-fuel import phase-out date is committed without a calendar date on the page.

System: Public transport and active travel

Scenario: The transport plan is put into effect
When the government puts the transport plan into effect
Then mass rapid transit is built in Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch with comprehensive bus lanes in all cities
And nationwide rapid rail connects regions for passengers and freight
And e-cargo bike subsidies and e-bike sharing at community hubs are introduced
And interregional rural buses and on-demand transport including by-Māori for-Māori services to kōhanga reo and rural schools are funded
And Community Connect provides free fares for students, apprentices, community service card holders, everyone under 18 and all Total Mobility card holders
And the Public Transport Operating Model is replaced to enable public not-for-profit transport
And safe walking and biking routes to community hubs are built with lower speeds near schools
And a phase-out date for fossil-fuel vehicle imports is implemented with more EV charging, car-sharing and EV incentives
Outcome communities rely less on private fossil-fuel vehicles
