# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Me whakangungu te mātauranga i te rangatahi mō tētahi ao uaua ake. Ka whakataurite ngā wāhi kōhungahunga ki ngā pokapū hapori, me ngā ture rahi me te ōwehenga kaha ake, me te ōrite utu mō ngā kaiako. Ka whakaako ngā kura i te mātauranga kirirarau mai i te Year 6, ka whakaheke i te wā mata i te akomanga, ā, ka tautoko ngā kura pō ā-hapori i te ako tūroa. Kei te tīmata tonu te kaupapa here taipitopito.

System: Future-fit education

Scenario: Ka panoni te ECE, ngā kura me te ako pakeke
When ka panonihia ngā tautuhinga mātauranga
Then ka whakaarahia te ECE ā-hapori ki te taumata o ngā pokapū kamupene
And ka herea te rahi pokapū, me ngā ōwehenga wāhi me te kaiwhakaako-ki-te-tamaiti kaha ake
And ka whiwhi te Playcentre, te Kōhanga Reo me ngā pokapū nā te hapori i ētahi tautoko anō
And he ōrite te utu o ngā kaiako ECE ki ngā kaiako kura tuatahi me te kura tuarua
And ka whakakotahitia te mātauranga kōhungahunga ki ngā tau tōmua o te kura tuatahi
And ka whakaakona te mātauranga kirirarau mai i te Year 6
And he whānui ngā tautoko ako mō ngā tamariki hauā
And ka utua ngā kaiako tohu mō ngā mahi tāpiri, ā, ka homai te whakawhanaketanga whai tohu me te ārahitanga
And ka akiakihia ngā kura kia whakaheke i te wā mata, kia whakamahi mata mō ngā whāinga motuhake anake
And ka utua ngā kura pō ā-hapori
Outcome he nui ake te wāhi ako tonu mō te rangatahi me ngā pakeke
