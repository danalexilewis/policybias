# National’s plan to get our country back on track

### In government, National's focus will be on creating and delivering opportunities for all Kiwis and their families to get ahead.

We will do this by rebuilding the economy to reduce the cost of living, restoring law and order and improving our schools and healthcare.

We will:

*   Encourage work, reward effort and let you keep more of what you earn with tax relief 
*   Cut red tape
*   Build infrastructure for growth like roads and public transport
*   Drive technology and innovation 
*   Support trade and investment
*   Grow skills and keep talent in New Zealand. 

A working economy means New Zealand can also afford the quality public services we all rely on.

**National is committed to delivering for you.**
