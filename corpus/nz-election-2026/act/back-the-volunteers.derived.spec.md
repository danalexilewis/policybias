# Our understanding

> The policy puts community groups first for DOC backcountry upkeep, allows multi-year contracts, and opens nature credits to accredited hunting groups. It states the hut, track, shortfall and closure figures. It does not define competitive funding, price the credit, or name the credit unit.

System: First-call upkeep funding and a nature credit without a unit

Scenario: Community groups get first opportunity for DOC upkeep funding
Given DOC is responsible for more than 950 huts and 15,000 kilometres of tracks
And DOC faces a 30 per cent maintenance shortfall
And DOC has issued more than 2,300 closure alerts since 2021
And hunting, tramping and conservation groups restore huts, maintain tracks and run trap lines at a fraction of the cost
And many groups survive on annual discretionary grants with no certainty of continued funding
When DOC is required to give community groups the first opportunity for funding when they offer a competitive option for backcountry upkeep
Then capable groups can deliver work DOC is not clearing alone
Outcome volunteer organisations may hold multi-year contracts instead of applying year by year
But groups that cannot meet an undefined competitive test stay on discretionary grants

Scenario: Accredited hunting groups earn nature credits
When the Voluntary Nature Credits Market is opened to accredited hunting groups for verified biodiversity results
Then a payment needs a measurable unit, a buyer, and a rule against selling the same gain twice
Outcome hunter-led trap lines and predator work are named as credit-eligible conservation
But until those rules exist, the market cannot pay

Scenario: Hunting bodies gain formal representation in predator-removal decisions
When formal representation is required for the New Zealand Deerstalkers Association and Game Animal Council in predator-removal decisions affecting hunters
Then people already doing trap-line work sit at the planning table from the start
Outcome decisions affecting hunters are made with those bodies in the room
But representation without a published decision rule can still leave hunters consulted after the real choice is settled
