# Our understanding

> Centre fees stop. Educator wages and places become a Crown cost. For-profit centres close or convert. Lunches widen. Families gain; the Crown and schools carry the standing bills.

System: Centre fees moving onto standing Crown bills

Scenario: Fees end and the wage bill lands on the Crown
When early childhood education is made free and provided as a public good
Then educator wages and places that parents used to fund become Crown cost
Outcome families stop paying centre fees
But the Crown takes on an open-ended bill for educator wages and places

Scenario: For-profit centres are stopped
When corporate for-profit centres are stopped
Then ownership and operation of those places must change
Outcome the market model the policy rejects is closed
But centre owners and families carry uncertainty until conversion or closure plans land

Scenario: Ka Ora, Ka Ako expands while curriculum resets
When Ka Ora, Ka Ako expands toward a universal warm nutritious locally made lunch
And curriculum and assessment reforms reset to the original Te Mātaiaho direction
Then lunch provision and curriculum change both run as standing system load
Outcome school food widens and curriculum direction reverts
But kitchens, suppliers and teachers carry extra load before any extra money arrives
