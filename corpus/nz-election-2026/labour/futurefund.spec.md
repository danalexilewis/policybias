# What the page states

> An independently governed Future Fund invests in infrastructure and innovative Kiwi businesses, seeded with Crown-owned assets and an initial capital contribution. It sits alongside and separate from the NZ Super Fund. Seeded assets are protected in law from sale.

System: New Zealand Future Fund

Scenario: Government establishes the Future Fund
Given Crown-owned assets with commercial and public good purpose exist today
When the New Zealand Future Fund is created
Then the Fund sits alongside and separate from the NZ Super Fund
Output seeded with existing Crown-owned assets plus an initial capital contribution
And independently governed by the Guardians of the Super Fund
Outcome seeded assets protected in law from sale
And Minister of Finance has no power to direct individual investments
