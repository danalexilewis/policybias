# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te piki te hara taiwhenua, ā, kāore ētahi kaipāmu e pūrongo ana i ngā mahi nā te iti o te whakawā. Ka pūtea te kaupapa here i ngā hāora patarō taiwhenua tāpiri, ka whakatū i tētahi rōpū hara taiwhenua motuhake, ka toro te Text 111 ki ngā wāhi kāore e pūmau te kapi waea pūkoro, ā, ka whakakaha ngā whiu mō te hopu kararehe me te tāhae kararehe. Kāore he tahua, he taipitopito whiu rānei i homai.

System: Te kaha ki te hara taiwhenua

Scenario: Ka urupare a Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa me ngā hapori taiwhenua ki te hara taiwhenua e tupu ana
Given i kitea e te Rural Crime Survey hou a Federated Farmers, 67% o ngā kaipāmu i pā te hara, he pikinga i te 41% i te tau 2016
And tata ki te haurua o ngā kaipāmu kāore e pūrongo i ngā mahi ki ngā pirihimana
And o te hunga e pūrongo ana, 15% anake ka kite i te tāngata hara e tirohia ana, e whakawākia ana
When ka pūteatia a Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa ki te utu āpiha anō mō ngā taiwhenua
Then ka whakatauhia he whāinga o ētahi 100,000 hāora patarō tāpiri ia tau
Output ka whakatūria tētahi rōpū hara taiwhenua motuhake
And ka toroa te āhei ki ngā ratonga Text 111 ki ngā tāngata i ngā taiwhenua kāore e pūmau te kapi waea pūkoro
Outcome ngā whiu nui ake me ngā hara kua whakakaha mō ngā kaipatu kararehe mau pū me te tāhae kararehe
And ngā taputapu kua whakakaha i waenga umanga mō te whakakotahi whakamana taiwhenua
And ka riro i ngā hapori taiwhenua te nui ake o te noho pirihimana, te hononga, me ngā aukati kaha ake
