# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka riro i te whakahou tangata me Te Pae Oranga he pūtea, ka whakawhānuihia ngā kōti mātanga, ka ngaro ngā papa here o te whiunga, ā, ka honoa te Steps to Freedom Grant ki te pikinga utu o ngā tau 30 rawa. Kāore he nama i te tatau o te whare herehere, i te paepae rangatahi hou, i ngā tāra takoha o tēnei rā.

System: Ngā kōti whakahoki me te awhe whiunga, kāore he huarahi herehere kua inehia

Outcome ka taea e ngā kaiwhakawā te whiu ki raro i ngā papa tawhito, ki runga ake i ngā paepae tawhito
But kei te whakahaere tonu ngā kaiwhakahaere whare herehere i ngā moenga ōrite kia huri tūturu ngā tauira whiunga

Outcome ka taea e te tini ake te kōwhiri i ngā huihuinga tikanga, mātanga rānei
