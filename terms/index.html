Policy Bias is free software that encodes published party policy. It is not a party, a campaign, or advice about how to vote.

The code is MIT. Party policy text, images, PDFs, and logos remain with those parties. We show them so you can tell whose policy it is. That does not give you those rights.

The cards are our reading of what the parties published. They can be wrong. Use the source on each card.

Playing the game is optional. A submitted score is public. Privacy says what that contains.
