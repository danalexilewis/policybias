# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ko ngā roa uru ki te GP ka waiho ngā kaiwhakarato rongoā kāore e tika te whakamahi, ahakoa ngā whakamātautau mate iti i angitu. Ka whakawhānui te kaupapa here i ngā mate ka taea e ngā kaiwhakarato rongoā te whakaora, ka āhei te aroturuki mate pūmau tūmau, ka whakamātautau tirotiro kiri, ka tango i ngā tepenga rangatiratanga whare rongoā, ā, ka āhei ngā raka tohutohu 24-hāora. Kāore he pūtea, he wātaka whakatinana rānei i homai.

System: Te mahi me te uru a ngā whare rongoā ā-hapori

Scenario: Ka rapu tētahi tūroro i te whakaora mō tētahi mate auau, mate pūmau tūmau rānei
Given e kī ana te kotahi o te whā o ngā pakeke ko te wā ki te whiwhi wānanga GP he ārai ki te kite i te GP
And nui ake i te kotahi mano ngā whare rongoā ā-hapori o Aotearoa, e whakakīia ana e ngā mātanga hauora tohu, kāore e tika te whakamahi i ō rātou pūkenga
And nui ake i te 120,000 tāngata i whakamahi i te Minor Health Conditions Service whakamātautau i te 2023, ā, 88 ōrau i tutuki ō rātou hiahia i te whare rongoā
When ka whakawhānuihia te mahi whakaora me te aroturuki a te kaiwhakarato rongoā, me ngā ture mārama mō te wā e hiahiatia ai te rata
Then ka taea e ngā whare rongoā te whakaora i ngā pokenga uma, pokenga taringa, pokenga kiri, mamae kaha, korokoro mamae, tonsillitis, school sores, styes, gout, osteoarthritis, vaginal thrush me te mamae marama, ā, te tuku tirotiro STI self-swab
Output ngā kaiwhakarato rongoā whakamanatia e aroturuki ana i te toto pēhanga tūmau, te matehuka momo 2 me te kōwaro teitei, ā, e whakarerekē ana i ngā rongoā i roto i ngā tepenga i whakatauria e te GP
And ngā kaiwhakarato rongoā e tono ana i ngā whakamātautau toto tika ina tonoa, ina hiahiatia rānei ā-haumanu, me ngā hua ki te pūkete hauora tiritiri
And tētahi whakamātautau tirotiro kiri i te whare rongoā mā te dermatoscope me tētahi taputapu aromatawai mōrearea AI kua whakaaetia, me ngā ara tuku
And te tango i te tepenga rangatiratanga e rima ngā whare rongoā me ngā herenga tata, me te whakaheke i ngā here raihana
And ngā raka tohutohu 24-hāora haumaru mō ngā rongoā e āhei ana
Outcome ka noho tonu ngā GP ki te kawenga haumanu whānui me te kite mō ngā mate pūmau e whakahaerehia ana e te kaiwhakarato rongoā
And ka kohikohi ngā tūroro i ngā rongoā i te wā e pai ana ki a rātou, kāore e tū ki ngā rārangi GP mō ngā mate ka taea e ngā kaiwhakarato rongoā te whakaora haumaru
