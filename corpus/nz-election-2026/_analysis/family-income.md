# Family income, welfare and savings

Reduces fourteen tagged dump pages into proposed events versus status-quo framing. Instruments kept separate: KiwiSaver (compulsory / birth seed / rates / PPL top-up / over-65 employer), PPL weeks and sharing, kids cash, welfare conditionality, Green benefit/PPL/super rules.

## national

### Given

Default KiwiSaver employee and employer contributions rise to 3.5% each on 1 April 2026 under the November 2025 glidepath National cites. Glidepath continues +0.5% from 2028 each year until employee and employer each reach 6% (combined 12%) on 1 April 2032. Today the Crown matches KiwiSaver only when a person on paid parental leave contributes themselves (~1 in 5 eligible did so in the year to June 2025). Employers need not contribute for employees aged 65+. Paid parental leave is 26 weeks. Under the Employment Leave Act, workers earn annual leave while on parental leave from 1 July 2027 and receive full holiday pay when they take that leave after return.

### When

**KiwiSaver — compulsory workforce.** Make KiwiSaver (or an equivalent scheme) compulsory for all workers from 1 July 2028 at the prevailing default rate on the glidepath to combined 12% by 1 April 2032 (`kiwisaver.md`).

**KiwiSaver — birth.** Auto-enrol every baby born in New Zealand into KiwiSaver from 1 July 2027 and pay a $1,500 Baby Boost Crown contribution (`kiwisaver.md`).

**KiwiSaver — PPL top-up.** From 1 July 2027, pay a KiwiSaver contribution for people on paid parental leave even when they do not contribute, at the default rate against their parental-leave payment (`kiwisaver.md`; also stated on `paid-parental-leave.md` — keep both pointers).

**KiwiSaver — over 65.** Require employer KiwiSaver contributions for contributing members aged over 65 from 1 July 2027, on the same basis as other employees (`kiwisaver.md`).

**PPL weeks.** Extend paid parental leave from 26 to 30 weeks over three Budgets, as a step toward a longer-term vision of 40 weeks / nine months (`paid-parental-leave.md`).

**PPL sharing.** Allow parents to take all or some of the combined paid parental leave entitlement at the same time or in overlapping instalments (`paid-parental-leave.md`).

**Holiday pay.** Protect the Employment Leave Act so parents earn annual leave on parental leave and receive full holiday pay when they take leave after returning (`paid-parental-leave.md`).

### Money

$1,500 Baby Boost per child born in New Zealand (`kiwisaver.md`). Illustrative compounding sketch: ~$5,000 by age 18 at ~7% with no further contributions — **sketch**, not a programme budget. PPL KiwiSaver top-up at default rate on leave payments — illustrative examples $625 then $952 for two maximum 26-week stints (`kiwisaver.md`; same rule on `paid-parental-leave.md` without those examples). Glidepath: employee and employer each 6% by 1 April 2032 (`kiwisaver.md`). Illustrative over-65 employer example: +$6,300 by retirement for a $70,000 earner working ages 65–68 (`kiwisaver.md`). PPL extension 26→30 weeks — weekly payment rate and per-Budget week increments unstated (`paid-parental-leave.md`).

## act

### Given

MSD already offers an optional designated-doctor pool for health and disability benefit certification. Electronic money management already exists as an option in certain circumstances.

### When

**Designated doctors.** Make designated-doctor certification mandatory for all health and disability benefits; assessments against clear objective criteria. Usual GP/specialist may still supply history and evidence. Reassess existing Jobseeker Support – Health Condition or Disability and Supported Living Payment recipients on a phased basis, starting with post-pandemic mental-health-related grants. Allow long-term arrangements for genuine enduring disability or health conditions (`welfare.md`).

**Payment card.** Introduce mandatory electronic money management for Jobseeker Support Work Ready recipients on a benefit for more than four months: payment card directed to essentials (groceries, rent, power, transport, health, childcare); block alcohol, gambling, tobacco, and cash withdrawals; carve-outs where safety or necessities would be blocked (`welfare.md`).

### Money

Unstated — no benefit-rate changes, card budgets, doctor-pool size, or reassessment schedule (`welfare.md`).

### Pointers

- `corpus/nz-election-2026/act/welfare.md` — https://www.act.org.nz/welfare

## nz-first

### Given

NZ First cites citizen births falling from 52,506 (2006) to 36,351 (2025) and non-citizen births rising from 8,001 to 14,380 over the same span; national birthrate 1.53 versus replacement 2.1 (`the_kiwi_kids_grant.md`). KiwiSaver enrolment is not compulsory at birth; workforce KiwiSaver is not compulsory for all workers.

### When

**Kids cash.** Introduce a universal, non-means-tested Kiwi Kids Grant: $5,000 per year tax-free for the first three years of each of the first three children, paid monthly through IRD, in addition to existing supports; option to take the first-year grant for the first child as an upfront lump sum. One parent must be a New Zealand citizen (`the_kiwi_kids_grant.md`).

**KiwiSaver — birth.** Compulsory KiwiSaver enrolment at birth with an immediate $1,000 Crown contribution for New Zealand citizens only (`establishing_a_kiwisaver_generation.md`).

**KiwiSaver — workforce.** Compulsory KiwiSaver enrolment for the wider workforce and increase employee and employer contributions to 8% initially and eventually to 10% — dump does not say whether 8%/10% is combined or per side, and does not date the transition (`establishing_a_kiwisaver_generation.md`).

### Money

Kiwi Kids Grant $5,000 per year per eligible child for three years; estimated programme cost $400 million by the third year once the full cycle is established (`the_kiwi_kids_grant.md`). Birth KiwiSaver Crown contribution $1,000 per New Zealand citizen (`establishing_a_kiwisaver_generation.md`). Workforce 8% then 10% — unstated whether combined or per party; timing unstated.

## green

### Given

Coalition framed as prioritising landlord/corporate tax cuts over liveable incomes (`manifesto_2026_income.md`). Pay-equity settings changed; Greens want claims resumed (`manifesto_2026_gender_equity.md`). Best Start exists but is not universal for every under-three. Paid parental leave is shorter than Greens propose. Overseas New Zealanders face a reduced residence period for NZ Super eligibility versus the prior 10-year rule Greens would restore (`manifesto_2026_overseas_new_zealanders.md`).

### When

**Welfare / benefits.** Reform the Social Security Act; high-trust Work and Income model; increase benefits to liveable rates; individualise entitlements; abolish benefit sanctions (`manifesto_2026_income.md`).

**ACC income.** Extend ACC to income support and treatment for injuries, disability, and illnesses regardless of origin (`manifesto_2026_income.md`).

**Working for Families.** Boost the rate, reduce abatements, extend tax credits to out-of-work families (`manifesto_2026_income.md`).

**Recoverable assistance / debt.** Grants for urgent needs instead of loans; work toward writing off MSD debt; ban attachment orders against beneficiaries (`manifesto_2026_income.md`).

**Best Start.** Increase Best Start and extend it to every child under three as a universal early-years child benefit (`manifesto_2026_childen.md`, `manifesto_2026_gender_equity.md`).

**Pay equity.** Undo Government pay-equity changes, resume claims, add pay transparency and pay-gap reporting (`manifesto_2026_gender_equity.md`).

**PPL.** Increase paid parental leave to 15 months for the primary caregiver, with additional ring-fenced partner leave, and increase payment rates (`manifesto_2026_gender_equity.md`).

**Super eligibility.** Restore the 10-year period of time spent in Aotearoa New Zealand for overseas New Zealanders to qualify for NZ Super (`manifesto_2026_overseas_new_zealanders.md`).

**Seniors income.** Long-term ageing strategy including equitable sufficient income for seniors — no NZ Super rate named (`manifesto_2026_seniors.md`).

Wealth-tax / progressive income-tax instruments: see `_analysis/tax.md` (one-line pointer only).

### Money

Liveable benefit rates, WFF boost, abatement cuts, Best Start increase amount, PPL payment uplift, and NZ Super rates — unstated. Named rules above stay in When with Money unstated (`manifesto_2026_income.md`, `manifesto_2026_childen.md`, `manifesto_2026_gender_equity.md`, `manifesto_2026_seniors.md`, `manifesto_2026_overseas_new_zealanders.md`).
