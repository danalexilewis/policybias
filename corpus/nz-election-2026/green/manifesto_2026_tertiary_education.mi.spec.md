# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka huri te pūtea mātauranga matua kia taea te utu i te ako, ā, kia mahi ngā whare mātauranga mō te painga tūmatanui. Ka kapi te moni ākonga mō te katoa i te ako paerua, ka utua ngā akoranga tūnga kia iti rawa ko te utu oranga, ā, ka hoki te mana ā-rohe ki ngā kuratini. Ka whakawhānuihia ngā mahi tāura kore utu, ka kōkiritia te mātauranga utu-kore me te mukunga nama ākonga, ā, ka hoki mai te mematanga taunoa ki ngā uniana ākonga. Kāore he wātaka.

System: Pūtea mātauranga matua me te tautoko ākonga

Scenario: Ka whakatinanatia te mahere mātauranga matua
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere mātauranga matua
Then ka tukua he moni ākonga mō te katoa, tae atu ki te ako paerua, ā, ka utua ngā akoranga tūnga kia iti rawa ko te utu oranga
And ka whakahoutia te pūtea mātauranga matua mō te rangahau, ngā akoranga, ngā ratonga ākonga, te utu kaimahi, me te uru a te hunga hauā
And ka whiwhi ngā kuratini i te pūtea e ranea ana me te mana ā-rohe, me te tiaki i ngā papa, ngā akoranga, me ngā kaimahi
And ka whakawhānuihia ngā mahi tāura kore utu, ka heke ngā utu nama, ā, ka kōkiritia te mātauranga matua utu-kore me te mukunga nama ākonga
And ka ū ngā whare katoa ki Te Tiriti, ka whakawhānuihia Māori and Pasifika Trades Training, ā, ka whai pūtea katoa ngā Whare Wānanga
And ka hoki mai te mematanga taunoa ki ngā uniana ākonga, me te mana whakahaere tiritiria mō ngā kaimahi, ngā ākonga, ngā hapori, me te tangata whenua
Outcome he iti ake te pīkaunga nama o te ako mātauranga matua
