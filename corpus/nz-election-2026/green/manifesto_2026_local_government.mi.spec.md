# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka kaha ake te manapori ā-rohe mā ngā pōtitanga e whakahaerehia ana e te Electoral Commission, te pōti i te 16, me te pōti huri kotahi (STV). Ka whakahokia ki ngā kaunihera ngā wāri Māori, ā, ka whakakapi ngā taputapu pūtea hou i te aukati reiti e marohitia ana. Ka noho ngā oranga e whā hei aronga matua, me ngā kawenga kua tukuna, me ngā tukanga e ārahi ana te hapori, pērā i ngā huihuinga kirirarau. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te manapori me te pūtea o te kāwanatanga ā-rohe

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere kāwanatanga ā-rohe
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere kāwanatanga ā-rohe
Then ka whakahaerehia ngā pōtitanga ā-rohe e te Electoral Commission, me te pōti i te 16, me te pōti huri kotahi
And ka mārama te tūranga hoa Tiriti o ngā kaunihera, ā, ka whakahokia ngā wāri Māori
And ka whakakorehia te aukati reiti e marohitia ana, ā, ka whakamanatia ngā taputapu pūtea hou pērā i ngā utu moenga, te utu kōpikopiko, me te tāke hokohoko (GST) mai i ngā reiti me te tipuranga
And ka noho ngā oranga e whā hei pūtake o te aronga o te kāwanatanga ā-rohe, me ētahi atu kawenga kua tukuna me ngā rauemi
And ka whakamōhio ngā huihuinga kirirarau me te nui ake o te kōrero a te hapori i te whakatikatika o te kāwanatanga ā-rohe
Outcome ka nui ake te kī a ngā hapori i ngā whakatau ā-rohe
