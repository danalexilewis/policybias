# What the page states

> More people than ever are displaced, while migration policy has become less secure for anyone already living, working or going to school in Sweden. The right to asylum is to be protected, quota refugees received, and young people who have put down roots are not to be deported. Permanent residence permits are to be the main rule and not to be withdrawn after the fact.

System: Right to asylum, a quota and a right to stay

Scenario: Quota, track-change and permanent permits are brought back
Given more people than ever are displaced by war, oppression, disasters and climate change
And migration policy has become less secure and less legally certain for people already living in Sweden
When the right to asylum is protected and legal routes to protection are opened
Then every person has their protection grounds examined individually
And young people who have grown up in Sweden are not to be deported when they turn 18
And particularly distressing circumstances are restored as a ground for a residence permit
And anyone who works and supports themselves can apply for a work permit without first leaving the country
And permanent residence permits become the main rule and cannot be withdrawn after the fact
Output 5,000 quota refugees per year
And 21 years of age for a residence permit as a dependant of parents
Outcome family reunification is strengthened so that more people can live together
And a wage at collective-agreement level is to be enough to stay
