# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka hanga te haumi waka i te waka tere nui ki ngā tāone nui, te rerewe tere puta noa i te motu, me ngā whatunga pahi me pahikara katoa. Ka whakawhānui a Community Connect i ngā utu-kore ki ngā ākonga, ngā kaimahi tāura, ngā kaipupuri community service card, te katoa i raro i te 18, me ngā kaipupuri Total Mobility card. Ka whakakapia te Public Transport Operating Model mō ngā ratonga tūmatanui kaua mō te hua, ā, ka whai rā whakamutu ngā kawemai waka hinu, me ētahi atu teihana utu EV me ngā whakatenatena. E here ana ki tētahi rā whakamutu kawemai hinu, engari kāore he rā maramataka i te whārangi. Kāore he nama tahua i homai.

System: Waka tūmatanui me te haere kori

Scenario: Ka whakatinanatia te mahere waka
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere waka
Then ka hangaia te waka tere nui ki Auckland, Wellington me Christchurch, me ngā ara pahi katoa ki ngā tāone katoa
And ka hono te rerewe tere puta noa i te motu i ngā rohe mō ngā kaihihi me ngā utanga
And ka whakaurua ngā tautoko pūtea pahikara uta hiko me te tiritiri pahikara hiko ki ngā pokapū hapori
And ka pūteatia ngā pahi taiwhenua ā-rohe me ngā waka i runga i te tono, tae atu ki ngā ratonga nā te Māori mō te Māori ki ngā kōhanga reo me ngā kura taiwhenua
And ka tukua e Community Connect ngā utu-kore mō ngā ākonga, ngā kaimahi tāura, ngā kaipupuri community service card, te katoa i raro i te 18, me ngā kaipupuri Total Mobility card katoa
And ka whakakapia te Public Transport Operating Model kia āhei ai te waka tūmatanui kaua mō te hua
And ka hangaia ngā huarahi hīkoi me pahikara haumaru ki ngā pokapū hapori, me ngā tere iti ake i te taha o ngā kura
And ka whakatinanatia he rā whakamutu mō ngā kawemai waka hinu, me ētahi atu teihana utu EV, tiritiri waka, me ngā whakatenatena EV
Outcome he iti ake te whakawhirinaki o ngā hapori ki ngā waka tūmataiti hinu
