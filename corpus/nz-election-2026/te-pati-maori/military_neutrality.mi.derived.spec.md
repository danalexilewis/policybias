# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Mā te whakapuaki i te noho taha-kore me te arotahi anō i Te Ope Kātua o Aotearoa ki te urupare aituā me te tautoko Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa ka huri te tū—engari kāore anō ngā āpiha e whai rārangi mō ēhea kawenata ka mutu, me te aha ka whakakapi i ngā mahi hononga. Me tae mai he whakamārama ā-ture o te noho taha-kore me te hononga kua rāhuitia i mua i te here o te whakapuakanga i tētahi.

System: Te noho taha-kore, kāore he rārangi putanga

Scenario: Ka whakapuakina te noho taha-kore
Given kaua te kaupapa here tāwāhi e whakahaua e ngā iwi kaha, ngā hononga, ngā pakanga rānei ehara nā tātou
When ka whakapuakina a Aotearoa he motu taha-kore ā-hoia
Then me kōwhiri anō he whakamārama ā-ture o te noho taha-kore me te hononga kua rāhuitia
Outcome ko te tū he tū motuhake i runga i ngā uara Māori me ngā hononga o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
But ki te kore tērā ture, he kī whakaaro noa te whakapuakanga

Scenario: Ka unumia ngā hononga, ka arotahi anō a Te Ope Kātua o Aotearoa
When ka arotakehia, ka unumia ngā hononga hoia e whakakore ana i te mana motuhake
And ka arotahi anō a Te Ope Kātua o Aotearoa ki te tiaki, urupare aituā, tautoko Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
Then me rārangi ēhea kawenata ka mutu, me te aha te hanganga ope ka whakakapi i ngā mahi hononga
Outcome ka arotahi a Te Ope Kātua o Aotearoa ki te tiaki kāinga me te tautoko Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
But kia tū rā anō te rārangi putanga me te hoahoa ope hou, ka pupuri ngā āpiha i ngā mahi tawhito me ngā mahi hou
