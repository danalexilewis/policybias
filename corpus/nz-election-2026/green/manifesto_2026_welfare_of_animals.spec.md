# What the page states

> Animal welfare gains an independent Commissioner and ministry with stronger farm codes from a resourced National Animal Welfare Advisory Committee. Harmful practices including factory farming, rodeo and farrowing crates phase out and imported products must meet local welfare standards. The live export ban at sea extends to air exports and wild-caught animals such as longfin eels. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Animal welfare law and standards

Scenario: The animal welfare plan is put into effect
When the government puts the animal welfare plan into effect
Then a Commissioner for Animals and independent ministry are created
And the National Animal Welfare Advisory Committee is resourced with stronger farmed-animal codes
And debeaking, intensive winter grazing, factory farming, octopus farming, farrowing crates and rodeo are phased out
And imported products must meet Aotearoa animal welfare standards including cosmetics
And enforceable standards apply to free range, cruelty free and vegan claims
And the ban on live exports by sea is retained and extended to air exports and wild-caught animals such as longfin eels
Outcome harmful farming and export practices end
