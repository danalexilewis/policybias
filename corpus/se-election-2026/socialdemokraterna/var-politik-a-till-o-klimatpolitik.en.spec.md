# What the page states

> Emissions are rising, and Sweden is to meet the climate targets to 2030 and reach net-zero emissions by 2045.
> Sweden fuel gives long procurement contracts and a stable base blend of biofuels to cut emissions and press prices.
> Klimatklivet and Industriklivet are to get stable conditions, permitting is to be made more efficient, and the EU is to have a binding emissions target to 2040.

System: Sweden fuel and climate leaps

Scenario: A base blend and an EU target are set while emissions are still rising
Given a trend of rising emissions and climate change as one of the greatest threats to society
When Sweden fuel is introduced and Klimatklivet is given stable conditions
Then blending of biofuels receives long procurement contracts, and permitting is made more efficient
Output 19,3 % base blend for diesel
And 10,0 % base blend for petrol
And at least a 95 percent cut in carbon dioxide emissions
Outcome a fair climate transition with support for electric-car leasing, charging infrastructure and compensation to sparsely populated areas and the countryside
