# Building the Future

## New Zealand's Next Billion Customers

### **National is committed to growing New Zealand's economy by helping Kiwi businesses sell more to the world.**
### Trade creates jobs, raises incomes, and brings more money into local communities. Whether it's horticulture, farming, manufacturing, technology, tourism, or services, New Zealand businesses succeed when they have access to more customers overseas. Trade currently supports one in four New Zealand jobs and plays a vital role in growing wages and creating opportunities across the country.
### To build on recent trade successes, National has announced a plan to connect New Zealand businesses with nearly one billion potential new customers around the world. The strategy focuses on expanding trade opportunities in Europe, South America, Asia, and Africa, opening new markets for New Zealand's high-quality products and services.
### **A re-elected National Government will**:

*   Pursue new trade agreements with fast-growing and high-value international markets.
*   Help New Zealand exporters access customers more easily and competitively.
*   Reduce unnecessary trade barriers and streamline border processes.
*   Strengthen the security of essential supply chains.
*   Support businesses with new digital tools to make exporting simpler.
*   Create more jobs, increase incomes, and strengthen local communities.  
      
    

### **Opening doors for Kiwi businesses**

The next phase of National's trade agenda focuses on growing opportunities in markets that together represent almost one billion potential customers. These markets are experiencing strong economic growth and increasing demand for the safe, high-quality food, products, services, and innovation that New Zealand is known for.

National will also continue supporting exporters by modernising trade processes, expanding digital trade systems, and reducing red tape so businesses can get their products to market faster and at lower cost.
