# What the page states

> Gang crime is a threat to the system. Society is to be hard on those who already commit serious crime, and early when children are at risk of being recruited. School, social services and cooperation within the EU are part of the same line.

System: An iron fist against the gangs and protection for children

Scenario: A criminal network continues with shootings and bombings
Given gang crime threatens people's safety and trust in society
And Sweden has for a long time had a very serious development of lethal violence linked to criminal networks
And volume discounts for offences have been abolished and the previously almost automatic release from prison sentences has been restricted
When sentences for serious and organised crime are tightened and the police get more effective tools
Then the possibilities for wiretapping and camera surveillance are strengthened
And the gangs' finances are choked
Outcome serious violence is met with forceful crime-fighting instead of short sentences

Scenario: A child without adult support approaches a gang
Given criminal networks recruit ever younger children
And gang leaders exploit children who often lack adult support and a belief in the future
When social services get more tools and school is strengthened as a protective factor
Then earlier interventions, more outreach work and stronger support for families are put in before the child ends up in crime
And more pupils reach an upper-secondary diploma
Outcome the child gets a path away from crime instead of a role in the network
