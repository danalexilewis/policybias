# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover prevention, mobile services, free care, maternity support, Hauora Māori, Pharmac, pharmacy, workforce, endometriosis and data. No dollar amounts appear.

System: Equitable and accessible health care

Scenario: The health plan is put into effect
When the government puts the health plan into effect
Then public health services focus on prevention and early screening for non-communicable diseases
And mobile health, mental health, diagnostics and dental vans serve rural communities and emergencies
And dental and primary care move toward publicly delivered fully free provision including by-Māori for-Māori clinics
And pā harakeke one-stop-shops support whānau through hapūtanga and early parenthood
And Hauora Māori funding arrangements are simplified working with Iwi Māori Partnership Boards
And sustained Pharmac funding increases for new medicines and devices
And the Minor Health Conditions Service expands and community pharmacy agreements are updated
And health workers receive fair wages, primary care nurse pay parity and safe staffing levels
And endometriosis guidelines and specialised clinics are funded
And health data and digital infrastructure funding increases with privacy regulation for private providers
Outcome health inequities for Māori and underserved communities are reduced
