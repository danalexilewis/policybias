# Our understanding

> Overcrowding is already blamed on longer sentences. A policy that rejects general tightening but still hardens selected crimes keeps feeding that stock, while welfare, border checks and a crash commission are asked to cut recruitment without a headcount.

System: Prevention beside a prison system the policy says is already full

Scenario: Selected sentences harden while remand wings stay over capacity
Given the Parliamentary Ombudsman has long criticised overcrowding
And prison terms have generally become longer
When harsher sentences still apply to violence, sex offences, crimes against children and weapons
Then those convicts still need cells
Outcome welfare and exit work must shrink inflow faster than the courts add nights
But a remand prisoner waits in a wing the policy already calls a crisis

Scenario: Exit programmes meet people the system did not hold well
Given cooperation between social services, prisons and care does not work
When the state takes a larger share of re-entry for serious offenders
Then housing, protection and care have to arrive together for the exit to stick
Outcome a person who wants to leave a gang still needs a protection programme that today is unnamed in size
But witnesses carry the risk if protection is thin
