# What the page states

> Sweden needs new nuclear power as baseload to push down electricity prices, strengthen industry and electrify. The target is fossil-free electricity, not only renewable. The state lends, shares risk and has gone in as majority owner at Ringhals so that reactors can be built.

System: State financing and ownership of new nuclear power

Scenario: A company wants to build new nuclear power
Given the energy target has been changed from 100 percent renewable to 100 percent fossil-free electricity production
And limits on more reactors and more sites have been abolished
When the firm seeks state support from the loan facility
Then the money is to be paid back when the plants are in place
And permitting processes are to be simpler
Outcome it is to be simple, attractive and profitable to establish nuclear power

Scenario: The state takes a place in the project at Ringhals
Given the state has become majority owner of Videberg Kraft, which is planning new reactors at Ringhals
And terms for loans, risk-sharing and long-term electricity-price contracts are agreed
And Blykalla and Studsvik AB have applied to take part in the loan facility
When the delivery phase takes over
Then ground can be broken for new reactors
And the electricity market is to reward stability, capacity and security of supply, not only kilowatt-hours produced
And electricity-grid expansion is to be sped up
Outcome electricity production is to be able to double by 2045 so that electrification and net zero become possible
