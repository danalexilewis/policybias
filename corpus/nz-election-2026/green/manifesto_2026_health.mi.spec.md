# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka neke te hauora tūmatanui ki te ārai mate me ngā ratonga nekeneke e tae ana ki ngā hapori taiwhenua me ngā ohorere. Ka neke te taurima niho me te taurima tuatahi ki te tuku tūmatanui kore utu katoa, me te tautoko hapūtanga tāwharau o te pā harakeke, te whakangāwari i te pūtea Hauora Māori, te haumitanga pūmau ki Te Pātaka Whaioranga, ngā ratonga whare rongoā kua whakawhānuihia, me te kaimahi haumaru me ngā utu tika. Ka pūteatia ngā aratohu endometriosis me ngā whare haumanu motuhake, me te kaha ake o te hanganga raraunga hauora me ngā ture tūmataiti mō ngā kaiwhakarato tūmataiti. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: He taurima hauora tōkeke, e taea te uru

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere hauora
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere hauora
Then ka arotahi ngā ratonga hauora tūmatanui ki te ārai mate me te tirotiro tōmua mō ngā mate kāore e pāpā
And ka mahi ngā waka hauora, hauora hinengaro, tautuhi mate me te niho nekeneke ki ngā hapori taiwhenua me ngā ohorere
And ka neke te taurima niho me te taurima tuatahi ki te tuku tūmatanui kore utu katoa, tae atu ki ngā whare haumanu nā te Māori, mō te Māori
And ka tautoko ngā ratonga kotahi o te pā harakeke i te whānau i te hapūtanga me te mātua tōmua
And ka whakangāwarihia ngā whakaritenga pūtea Hauora Māori, e mahi tahi ana me ngā Iwi Māori Partnership Boards
And ka piki pūmau te pūtea a Te Pātaka Whaioranga mō ngā rongoā me ngā pūrere hōu
And ka whakawhānuihia te Minor Health Conditions Service, ā, ka whakahoungia ngā whakaaetanga whare rongoā ā-hapori
And ka whiwhi ngā kaimahi hauora i ngā utu tika, te ōrite utu nēhi taurima tuatahi me ngā taumata kaimahi haumaru
And ka pūteatia ngā aratohu endometriosis me ngā whare haumanu motuhake
And ka piki te pūtea mō ngā raraunga hauora me te hanganga matihiko, me ngā waeture tūmataiti mō ngā kaiwhakarato tūmataiti
Outcome ka whakahekehia ngā koretake hauora mō te Māori me ngā hapori kāore e tika te taurima
