# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā ngā rōpū me ngā pūtea aratakina e te Māori e whakatipu te hinonga Māori. Ka tautokona te tauhokohoko, te auaha, me te whakatipu rawa Māori mō te pae tawhiti. Ka tiakina ngā raraunga me ngā mātauranga Māori mā te mana motuhake o ngā raraunga.

System: Te whakawhanaketanga ōhanga Māori me te mana motuhake o ngā raraunga

Scenario: Mā ngā rōpū me ngā pūtea aratakina e te Māori e whakatipu te hinonga Māori
Given e kore e taea e te tōnui Māori te whakawhirinaki ki ngā pūnaha i hangaia hei aukati i te Māori
When ka hangaia ngā rōpū me ngā pūtea aratakina e te Māori hei whakatipu i te hinonga Māori
Then ka tautokona te tauhokohoko, te auaha, me te whakatipu rawa Māori mō te pae tawhiti
And ka tiakina ngā raraunga me ngā mātauranga Māori mā te mana motuhake o ngā raraunga
Outcome ka tōnui te Māori ā-ōhanga i runga i ō rātou ake tikanga
