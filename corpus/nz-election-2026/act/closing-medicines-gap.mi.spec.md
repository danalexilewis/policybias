# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> He iti ake te wāhanga o te tahua hauora e whakapaua ana e Aotearoa ki ngā rongoā i tā Ahitereiria me te OECD. Kua aromatawai kē a Te Pātaka Whaioranga i te maha o ngā maimoatanga e ngaro ana he tika kia pūteatia, engari ka pau te tahua rongoā. Ka hiki te kaupapa here i te wāhanga rongoā o ngā whakapaunga hauora mā te kotahi ōrau ia tau kia ōrite ki a Ahitereiria i te 2033, mā te tipu e tūmanakohia ana o te tahua hauora, kaua mā ngā tāke hou, mā te tapahi rānei i te toenga o te hauora.

System: Te kati i te āputa pūtea rongoā me Te Pātaka Whaioranga

Scenario: Ka hiki te kāwanatanga i te wāhanga rongoā o ngā whakapaunga hauora ia tau
Given e 4.9 ōrau o tōna tahua hauora e whakapaua ana e Aotearoa ki ngā rongoā
And e 12.2 ōrau tā Ahitereiria, ā, ko te toharite OECD ko te 13.3 ōrau
And e pūtea ana a Ahitereiria i ngā rongoā hou 142 kāore e pūteatia ana i Aotearoa, 38 ōrau o ēnei he rongoā matepukupuku
And ka noho ngā rongoā i te rārangi Options for Investment a Te Pātaka Whaioranga kāore e wātea ina pau te tahua rongoā
And tata ki te $1.8 billion te tahua rongoā 2026/27
And e waru ōrau te toharite tipu o ngā whakapaunga hauora i ngā tau e rima kua hipa
And i whiwhi a Hauora i te $1.5 billion tāpiri i te Tahua 2026, ā, i whiwhi a Te Pātaka Whaioranga i te $13.5 million
And tata ki te $1.2 billion e toe ana mō te toenga o te pūnaha hauora i te tauira mahi o te Tahua 2026
When ka hikitia te wāhanga rongoā o te tahua hauora ia tau
Then ka eke a Te Pātaka Whaioranga ki te ōrite ki a Ahitereiria i te 2033
Output te pikinga 1 ōrau i te wāhanga rongoā o te tahua hauora ia tau
And tētahi āputa e whitu ōrau kua katia i roto i ngā tau e whitu
And te wāhanga rongoā 12% i te 2033/34
And tata ki te $5.4 billion–$6.1 billion te whakapaunga rongoā hei te 2033/34
And $500–650 million te whakapaunga rongoā tāpiri ia tau i te toharite
And tata ki te $320 million anō mō Te Pātaka Whaioranga mēnā i whai te pikinga Tahua 2026 i tēnei ture
And ngā pikinga pūtea anamata a Te Pātaka Whaioranga kua tohua i mua
And ka tango pūtea i te tipu e tūmanakohia ana o te tahua hauora, kāore he tāke tāpiri, he tapahi rānei i ngā whakapaunga hauora ehara i te rongoā
Outcome ka wātea ētahi atu rongoā i te rārangi Options for Investment a Te Pātaka Whaioranga
And ka tonoa ētahi atu rōpū kia ū ki taua ara tau e whitu kia kore e takahia e te huringa kāwanatanga
