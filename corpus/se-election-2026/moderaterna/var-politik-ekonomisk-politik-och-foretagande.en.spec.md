# What the page states

> Sweden should have the EU's highest standard of living within ten years. Those who work should keep more of their wages, companies should face lower tax and shorter permits, and work should always pay better than benefits.

System: At least half retained, lower corporate tax and simpler permits

Scenario: A household in ordinary occupations passes the threshold
Given Sweden is among the ten countries with the highest tax burden in the OECD
And 1.3 million people are not self-supporting
And those who work shifts or take extra shifts can pay more than 50 percent in tax on every hundred-krona above the state income threshold
When state income tax is changed so that more people in ordinary occupations retain at least half of their earned wages
Then corporate tax and employer contributions should be lowered so that more companies can grow and employ
And the Environmental Code should be reformed and a central environmental permitting authority should take over permits from county administrative boards and land and environment courts
Output at least 50 percent of earned wages retained above the state income threshold
Outcome work should pay better than benefits and more companies should be able to grow
