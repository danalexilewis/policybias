# What the page states

> Close to 80 decisions during the term of office are said to strengthen the energy supply. Combined heat and power, small- and large-scale hydropower and existing nuclear plants are to have good conditions, and a financial framework for new nuclear construction has been drawn up. Permits for the grid have been simplified and lead times almost halved. Policy is aimed at a fully fossil-free electricity system, with a security-of-supply target and a planning target of 300 TWh by 2045. The course is to be held there.
