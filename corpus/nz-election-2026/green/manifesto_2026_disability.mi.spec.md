# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka noho ngā tāngata whaikaha me ō rātou whānau me te mana mā te ture mōtika tāngata whaikaha e tū ana i Te Tiriti, me ngā paerewa uru e taea te whakamana. Ka tutuki te tautoko moni whiwhi i ngā utu noho me ngā utu hauora e pā ana ki te whaikaha, ka anga te whare noho ki te hoahoa whānui, ā, ka tū tonu te kaupapa taxi Total Mobility me te whakamahi māmā ake me te nui ake o ngā waka. Mā te huarahi Enabling Good Lives e ārahi ngā tari, ka kōkiri ngā mahere ohorere i ngā hiahia o ngā tāngata whaikaha, ā, ka whakakapi te whakatau tautoko i ngā aromatawai āheinga ā-ture.

System: Ngā mōtika tāngata whaikaha me ngā ratonga e taea te uru

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere tāngata whaikaha
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere tāngata whaikaha
Then ka whakaurua he ture mōtika tāngata whaikaha e tū ana i Te Tiriti, me ngā paerewa uru e taea te whakamana
And ka tutuki te tautoko moni whiwhi e māmā, e wawe ana i ngā utu noho me ngā utu hauora katoa e pā ana ki te whaikaha, ahakoa te tūnga hononga me te pūtake o te whaikaha
And ka tautoko ngā paerewa hoahoa whānui i te whare noho haumaru, e taea te uru, utu ngāwari hoki i roto i ngā hapori e kōwhiria ana
And ka tū tonu te kaupapa taxi Total Mobility me te whakamahi māmā ake me te nui ake o ngā waka nekeneke
And mā te huarahi Enabling Good Lives e ārahi ngā tari, ā, ka wewetea ngā aukati ki te hauora, te waka, te mātauranga me te tautoko tāngata whaikaha
And ka kōkiri ngā mahere urupare ohorere ā-motu, ā-rohe hoki i ngā hiahia o ngā tāngata whaikaha
And ka whakakapi te whakatau tautoko i ngā aromatawai āheinga ā-ture
Output 75% tautoko mō te kaupapa taxi Total Mobility
Outcome ka noho ngā tāngata whaikaha me ō rātou whānau me te mana me te rangatiratanga
