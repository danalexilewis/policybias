# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakamanahia i te ture ngā momo kua kawea mai e whai uara ana i raro i tētahi pou tarāwaho whakahaere kararehe mohoao kua whakaritea. Ka whakakahangia te Game Animal Council hei kaiwhakahaere ā-ture, ā, ka whakahoungia a Fish & Game mā te ture. Ka huakina te whenua Karauna e tika ana mō te hopu kararehe me te hī ika, me ngā ture kaweake taonga māmā ake, me te tiaki taiao whānui ake e ārahi ana ngā kaihopu.

System: Mahere mō te hopu kararehe me te hī ika

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere hopu kararehe me te hī ika
Given i ēnei rā ka taea te whakaaro ngā momo kua kawea mai e whai uara ana hei riha mā te taunoa
When ka whakatūria tētahi pou tarāwaho whakahaere kararehe mohoao kua whakaritea
Then ka whakamanahia ōkawa i te ture ngā momo kua kawea mai e whai uara ana
Output ka whakakahangia te Game Animal Council hei kaiwhakahaere ā-ture o ngā kararehe tākaro e whai uara ana
And ka whakahoungia a Fish & Game mā te ture
Outcome ka huakina te whenua Karauna e tika ana mō te hopu kararehe me te hī ika i raro i ngā ture whenua atawhai
