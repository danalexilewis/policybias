# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover adaptation funding, insurance, planning and Pacific support. No dollar amounts or dates appear.

System: Climate adaptation and resilience

Scenario: The climate adaptation plan is put into effect
When the government puts the climate adaptation plan into effect
Then climate adaptation planning affirms tino rangatiratanga and funds a Māori Climate Resilience Fund
And the Natural Hazards Commission expands to cover climate-related weather events and support community-led adaptation and retreat
And long-term nature-based solutions are prioritised with increased funding for hapū, iwi and community organisations
And planning frameworks give effect to climate adaptation in land and water use
And a dedicated fund is established for central and local government adaptation and resilience measures
And support for Pacific neighbours to adapt is scaled up with advocacy for an international agreement on climate displacement
Outcome hapū, iwi and community organisations lead their own climate response plans anchored by Te Tiriti o Waitangi
