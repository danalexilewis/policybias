# What the page states

> Ordinary people should not have to stay indoors because dangerous people move outdoors. Victims of crime are to receive redress and sentences are to match the seriousness. The police are to get more resources and better tools against gang violence, everyday crime and violence in the home.

System: Tougher sentences, more police and more prison places

Scenario: Shootings and explosions shape everyday life
Given Sweden had 391 shootings and 90 explosions during 2022
And the number of shootings has since fallen by 60 percent
And the appropriation to the Police Authority is estimated to rise by 40 percent between 2023 and 2027
And Sweden has 1 118 more officers in uniformed duty since 2022
When the police get stay-away orders, security zones, preventive coercive measures and more camera surveillance
Then municipalities are to get expanded tools against public-order offences and volume crime
And a new occupational category of security guards is to be able to complement police and order guards
Outcome crime is to be able to be stopped, investigated and cleared up across the country

Scenario: A victim of crime has to recover the damages themselves
Given 46 000 people have unpaid debts of over 10 billion kronor for criminal damages at the Enforcement Authority
And an offender was convicted in 2021 of 35 sexual offences including seven rapes and released after just over three years
And another offender received 840 000 kronor in damages, almost four times more than the victim
When the volume discount is abolished and the whole sentencing scale is to be used
Then the state is to pay out the damages and recover the money from the offender itself
And the right to counsel for the injured party is to be strengthened especially for older people and women exposed to violence
And confiscated criminal profits are to go to a confiscation fund for victims of crime and preventive work
Output around fifty sentence tightenings
And double sentences for gang criminals
Outcome focus moves from the perpetrator to the victim of crime and society's need for protection

Scenario: Women and children are exposed to violence in a close relationship
Given around 15 women are murdered each year by a current or former partner
And one in four women is at some time exposed to psychological or physical violence in a close relationship
And hundreds of thousands of young people live under honour-based oppression
When economic and psychological violence is criminalised and the offence of exceptionally gross rape is introduced
Then the maximum duration of a restraining order is to be extended and automatic restraining orders are to apply in more cases
And the patient fee is abolished for women who seek care after rape
And virginity checks, virginity certificates and virginity operations are criminalised
Outcome violence against women and children is to be fought with the same force as gang crime

Scenario: Prisons and remand centres run out when sentences become longer
Given the Prison and Probation Service's budget is to more than double from 14 to 31.5 billion kronor between 2023 and 2028
When more people are sentenced to longer prison terms
Then the number of prison and remand places is to have increased
Output 5 200 more prison and remand places
And 10 billion kronor over five years for the new Social Services Act
Outcome there are places to carry out the tougher sentences
