# What the page states

> Te Tiriti sits at the heart of constitutional partnership through Treaty Act reform and genuine decision-making across government. Treaty settlements reform removes Full and Final clauses and validates hapū rangatiratanga while Waitangi Tribunal resourcing increases with binding land return recommendations. Wrongfully alienated whenua returns including exploring private land first refusal at point of sale. No dollar amounts or dates are given.

System: Te Tiriti o Waitangi constitutional and settlement reform

Scenario: The Te Tiriti plan is put into effect
When the government puts the Te Tiriti plan into effect
Then the Treaty of Waitangi Act recognises Te Tiriti as the authoritative text working with He Whakaputanga
And Te Tiriti-based decision-making is implemented across government with iwi and hapū resourced as partners
And Treaty Settlements reform enables fair negotiations without Full and Final clauses, ends large natural grouping and validates hapū rangatiratanga
And Waitangi Tribunal resourcing increases with binding recommendations for return of Crown and private land
And whenua wrongfully alienated is returned including exploring right-of-first-refusal for private land at point of sale
Outcome hapū rangatiratanga is validated in settlements and land return
