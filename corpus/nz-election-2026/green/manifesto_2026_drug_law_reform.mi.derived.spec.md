# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakakapia e te kaupapa here te Misuse of Drugs Act ki tētahi huarahi hauora me te uru kānapa kua waeturehia, ka whakanuia te whakaheke mate, ka whakamana i te raihana psychedelic, ā, ka heke āta ngā pānui waipiro me ngā kōkiri e utua ana e te reeti. Kāore te tauira kānapa, ngā tapeke pūtea me ngā rā heke āta i tāpirihia.
