# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā tētahi pōti motuhake i muri i te pōtitanga e tū ake e tuku te iwi kia whakatau mehemea e whai hua, e hāngai, e hiahiatia ana tonu ngā tūru Māori o te Pāremata. E tohe ana kua iti ake te hiahia ki ngā tūru motuhake nā te MMP, inā nui ake te māngai Māori i te Whare i te wāhanga e kīia ana mō te taupori. Kāore he kupu pātai, he rā, he tūnga here rānei i homai.

System: Pōti motuhake mō ngā tūru Māori o te Pāremata

Scenario: Ka whakahaeretia he pōti motuhake mō ngā tūru Māori
Given e rua tekau mā whitu ōrau o te Whare he takenga Māori i raro i te MMP
And e kīia ana te Māori he tekau mā whitu ōrau o te taupori
And kua heke pūmau te rēhita ki te rārangi Māori ki te haurua o ngā Māori katoa kua rēhitatia
When ka whakahaeretia he pōti motuhake mō te anamata o ngā tūru Māori i muri i te pōtitanga e tū ake
Then ka whakatau te iwi mehemea e whai hua, e hāngai, e hiahiatia ana tonu ngā tūru Māori
Outcome tērā pea ka puritia ngā tūru Māori mēnā he nui te hunga e tautoko ana
