# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka utua e te kaupapa here tētahi nekehanga whakahou ki ngā pūtea tārewa me ngā takuhe, ka aukatia te palm kernel expeller, ka herea te tapahi-katoa, ka hangaia te tukatuka rākau ā-rohe me te ngahere e ārahi ana a Māori, ā, ka tukuna ngā whiwhinga kanorau koiora hei kōwhiringa ETS i runga i te hiahia. Kāore he rahi tārewa, he waeine whiwhinga, he paepae tapahi-katoa rānei i tāpirihia.

System: Te nekehanga whakahou, kāore he whiwhinga kua utua

Scenario: Ka tango ngā kaipāmu i ngā pūtea tārewa me ngā takuhe whakawhiti
Given te pāmu kaha e whakamāmā ana i te hua poto, i te wā e uaua ana ngā pakihi ā-whānau
When ka tautoko ngā pūtea tārewa iti-hua me ngā takuhe i ngā kaipāmu e neke ana ki te whakaputa toitū ake
Then kei reira te pūtea hei huri tikanga
Outcome ka taea te whānui o te whakaputa whakahou ki reira e rere ana te pūtea
But ka tatari tonu ngā kaipāmu ki ngā rahi tārewa me ngā takuhe i mua i tā rātou whakamahere i te neke

Scenario: Ka utua tētahi kaipupuri whenua mā ngā whiwhinga kanorau koiora
When ka tuku tētahi kaupapa whiwhinga kanorau koiora i te moni whiwhi i runga i te hiahia hei kōwhiringa ki te Emissions Trading Scheme, e whakamāmā ana i te whenua Māori taha-mōrearea ki reira e whakaaetia ana
Then ka taea e te whakaora me te tiaki te whiwhi moni, ehara i te ngahere ETS anake
Outcome kei ngā kaipupuri whenua e whakaora whenua ana tētahi huarahi utu kua whakaingoatia
But kia tū rā anō tētahi waeine, rēhita me te kaihoko, kāore e taea e te kaupapa te utu

Scenario: Ka aukatia te palm kernel expeller
Given te pāmu miraka ahumahi te kaipara āhuarangi nui rawa e poke ana i te wai inu ki te pāhūpāhū
When ka aukatia te palm kernel expeller, ā, me tiki toitū te kai tāpiri
Then ka wehe tētahi rawa kai kawemai nui i te pūnaha kaha
Outcome ka mutu te pēhanga kawe i taua kai
But me kai tonu ngā kāhui, nā reira ka neke te utu ki tērā whakakapi e whakakapi ana
