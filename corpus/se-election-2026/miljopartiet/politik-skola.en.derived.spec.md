# Our understanding

> Group-size law and a company ban are the two hard edges. The state is asked to carry more of the school bill, but the extra adults must still be hired locally. Choice remains; corporate form does not.

System: Rights and a company fence

Scenario: Universal preschool hours are extended
When universal preschool moves toward a longer week
Then more children are in publicly paid hours earlier
Outcome municipalities staff longer days in preschool
But the extra hours are a standing cost without a published price

Scenario: Company schools are closed as a form
When companies are banned from running schools and municipalities may refuse new units
Then public money should stay in teaching, support and student health
Outcome existing company schools must change form or leave
But how that conversion is paid, timed or staffed is not set
