### What we’ll do

*   Strengthen te reo Māori and normalise it everywhere.
*   Invest in te reo Māori learning pathways and community-led language revitalisation.
*   Make sure the state is accountable for protecting te reo Māori.

### Why it matters

Te reo Māori is not just a language. It is identity, memory, authority, and the sound of our survival. When te reo thrives, our people stand taller and our nation becomes more honest about who it is.
