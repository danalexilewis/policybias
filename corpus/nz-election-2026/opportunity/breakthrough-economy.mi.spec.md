# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te pōturi haere te tipu nā te mea kāore ngā tautuhinga ōhanga i panoni mō ngā tekau tau. Ka tika te pūtea mō te pūtaiao me te auahatanga, ka whiwhi te Commerce Commission i te mana High Court hei wehewehe i ngā duopoly, ā, ka tautokona ngā pakihi iti ki te āwhina tahua, te tika o Te Kaporeihana Āwhina Hunga Whara me ngā utu hokohoko, me tētahi hanganga Impact Company hou.

System: Breakthrough Economy

Scenario: Ka panoni ngā tautuhinga pūtaiao, whakataetae me ngā pakihi iti
Given kei te utua e ngā hokomaha me ētahi atu rāngai kāore e whakataetae te $30 ia wiki mō te whānau toharite
When ka whakaarahia te whakapaunga rangahau me te whanaketanga
And ka whakaurua ngā tāke whakaiti haumi hangarau hou, ā, ka ruarua te pūtea whakatere kua whakamātauria
And ka murua te huamoni nama ākonga mō ngā Kiwi e hoki mai ana
And ka noho ngā polytech hei pūtahi auahatanga ā-rohe
And ka whakarewaina he rautaki taupori ā-rōpū
And ka homai ki te Commerce Commission te mana ture me te pūtea hei wehewehe i ngā duopoly me ētahi atu mākete kāore e whakataetae mā te High Court
And ka utua ngā ratonga tahua pakihi iti, ka tere ake te uru ki te tāke whakaiti rangahau me te whanaketanga, ā, ka tika Te Kaporeihana Āwhina Hunga Whara me te pūnaha utu hokohoko
And ka waihangatia he hanganga ture Impact Company mō ngā pakihi e whai hua ana i te mahi pai pāpori me te taiao
Then ka tūturu te morearea wehewehe mō ngā hokomaha me ētahi atu rāngai kāore e whakataetae
Output te whakapaunga rangahau me te whanaketanga 2% i te iti rawa o te GDP
And $12 ki te $31 ia wiki e tiakina ana mō ngā whānau Kiwi nā te whakataetae nui ake i ngā hokomaha, ngā pēke me ētahi atu rāngai
Outcome he nui ake te wāhi tipu mō ngā kaipūtaiao, ngā paetahi e hoki mai ana, me ngā pakihi iti
