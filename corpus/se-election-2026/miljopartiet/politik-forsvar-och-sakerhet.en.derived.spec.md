# Our understanding

> Rearmament is supposed to be paid at the top of the income scale so hospitals and schools are not the residual. NATO is accepted, not reversed. The nuclear ban and the mine ban are legal fences inside that membership. Ukraine support is open-ended in time and unpriced in kronor.

System: Funded total defence inside a fenced alliance

Scenario: Welfare is ring-fenced by a top-end preparedness tax
When a preparedness tax on the highest incomes is levied to pay for rearmament
Then the military and civil build is meant not to raid the welfare budget
Outcome hospitals, rail and food stocks can in principle rise together
But if the tax does not raise enough, the crowding-out the policy wants to avoid returns through the ordinary budget

Scenario: Nuclear weapons are banned on Swedish soil while NATO membership stands
When a law forbids nuclear weapons on Swedish territory in peace and in war
Then no allied warhead is meant to be based or parked here
Outcome deterrence for the alliance still rests on nuclear weapons held elsewhere
But transit, consultation and wartime basing are alliance practices the statute would have to override
