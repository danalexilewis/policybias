# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka mārama ake te tautoko ki ngā hapori kia whakarite mō ngā tūpuhi, ngā waipuke me ngā tauraki e kaha haere ana. Mā te whakamahere urutaunga e whakapūmau te tino rangatiratanga, e pūtea tētahi Māori Climate Resilience Fund, ā, e whānui ake te kapi a te Natural Hazards Commission mō ngā pāngia āhuarangi, tae atu ki te hūnuku nā te hapori e ārahi ana. Mā ngā rongoā ā-taiao, me te pūtea motuhake a te kāwanatanga matua me te kāwanatanga ā-rohe, e tautoko ngā mahere nā te hapū, te iwi me te hapori e ārahi ana, me te tautoko urutaunga nui ake ki Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te urutaunga me te manawaroa āhuarangi

Scenario: Ka whakamanahia te mahere urutaunga āhuarangi
When ka whakamanahia e te kāwanatanga te mahere urutaunga āhuarangi
Then ka whakapūmau te whakamahere urutaunga āhuarangi i te tino rangatiratanga, ā, ka pūteatia tētahi Māori Climate Resilience Fund
And ka whānui ake te Natural Hazards Commission ki te kapi i ngā pāngia huarere āhuarangi, me te tautoko i te urutaunga me te hūnuku nā te hapori e ārahi ana
And ka whakamua ngā rongoā ā-taiao mohoa roa, me te piki pūtea mō ngā hapū, ngā iwi me ngā rōpū hapori
And ka whakamana ngā pou tarāwaho whakamahere i te urutaunga āhuarangi i te whakamahi whenua me te wai
And ka whakatūria he pūtea motuhake mō ngā mahi urutaunga me te manawaroa a te kāwanatanga matua me te kāwanatanga ā-rohe
And ka nui ake te tautoko ki ngā hoa o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa kia urutau, me te tautoko i tētahi whakaaetanga ā-ao mō te nekehanga āhuarangi
Outcome ka ārahi ngā hapū, ngā iwi me ngā rōpū hapori i ō rātou ake mahere urupare āhuarangi, e ū ana ki Te Tiriti o Waitangi
