# Our understanding

> The package gathers allowances, an abolished qualifying day and cheaper medicine so that most households can count a plus. The claim that almost everyone gains requires a counterpart: those who do not gain, or the institution that is to cut its take. An activity duty and a jobs ladder can increase the supply of hours worked, but they do not create demand. The rural lift and the construction credits are separate commitments without amounts.

System: A visible household plus against an unnamed counterpart

Scenario: A household counts the wallet pledge
Given raised child and study allowances, cheaper medicines and free dental care for the young
When the package is introduced
Then everyday outgoings can fall
Outcome most households can see a plus
But the tenth who does not gain is not pointed out

Scenario: An assistant nurse is spared the qualifying day
Given a qualifying day that today takes several thousand kronor a year from those who cannot work from home
When the qualifying day is abolished
Then a sick day no longer cuts pay in the same way
Outcome the pension is less affected by short sickness absence
But someone else pays for the day

Scenario: The unemployed person meets an activity duty
Given mass unemployment and a requirement to take the jobs that exist
When the jobs ladder and the activity duty are introduced
Then the benefit is made conditional on activity
Outcome fewer can stay in exclusion without requirements
But the duty does not fill a workplace that has not been advertised
