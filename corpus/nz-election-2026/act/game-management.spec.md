# What the page states

> The transfer from DOC to the Game Animal Council, full funding commitment, legislative recognition of game species, and Herds of Special Interest starting with Stewart Island whitetail are all on the source page. No dollar figures or implementation dates appear.

System: Hunter-led game management on conservation land

Scenario: Game animal management moves from DOC to the Game Animal Council
Given DOC manages game animals on conservation land under legislation that starts from controlling or exterminating them
And the Game Animal Council can advise while DOC keeps authority
When responsibility for managing game animals on conservation land is transferred to the Game Animal Council
Then the Council is fully funded to carry out its current and proposed responsibilities
Output game-animal and game-bird species recognised in relevant legislation as valued natural resources to be sustainably managed
And New Zealand's most valued game herds designated as Herds of Special Interest, beginning with Stewart Island whitetail deer
Outcome game animals are managed sustainably within conservation limits
And hunters with knowledge of herds and country make game-management decisions instead of advisory-only consultation
