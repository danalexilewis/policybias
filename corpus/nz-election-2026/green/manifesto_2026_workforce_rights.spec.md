# What the page states

> Employment law shifts toward collective voice with default union membership and expanded strike rights including political and solidarity action. A right to disconnect protects out-of-hours contact while minimum wage rises at least match inflation and move toward the living wage. Paid parental leave increases to 15 months for the primary caregiver with ring-fenced partner leave and increased payments. Parental leave payment rates are not stated. No timetable is given.

System: Workforce rights and pay

Scenario: The workforce rights plan is put into effect
When the government puts the workforce rights plan into effect
Then employment law defaults to union membership with opt-out
And the right to strike expands including for collective agreement breaches and political and solidarity reasons
And a right to disconnect protects workers from out-of-hours contact and surveillance
And workers' representatives on boards of listed companies are explored
And annual minimum wage increases at least keep up with inflation, publicly funded roles pay a living wage and the minimum wage moves toward the living wage
And paid parental leave increases to 15 months for the primary caregiver with ring-fenced partner leave and increased payments
And the contractor versus employee test strengthens for gig and platform workers
And gender and ethnic pay gaps are closed through pay equity, transparency and reporting
Outcome employment law covers time, pay and organising rights
