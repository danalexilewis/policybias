# What the page states

> The two new infringement offences for line-of-fire obstruction and aircraft harassment of game-bird shooters are all on the source page. No fine amounts, penalties or implementation dates appear.

System: Protect lawful hunting from deliberate interference

Scenario: Someone deliberately obstructs a lawful hunter or game-bird shoot
Given hunters acting safely and within the law can be deliberately obstructed by people who step into the line of fire with no specific infringement offence
And drones or helicopters can be used to harass game-bird shooters by scattering or flushing birds with little legal remedy
When specific infringement offences are created for these behaviours
Then deliberately standing in the line of fire of a lawful hunter is an infringement offence
Output an infringement offence for using drones or helicopters to harass game-bird shooters
Outcome people remain free to oppose hunting but not to interfere in ways that put others at risk
And hunters and shooters expected to follow strict safety rules are not undermined by deliberate obstruction
