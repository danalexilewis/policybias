# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka taea e ngā whare wānanga te rāhui karahipi, nohoanga me ētahi atu painga e pūteatia ana e te kaipupuri tāke i runga i te iwi, ahakoa te tohutohu 2024 ki ētahi atu tari tūmatanui. Mā te Equal Treatment Amendment Bill e aukati te tohatoha painga mātauranga matua tūmatanui i runga i te iwi, me te pupuri i te pūkenga, te hiahia, te hauātanga, me te whāinga kaituku tūmataiti. Kāore he nama, he wātaka rānei i te whārangi.

System: Taurite te atawhai i ngā whare mātauranga matua e pūteatia ana e te kaipupuri tāke

Scenario: Ka whakamanahia e te Pāremata te Education and Training (Equal Treatment) Amendment Bill
Given ka taea ināianei e ngā whare wānanga te rāhui karahipi, nohoanga, wāhi ako me ētahi atu āheinga i runga i te iwi
And i te tau 2024 i tohutohu te Kāwanatanga i ngā tari tūmatanui ki te tohatoha tautoko i runga i te hiahia me te uara, kaua i te iwi, engari kāore i kapi ngā whare mātauranga matua
When ka whakatikatikahia te Education and Training Act kia kore e taea e ngā whare mātauranga matua te tuku painga i runga i te iwi, te takenga iwi rānei
Then ka pā te ture ki ngā karahipi, ngā takoha, te āwhina pūtea, te nohoanga, ngā wāhi kua tohua, me ētahi atu painga, āheinga rānei
Outcome ka taea tonu e ngā whare wānanga te tohatoha tautoko i runga i te pūkenga mātauranga, te hiahia mātauranga, te wāhi whenua, ngā āhuatanga ōhanga, te hauātanga rānei
And ka noho wehe ngā pūtea tuku, ngā waihotanga me ētahi atu āwhina a ngā tāngata tūmataiti, iwi, amorangi, tarahiti rānei mō tētahi kaupapa kua tohua
