# Our understanding

> Group-size law and a company ban are the two hard edges. The state is asked to carry more of the school bill, but the extra adults still have to be hired locally. Choice remains; the corporate form does not.

System: Entitlements and a company fence

Scenario: Universal preschool hours expand
When general preschool moves toward a longer week
Then more children are in publicly paid hours earlier
Outcome municipalities staff longer days in preschool
But the extra hours are a standing cost with no published price

Scenario: Company schools are closed as a form
When companies are barred from running schools and municipalities may refuse new sites
Then tax money is meant to stay in teaching, support and pupil health
Outcome existing company schools must change form or exit
But how that conversion is paid, timed or staffed is not set
