# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka rite te mahi a ngā whāinga āhuarangi o nāianei i te mewaro ki te waro kōhuke, ā, ka pana te whenua pāmu ki te ngahere ahakoa te mahi miraka kau whaihua. Ka tuku te kaupapa here i tētahi NDC wehe-hā i te wā Pāremata e tū mai nei, ka tautuhi anō ngā Mahere Whakaheke Tukunga ki ngā whāinga pono, ka noho te ahuwhenua ki waho o te ETS, ā, ka whakahaerehia te mewaro koiora i raro i te huarahi No Additional Warming. Tērā pea ka wehe a Aotearoa i te Paris Agreement mēnā kāore e taea e ngā whāinga te whakaata i tōna āhua tukunga. Kāore he whāinga NDC ā-nama e puta.

System: Kaupapa here āhuarangi Paris Agreement mō ngā tukunga a Aotearoa

Scenario: Ka tuku a Aotearoa i tētahi Nationally Determined Contribution wehe-hā
Given he 46% te iti ake o te tapuwae waro o te miraka kau o Aotearoa i te toharite o te ao
And ka rite te mahi a ngā whāinga āhuarangi o nāianei i te mewaro nō ngā kararehe pāmu ki te waro nō ngā kora kōhuke
And ka hurihia te whenua pāmu pai ki te rākau paina i raro i te huarahi o nāianei
When ka tuku te kāwanatanga hou i tana ake Nationally Determined Contribution i te wā Pāremata e tū mai nei
Then ka rerekē te mahi i ngā hā roa-ora pērā i te hauhā me ngā hā poto-ora pērā i te mewaro i roto i te NDC
Output ka tautuhi anō ngā Mahere Whakaheke Tukunga ki ngā whāinga pono, te huarahi wehe-hā, me ngā hua taiao tūturu
And ka noho pū te ahuwhenua ki waho o te Emissions Trading Scheme
Outcome ka whakahaerehia te mewaro koiora i raro i te huarahi No Additional Warming, ā, ka haere tonu ngā hā roa-ora i te ara whakaheke tukunga
And tērā pea ka wehe a Aotearoa i te Paris Agreement mēnā kāore e taea te whakauru i ngā whāinga e whakaata ana i te pānga whakamahana tūturu o ngā tukunga a Aotearoa
