# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka hōhonu ake te pātuitanga o te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa mā te haere visa-kore, te whakahoki i te āwhina āhuarangi, me te mahi kaupeka tōkeke hei whakakapi i te Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme. Ka pūteatia te ārahitanga āhuarangi a te tangata moana i Aotearoa, ā, ka tautokona te mana motuhake o West Papua me French-occupied Polynesia. Ka piki te pūtea tiaki moana mō te South Pacific Regional Environment Programme me ngā wāhi rāhui o te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa i raro i te High Seas Treaty. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Te kaupapa here mō Te Manatū mō ngā Iwi ō te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa me te kotahitanga ā-rohe

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere mō ngā iwi ō te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere mō ngā iwi ō te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa
Then ka whakaroaina te haere visa-kore ki Aotearoa ki ngā manuhiri nō ngā motu o te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa
And ka whakahokia, ka whakanuia te pūtea āwhina āhuarangi ā-ao, me te tautoko i ngā whiriwhiringa āhuarangi o te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa
And ka pūteatia te ārahitanga āhuarangi a te tangata moana i Aotearoa
And ka whakakapia te Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme ki ngā utu tōkeke, te whare noho, ngā tika mahi tuwhera me ngā huarahi noho tūturu
And ka tautokona te mana motuhake o West Papua me French-occupied Polynesia, me tētahi uiui tika tangata e ārahitia ana e te iwi taketake
And ka nui ake te pūtea tautoko mō te South Pacific Regional Environment Programme me ngā wāhi rāhui moana o te Moana-nui-ā-Kiwa i raro i te High Seas Treaty
Outcome kāore ngā kaimahi kaupeka e herea ki tētahi kaituku mahi anake
