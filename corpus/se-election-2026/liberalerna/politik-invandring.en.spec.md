# What the page states

> The right to asylum is to be protected. It is the individual's protection grounds that decide who may stay. Those without grounds are to return. The EU needs a common asylum policy, more legal routes and stronger border protection. Labour immigration is to be simple for firms that need key skills.

System: Protection for those who flee and return for those who are refused

Scenario: An asylum seeker waits for a decision
Given millions of people are displaced around the world
And processing times for asylum decisions are too long
When the right to asylum is protected and protection grounds are examined for every person
Then those fleeing war and persecution can receive protection
And some people without asylum grounds may still stay because of compassionate circumstances
And those without protection grounds leave the country
And cooperation between the police and the Swedish Migration Agency increases
Outcome the focus is on those who need protection most
And parents who have fled can be reunited with their children

Scenario: A firm needs key skills from a country outside the EU
Given labour immigration is good for Sweden
When it becomes simple for firms to hire people from other countries
Then it becomes attractive for people with key skills to choose Sweden
Outcome legal routes in complement the asylum examination
And the EU's common system for border protection and coastguard is strengthened
