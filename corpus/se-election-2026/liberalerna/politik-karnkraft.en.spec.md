# What the page states

> Energy must always be there when, and where, people need it. Nuclear power is dispatchable, fossil-free and can be expanded at scale. The ban on new nuclear power has been abolished. More than ten reactors may be in operation at the same time, including at sites other than Forsmark, Ringhals and Oskarshamn. The next reactor will probably be smaller.

System: Dispatchable nuclear power in the electricity system

Scenario: Electricity is to suffice when the wind drops
Given we must be able to be sure that electricity, heat and the supply of fuels work regardless of weather and wind
And nuclear power's strength is that it delivers fossil-free energy when we need it and where we need it
When new nuclear power is expanded and smaller reactors take a place in the electricity system
Then nuclear power contributes strongly to stability in the electricity system
And weather-dependent electricity production can be absorbed
And small reactors can produce process heat, district heating or hydrogen
And the universities develop nuclear technology and education in the field
Outcome fossil-free nuclear electricity plays a central role in the future Swedish energy mix
And electrification gets increased electricity production as fossil fuels are phased out
