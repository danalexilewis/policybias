# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> He nui ake ngā āpiha haumaru i ngā pirihimana, engari kāore ō rātou mana nui ake i tō te kirirarau noa, ā, he piriona te utu o te hara hokohoko. Ka whakauru te kaupapa here i ngā raihana Accredited Security Operator me ngā mana i runga whenua tūmataiti, tae atu ki ngā tono ingoa me te wāhi noho, ngā pānui aukati whenua-maha, te tango, te kaha whāiti, me ngā rapunga ā-whakaae. Kāore he utu raihana, he wātaka whakatinana rānei i homai.

System: Accredited Security Operators mō te haumaru tūmatanui me te pakihi

Scenario: Ka urupare tētahi Accredited Security Operator raihana ki te ngangau i runga whenua tūmataiti
Given e rua ngā āpiha haumaru raihana o Aotearoa mō ia āpiha pirihimana, engari kāore ō rātou mana nui ake i tō tētahi atu kirirarau i tēnei wā
And kāore e taea e Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa te urupare tere ki ia waea
And he $2.6 piriona te utu o te hara hokohoko ki te rāngai ia tau e ai ki te Pūrongo Hara a Retail NZ
When ka whakaurua he raihana hou e taea e ngā ngaio haumaru te whiwhi mā te whakangungu
Then ka āhei ngā Accredited Security Operators raihana ki te tono i te ingoa me te wāhi noho o tētahi tangata i runga whenua tūmataiti, ā, ki te pupuri i a ia kia tae mai a Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa mēnā ka whakahē, ā, kāore e wehe
Output ngā pānui aukati ā-whanonga e taea te whakamana mō te toru tau te roa ki ngā whenua maha, e mau ana i tētahi rēhita i raro i te Privacy Act
And te tango i ngā tāngata e whakatuma ana i te haumaru, e whakakino ana, e takahi ana rānei i ngā tikanga tomo o te wāhi
And te kaha tika, ōrite hoki hei huarahi whakamutunga, ka mutu inā ū te tangata, inā tae mai rānei a Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa
And ngā rapunga haumaru ā-whakaae i runga whenua, ā, ko te whakahē i te rapu te tikanga kāore e tomo
Outcome ka taea e ngā kaihokohoko, ngā rōpū pakihi, ngā rārangi toa me ngā kaiwhakahaere waka te whakamahi i ngā kaiwhakahaere me ngā mana tāpiri
And kei ngā whenua tūmataiti ngā kaiwhakahaere kua whakangungua me ngā mana tāpiri kia tae mai a Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa
