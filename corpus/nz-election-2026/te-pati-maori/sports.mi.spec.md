# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakangaohia ngā kaupapa hākinakina me ngā kaupapa hapori e whakatipu ana i te whanaungatanga, te oranga me te āheinga. Ka tautokona ngā ara mō ngā rangatahi mā ngā karapu, ngā hōtaka me ngā wāhi ā-rohe.

System: Te hākinakina hapori me ngā ara rangatahi

Scenario: Ka whakangaohia ngā kaupapa hākinakina me ngā kaupapa hapori
Given ka whakatipu te hākinakina i te whanaungatanga, te manawanui, te kaiārahitanga me te hononga mō ngā rangatahi
When ka whakangaohia ngā kaupapa hākinakina me ngā kaupapa hapori e whakatipu ana i te whanaungatanga, te oranga me te āheinga
Then ka tautokona ngā ara mō ngā rangatahi mā ngā karapu, ngā hōtaka me ngā wāhi ā-rohe
Outcome he tūrangawaewae ō ngā rangatahi, he tāngata hei tū tahi, he anamata hei oma atu
