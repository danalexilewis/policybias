# What the page states

> Disestablishment via a member's bill, democratic accountability rationale, and orderly transition of functions, property and obligations are on the page. The $3.5 million annual IMSB cost is current spend cited as context, not a new fiscal Output.

System: Disestablishment of Auckland's Independent Māori Statutory Board

Scenario: The Independent Māori Statutory Board is disestablished
Given the Independent Māori Statutory Board has exercised significant influence over council decision making since 2010
And ratepayers pay $3.5 million annually to support IMSB operations with no ability to elect or remove its members
When the Independent Māori Statutory Board is disestablished
Then those who influence public spending and decision making are directly accountable to the public
And the IMSB's functions, property and obligations transition back to Auckland Council
Outcome transparency, accountability and public trust restored without preventing council engagement with Māori
