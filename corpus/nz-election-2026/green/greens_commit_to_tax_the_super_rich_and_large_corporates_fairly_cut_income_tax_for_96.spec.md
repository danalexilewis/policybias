# What the page states

> Tax settings would shift wealth and corporate contributions while cutting income tax for most wage earners. A net-assets tax on the super-rich, a Capital Acquisitions Tax, higher corporate and bank levies, and enforced withholding on offshore tech profits would fund critical services. Recent landlord tax cuts and brightline changes would be reversed. A tax-free threshold and new rate on high incomes would follow; net revenue forecasts run from 2027/28 to 2030/31.

System: A tax system for all of us

Scenario: Wealth, corporate and income tax settings change
Given tax settings that allow multi-millionaires to pay $9 in tax out of every $100 they make while a teacher pays $22
And the corporate tax rate is 28 percent for small and medium enterprises
When wealth, corporate and income tax settings change
And recent landlord tax cuts and brightline test changes are reversed
Output 2.5 percent tax on net assets of the super-rich above $10 million with a family home exemption
And a Capital Acquisitions Tax on assets and gifts received worth over $1 million with family farms and family homes exempted
And corporate tax returning to 33 percent for the 0.7 percent biggest corporations while staying 28 percent for small and medium enterprises
And a Bank Levy of 0.06 percent on the liabilities of the four big banks
And the 5 percent withholding tax rate enforced on offshore profits sent by big tech companies
And a tax-free threshold of $10,000
And a new tax rate on income over $160,000
And 96 percent of New Zealanders receiving a tax cut
And net revenue increases by $5.15 billion in 2027/28
And net revenue rises to $5.73 billion by 2030/31
Outcome inequality and corporate under-taxation are tackled to fund critical social services
