# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Mēnā ka herea ngā tūtohutanga a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi, ka neke te kupu whakamutunga i te mana whiriwhiri a ngā minita ki Te Rōpū Whakamana—he huringa tūāpapa ture, ehara i te whakatikatika tukanga. Ko ēhea tūtohutanga ka here, me pēhea te whakamana ki te Karauna, me ēhea mana o te Kaikōmihana me ngā Tauākī Pānga ki Te Tiriti, kei te ture anō ēnei e tuhi.

System: Te herenga, kāore te hoahoa whakamana

Scenario: Ka herea ngā tūtohutanga
Given ka taea e ngā kāwanatanga te whakakāhore noa i ngā kitenga a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi i ēnei rā
When ka herea ngā tūtohutanga a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi
Then ka wehe te kupu whakamutunga i te mana whiriwhiri a te Karauna, ka tau ki Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi
Outcome kāore e wātea ana te whakakāhore i tētahi kitenga hei kōwhiringa tōrangapū
But kia tautuhia rā anō e te ture te whānui me ngā rongoā, ka taea e ngā minita te kī he kōwhiri noa ngā kitenga

Scenario: Ka tāpirihia he Kaikōmihana me ngā Tauākī Pānga
When ka whakatūria he Kaikōmihana Pāremata mō Te Tiriti, ā, me whai Tauākī Pānga ki Te Tiriti ia pire
Then me whai mana me te ihirangi e hiahiatia ana ēnei taputapu e rua i mua i te here i ngā minita, i ngā mema pāremata rānei
Outcome kua whakaingoatia kia kaha ake ngā whakahaere e hāpai ana i Te Tiriti
But ki te kore aua ture, ka taea te whakakāhore i tētahi o aua taputapu i te mahi
