# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka mutu ngā utu pokapū. Ka noho ngā utu kaiako me ngā wāhi hei utu Karauna. Ka kati, ka huri rānei ngā pokapū whai-hua. Ka whānui ngā tina. Ka whiwhi ngā whānau; ka amo te Karauna me ngā kura i ngā pire tū tonu.

System: Ko ngā utu pokapū e neke ana ki ngā pire tū tonu ā-Karauna

Scenario: Ka mutu ngā utu, ka tau te pire utu ki te Karauna
When ka utukorea te mātauranga kōhungahunga, ka tukuna hei painga tūmatanui
Then ka noho ngā utu kaiako me ngā wāhi i utua e ngā mātua hei utu Karauna
Outcome ka mutu te utu a ngā whānau i ngā utu pokapū
But ka tango te Karauna i tētahi pire tuwhera mō ngā utu kaiako me ngā wāhi

Scenario: Ka aukatia ngā pokapū whai-hua
When ka aukatia ngā pokapū kamupene whai-hua
Then me huri te mana me te whakahaere o aua wāhi
Outcome ka kati te tauira mākete e whakahēngia ana e te kaupapa here
But ka amo ngā rangatira pokapū me ngā whānau i te rangirua kia tau rā anō ngā mahere huri, kati rānei

Scenario: Ka whānui a Ka Ora, Ka Ako i te wā ka tautuhi anō te marautanga
When ka whānui a Ka Ora, Ka Ako ki tētahi tina mahana, whai kai, i hangaia ā-rohe, mō te katoa
And ka tautuhi anō ngā whakahou marautanga me te aromatawai ki te ahunga taketake o Te Mātaiaho
Then ka rere te tuku tina me te panoni marautanga hei kawenga pūnaha tū tonu
Outcome ka whānui te kai kura, ka hoki te ahunga marautanga
But ka amo ngā kīhini, ngā kaihoko me ngā kaiako i te kawenga tāpiri i mua i te taenga mai o tētahi moni tāpiri
