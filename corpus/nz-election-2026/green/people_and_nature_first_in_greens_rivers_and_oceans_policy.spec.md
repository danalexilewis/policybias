# What the page states

> Weak water rules have left drinking water, rivers and oceans open to industrial exploitation. The Drink Swim Fish programme names three of eight interventions here: stronger drinking-water limits, tighter wastewater discharge rules, and a phase-out of destructive bottom trawling. Numeric limits, budgets and dates are not given.

System: Drink Swim Fish water and oceans protection

Scenario: Drinking water limits, wastewater rules and a bottom-trawling phase-out take effect
Given up to 100,000 people become sick from unsafe drinking water every year in Aotearoa
When drinking water is protected by setting strong environmental limits and lowering nitrate limits
And rivers and coasts are protected by strengthening rules for wastewater discharges
And moana is protected by phasing out destructive bottom trawling
Then environmental degradation is reversed
