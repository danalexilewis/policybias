# Restoring Law and Order

# National is continuing to restore law and order, making New Zealand safer and putting victims at the heart of our justice system.
# So far, we have:

*   **Reduced violent crime** with **49,000 fewer victims** than when we came into Government.
*   **Reduced ram raids by 85%.**
*   **Reduced serious youth offending by 16%**.
*   **Increased Police patrols.**

* * *

  

# Cracked down on the gangs

*   **Banned gang patches in public.**
*   **Given Police the power to issue non-consorting and dispersal orders.**

  

* * *

  

# **Restored real consequences for crime**

*   **Restoring Three Strikes.**
*   **Capped sentence discounts at 40%.**
*   **Removed repeat discounts** for youth and remorse.
*   **Implemented a sliding scale for early guilty pleas** with a maximum sentence discount of 25%.
*   **Encouraged cumulative sentencing** for offences committed while on bail, in custody, or on parole.  
      
    

* * *
