# What the page states

> Producer statement formalisation, specialist BCA for large commercial buildings, and expected benefits (faster consents, reduced duplication, council focus on residential) are on the page. No dollar commitments appear.

System: Streamlined commercial building consents

Scenario: A developer consents a large commercial building
Given commercial buildings make up around $9 billion of consented work each year
When producer statements are formalised in the Building Act
Then qualified engineers can sign off certain building work without council inspection
Output a specialist Building Consent Authority for large commercial buildings
Outcome faster consent processes for major commercial developments
