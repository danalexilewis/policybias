# What the page states

> Three commitments on the source page. No figures appear.

System: Climate protection aligned with Māori values

Scenario: Whenua and moana are protected from extractive industries
Given whenua, awa, moana, marae, wāhi tapu, mahinga kai and whakapapa are treated as inheritance and responsibility
When whenua and moana are protected from extractive industries including seabed mining
Then climate resilience is invested in for communities most at risk
And regenerative and sustainable approaches aligned with Māori values are backed
Outcome whānau can live safely, affordably and sovereign on their own whenua
