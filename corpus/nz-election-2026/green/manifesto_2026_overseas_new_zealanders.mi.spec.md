# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka mau tonu ngā here kaha a ngā tāngata o Aotearoa e noho ana ki tāwāhi mā ngā tautuhinga kirirarau, pōti, pūtea taurewa me te penihana kaumātua. Mā tētahi huarahi i runga i te tikanga e taea ai te kirirarau mō te hunga e whakapapa Māori ana, ahakoa te wāhi noho, te whenua whānau rānei. Ka māmā ake ngā tikanga pūtea taurewa ākonga mō ngā kaipupuri taurewa ki tāwāhi, ā, ka whakahokia te wā noho 10 tau e hiahiatia ana mō te āheinga penihana kaumātua. Ka whakawhānuihia ngā whakaritenga hauora, oranga me te visa tauutuutu. Kāore he wātaka.

System: Ngā tika me te tautoko mō ngā tāngata o Aotearoa e noho ana ki tāwāhi

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere mō ngā tāngata o Aotearoa e noho ana ki tāwāhi
Given e noho ana ki tāwāhi tōna 15 ōrau o ngā tāngata o Aotearoa
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere mō ngā tāngata o Aotearoa e noho ana ki tāwāhi
Then mā tētahi huarahi i runga i te tikanga e taea ai e te hunga e whakapapa Māori ana te whakatau kirirarau, ahakoa te wāhi noho, te whenua whānau rānei
And ka whakahekehia, ka whakatikaia anō ngā utu me ngā reiti huamoni o ngā pūtea taurewa ākonga mō ngā kaipupuri taurewa ki tāwāhi, ka whakaroaina ngā hararei utu, ā, ka whakakorehia ngā hopukanga i te rohe mō ngā takahi pūtea taurewa
And ka whakaroaina te wā e taea ai e ngā tāngata o Aotearoa te noho ki tāwāhi me te kore e ngaro te āheinga pōti
And ka whakahokia te wā noho 10 tau i Aotearoa e hiahiatia ana mō te āheinga penihana kaumātua
And ka whāia, ka whakawhānuihia ngā whakaritenga taurima hauora, tautoko oranga me te wehenga visa tauutuutu ki ngā manuhiri pōturi
Outcome ka taea e te hunga e noho ana ki tāwāhi te whai wāhi ki te pōti me te haumaru pāpori i runga i ngā tikanga kua kīia
