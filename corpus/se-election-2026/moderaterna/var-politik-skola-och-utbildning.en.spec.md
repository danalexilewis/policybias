# What the page states

> The knowledge school should be restored with high expectations, skilled teachers and study peace. New grades and curricula should give more accurate knowledge assessment. Teachers should get time to teach. Swedish should weigh heavier, and mother-tongue instruction should be reformed.

System: Knowledge school with new grades and more Swedish

Scenario: The knowledge school is reintroduced with new grades and more Swedish
Given one in six pupils leaves compulsory school without eligibility for upper secondary school
And that corresponds to 800 school classes each year
And 35,000 trained teachers do not work in schools
And almost 30 percent of teachers in compulsory school are unqualified
And 314,000 compulsory school pupils have the right to mother-tongue instruction
And mother-tongue instruction costs around 2.2 billion kronor per year
When a new grading system and new knowledge-focused curricula are introduced
Then screening in lower primary school should be expanded and support teaching in smaller groups made easier to offer
And teachers' teaching duty should be regulated anew and all lower-primary teachers offered training in evidence-based reading instruction
And teacher education should be reformed with new content and raised admission thresholds
And principals' and teachers' mandate to secure safety and study peace should be clarified with expectation documents and tightened suspension powers
And Swedish as a second language should be abolished and replaced with basic language instruction
And mother-tongue instruction should be reformed with a requirement for qualified teachers
And language and suitability tests should be introduced at hiring in preschool and school
Output introduction from autumn term 2028 for grades and curricula
And regulated teaching duty from autumn 2027
And reformed teacher education during 2027 and 2028
And clearer mandate and power to suspend pupils from autumn 2026
Outcome effort should pay and all children should get the same chance to master the Swedish language
