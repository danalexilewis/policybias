# Tō mātou māramatanga

> He whakaaro te whāinga wāhanga kirimana tekau mā rima ōrau kia tautuhia e tētahi te ine me te whakamana. Ka piki te wehewehe kirimana nui ki ngā wāhanga iti i te utu tono me te whakahaere mō ngā kaihoko me ngā kaitono, ā, me whai tonu te kōrero-ki-te-kāwanatanga-kotahi i tētahi rere raraunga tiritahi kāore anō kia tū.

System: Ko te Small Business First hei whakaaro, wāhanga me te rere raraunga

Scenario: Ka whakatakotoria te whāinga wāhanga kirimana
When ka whakatakotoria he whāinga mō ngā kirimana kāwanatanga ki ngā pakihi iti me ngā tīmatanga
Then me whai te tutukitanga i tētahi ine me tētahi ture whakamana
Output 15% target for government contracts to small businesses and start-ups
Outcome ki te kore aua mea, he whakaaro te whāinga
But kāore ngā kamupene iti e whiwhi i tētahi mea i tētahi whāinga kāore e taea te pupuri i ngā tari ki a ia

Scenario: Ka wehewehea tētahi kirimana nui ki ngā wāhanga iti
When ka wehewehea tētahi kirimana nui ki ngā mahi iti
Then ka taea e ētahi atu kamupene te tono mō ngā wāhanga ka taea e rātou te tuku
Outcome ka piki ngā utu tono me te whakahaere mō te kaihoko me ia wāhanga
But ka mau ngā kaihoko me ia kaitono wāhanga i te utu tono hoko me te whakahaere kirimana tāpiri

Scenario: Ka whakamahi anō ngā tari i ngā mōhiohio kua mau kē
When ka horahia te kōrero-ki-te-kāwanatanga-kotahi
Then me whai ngā tari i tētahi rere raraunga tiritahi me ngā tohu tautuhi ōrite
Outcome ka whakautu ngā rangatira i ētahi puka tārite iti ake ki te tū tērā rere
But ka whakautu tonu ngā rangatira i ngā puka tārite kia tiritahi ngā tari i ngā tohu tautuhi me ngā raraunga
Activates labour-small-business-action-plan
