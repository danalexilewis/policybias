# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka oati te haereere kore visa o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa, te āwhina āhuarangi nui ake, te hautūtanga tangata moana, tētahi whakakapi RSE me ngā huarahi noho tūturu, me te tautoko nui ake ki SPREP me High Seas. Kāore he nama mō ngā rārangi whenua, ngā tāra āwhina, me te maha o ngā kaimahi kaupeka ka noho tūturu.

System: Te urunga o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa me tētahi huarahi kaupeka kāore i utua

Scenario: Ka tae mai tētahi manuhiri o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa me te kore visa
When ka whakawhānuitia te haereere kore visa ki ngā manuhiri mai i ngā motu o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa
Then kāore e whakawhirinaki te taenga mai ki te tuku visa mō aua whenua
Outcome ka taea te nuku noho poto nui ake
But me whai tonu ngā āpiha rohenga i tētahi rārangi whenua me ngā ture urunga i mua i te eke o tētahi

Scenario: Kāore e herea te mahi kaupeka ki tētahi kaiwhakawhiwhi mahi kotahi
When ka whakakapia te Recognised Seasonal Employer Scheme ki ngā utu tika, te whare noho, ngā mōtika mahi tuwhera me ngā huarahi noho tūturu
Then ka taea e te kaimahi kaupeka te wehe i tētahi kaiwhakawhiwhi mahi me te kore ngaro i te mōtika mahi
Outcome ka taea tonu te whakakī i te korenga kaimahi
But ka tango ngā hapori me ngā kaunihera i ngā kaipupuri noho mai i tētahi rere kaupeka kāore tētahi i here

Scenario: Ka whakahokia te āwhina āhuarangi, kāore he nama
When ka whakahokia, ka nui ake te pūtea āwhina āhuarangi ā-ao
Then ka nui ake te tautoko ki ngā whakawhitinga āhuarangi o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa i tēnei rā
Outcome ko te kotahitanga te ahunga kua whakaingoatia
But ka tatari ngā hoa o Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa ki Te Tai Ōhanga mō tētahi rārangi tāra i mua i te whakatairite i ngā kōpaki āwhina
