Privacy

We do not run accounts. We do not collect a name, email, or login.

This browser may keep a lifetime score and a language choice in local storage. That stays on your device.

If you finish a game, you can add an anonymous row to a public dataset: the score, each guess, the calendar day, and optional questions (age band, ethnicity, intended vote, felt wealth). We do not store a cookie, IP address, or user agent with that row. Anyone can read the dataset.

Vercel hosts the site and counts visits without cookies. Ordinary web requests still reach the host.
