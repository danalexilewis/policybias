# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei a Te Aorerekura te pūtake o te ārai tūkinotanga ā-whānau me te tūkinotanga ā-ai, me te whai pūtea o ngā otinga kaupapa Māori me ngā otinga aratakina e te hapori. Ka hara te Crimes Act i te mana herekore, ka whakahou i te whakaae, ā, ka whakahokia ngā aratohu mātauranga hononga me te hōkakatanga ki ngā kura katoa. Ka piki te tautoko tohutohu me te hauora hinengaro mō te katoa e pā ana ki te pūnaha ture. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Ārai tūkinotanga ā-whānau me te tūkinotanga ā-ai

Scenario: Ka whakatinanatia te mahere ārai tūkinotanga
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere ārai tūkinotanga
Then ka pūteatia te mahere mahi Te Aorerekura, ko Te Tiriti te pūtake
And ka whai pūtea ngā otinga kaupapa Māori me ngā otinga aratakina e te hapori mō te tūkinotanga ā-whānau me te tūkinotanga ā-ai, mō ngā wāhine, ngā hapori Pasifika, rainbow, me te hunga hauā
And ka pai ake te māramatanga o te ohumahi me te iwi whānui ki ngā take o te tūkinotanga me te urupare haumaru
And ka whakahoutia te Crimes Act kia hara te mana herekore, kia whakahou i te whakaae, tae atu ki te aukati i te whakaae manawa-rū
And ka whakahokia ki ngā kura katoa ngā aratohu mātauranga hononga me te hōkakatanga mō ngā hononga hauora me te whakaae
And ka piki te tautoko tohutohu me te hauora hinengaro mō ngā tāngata i tūkinotia, ngā whānau, ngā kaititiro, ngā kaikōrero, me ngā mātanga ture
Outcome ka ārahi ngā hapori i ngā otinga haumaru e ū ana ki te tikanga
