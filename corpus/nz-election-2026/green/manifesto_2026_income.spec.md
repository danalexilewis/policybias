# What the page states

> The 2.5% tax on net wealth over $10 million is on the source page. Liveable benefit rates, the tax-free threshold and the top income tax rate are not. No timetable is given.

System: Liveable incomes and tax settings

Scenario: The income plan is put into effect
When the government puts the income plan into effect
Then the Social Security Act is reformed with a high-trust model at Work and Income
And benefits increase to liveable rates with individualised entitlements and sanctions abolished
And ACC covers income support and treatment regardless of injury, disability or illness origin
And Working for Families rates boost, abatements reduce and tax credits extend to out-of-work families
And income tax becomes progressive with a tax-free threshold, lower rates for low and middle earners and a higher top rate
And recoverable assistance becomes grants, MSD debt is written down and attachment orders against beneficiaries are banned
Output 2.5% tax on net wealth over $10 million
Outcome poverty is reduced through social welfare and tax changes
Activates green-tax-system-for-all
