# Tō mātou māramatanga

> Ka whakahou te kaupapa here i te Treaty of Waitangi Act, ka whakanoho i te whakatau take-Tiriti ki te kāwanatanga katoa, ka whakahou i ngā whakataunga kāore he rara Full and Final, ka whai rauemi a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi ki ngā mana whakahoki whenua here, ā, ka tirotiro i te whakakāhore-tuatahi whenua motuhake. Me whai ture ka tae ki te taitara te whakahoki whenua motuhake here; ko te tango i te Full and Final ka waiho ngā kerēme kia tuwhera.

System: Te whakahoki here me ngā whakataunga mutunga-kore

Scenario: Ka riro i te Taraipiunara ngā tūtohutanga here mō te whakahoki whenua
When ka piki ngā rauemi o Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi me te mana ki te tuku tūtohutanga here mō te whakahoki whenua Karauna me te whenua motuhake
Then ka taea te neke o te whenua Karauna i runga i tētahi huarahi here mēnā ka ū te ture
Outcome ehara te whakahoki whenua i te tūtohutanga anake i ngā kupu o te kaupapa here
But ka neke te whenua motuhake mēnā ka taea e te ture te here i te taitara motuhake; ki te kore, ka mutu te tūtohutanga ki te kēti o te kaipupuri

Scenario: Ka tangohia ngā rara Full and Final i ngā whakataunga
When ka tango te whakahou Treaty Settlements i ngā rara e kōrero ana mō te whakataunga Full and Final
And ka mutu te large natural grouping, ā, ka whakamanatia te rangatiratanga hapū
Then kāore tētahi whakataunga kua hainatia e kati anō i tētahi kerēme anō i runga i ngā tikanga Full and Final
Outcome ka nui ake ngā rōpū ka taea te whiriwhiri i te taumata hapū
But kāore ngā kerēme i kitea he kati i raro i te Full and Final e whai anō i tērā kati i runga i te ārahi kua whakahouria

Scenario: Ko te whakakāhore-tuatahi whenua motuhake e tīrohia ana anake
When ka uru ki te whakahoki whenua i raupatutia hētia te tirotiro i tētahi motika-whakakāhore-tuatahi i te wā hoko
Then ka taea e ngā āpiha te hoahoa i tētahi tukanga kia hoko tuatahi ai te iwi, te hapū me te whānau
Outcome ka waiho e te kaupapa here te kūaha kia tuwhera ki te whakahoki whenua motuhake ki waho o ngā whakataunga
But ehara te tirotiro i tētahi kaupapa whakakāhore-tuatahi e mahi ana kia ū rā anō ngā waeture, te ture rānei
