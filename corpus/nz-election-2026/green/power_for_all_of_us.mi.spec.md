# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka neke te hiko hou utu-ngāwari i te pūngao kāinga me te hapori ki te pūngao rā o te tuanui, ki a Warmer Kiwi Homes me Kiwipower nā te iwi. Ko ngā taurewa pūngao mā huamoni-kore, ngā utu kaweake pūngao rā tika me te haumi pūngao hapori e whai ana ki te whakaheke nama me te whakaiti i te mana mākete o ngā kaihanga nui. Ka whakatūria a Kiwipower hei hinonga Karauna e haumi ana ki te whakaputa hou me te kaha whakapūmau, tae atu ki te tuku tau-maroke me te kore tauranga LNG, ka utua e te Tāke Hunga Tino Whai Rawa.

System: Te hiko hou utu-ngāwari mō ngā kāinga me ngā hapori

Scenario: Ka mana ngā taurewa huamoni-kore, te pūngao hapori me Kiwipower
Given e whakahaere ana ngā kamupene hiko nui e whā i tua atu i te 85 ōrau o ngā mākete hoko-iti me te whakaputa
And e kore e taea e ētahi kāinga nui ake i te 200,000 te whakamahana i ō rātou kāinga
When ka whakaurua tētahi kaupapa taurewa pūngao mā huamoni-kore nā te kāwanatanga
And ka whiwhi ngā kāinga me ngā pakihi he pūngao rā tuanui i tētahi utu kaweake tika
And ka whakamanahia te pūngao rā tūhono, ā, ka whiwhi ngā kairēti i te mōtika ki te pūngao rā
And ka whakawhānuihia a Warmer Kiwi Homes
And ka pūteatia ngā kaupapa pūngao hapori
And ka herea te pūngao rā ki ngā whare tūmatanui hou, kua whakahoutia rānei, ina tika
And ka pūteatia te pūngao hou mō ngā whare Māori
And ka whakatūria a Kiwipower hei hinonga Karauna nā te iwi e haumi ana ki te whakaputa hou me te kaha whakapūmau
Then ka ngāwari ake ngā nama hiko kāinga
Output te kapi taurewa mō te 90% o ngā kaipupuri kāinga
And 50,000 whakahou Warmer Kiwi Homes i ngā tau e whā i te 80 ōrau tautoko
And $980 miriona whakataunga mō Kiwipower i ngā tau e whā
And $100 miriona ia tau mō te whakahaere me te $142 miriona ia tau haumi hou i te tīmatanga
And $421.2 miriona opex katoa mō ngā taurewa huamoni-kore
And $969.8 miriona opex katoa mō Warmer Kiwi Homes
And $460 miriona capex katoa mō te pūngao rā o ngā whare tūmatanui
And $200 miriona i haumitia ki ngā kaupapa pūngao hapori
And $80 miriona i haumitia ki te pūngao hou mō ngā whare Māori
And te pūngao rā ki tua atu i te haurua o ngā whare tūmatanui katoa i roto i ngā tau e whā
Outcome ka nui ake te mana o ngā hapori ki te pūngao

Scenario: Ka hangaia te whakapūmau tau-maroke me te kore tauranga LNG
Given e whakahaere ana ngā kamupene hiko nui e whā i tua atu i te 85 ōrau o ngā mākete hoko-iti me te whakaputa
And 200,000 kāinga e kore e taea te whakamahana i ō rātou kāinga
When ka haumi a Kiwipower ki te whakaputa hou, ā, ka kirimana i te whakapūmau tau-maroke
Then ka tukuna te whakapūmau me te kore tauranga LNG
And ka taea e ngā kaihanga motuhake me ngā kaihoko-iti te whakamahi i tērā kaha
Outcome ka tutuki te mōrearea tau-maroke mā te whakapūmau hou tūmatanui, kaua mā te hau kawemai
