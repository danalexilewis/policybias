# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka huri te ture mahi ki te reo tahi, mā te mematanga uniana taunoa me ngā mōtika patu mahi whānui ake, tae atu ki te mahi tōrangapū me te mahi tautoko. Mā te mōtika wehe e tiaki te whakapā i waho o ngā hāora mahi, ā, ka piki te utu iti rawa kia rite rawa ki te pikinga utu, ka neke hoki ki te utu oranga. Ka piki te whakamatuatanga utua ki te 15 marama mō te kaitiaki matua, me te wā hoa kua rāhuihia me ngā utu kua piki. Kāore i whakapuakina ngā pāpātanga utu whakamatuatanga. Kāore he wātaka.

System: Ngā mōtika kaimahi me te utu

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere mōtika kaimahi
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere mōtika kaimahi
Then ka noho taunoa te ture mahi ki te mematanga uniana me te kōwhiri wehe
And ka whānui ake te mōtika patu mahi, tae atu ki ngā takahanga kirimana tahi me ngā take tōrangapū me te tautoko
And mā te mōtika wehe e tiaki ngā kaimahi i te whakapā me te mātaki i waho o ngā hāora mahi
And ka tūhuratia ngā māngai kaimahi ki ngā poari o ngā kamupene rārangitia
And ka piki ia tau te utu iti rawa kia rite rawa ki te pikinga utu, ka utua ngā tūranga pūtea tūmatanui ki te utu oranga, ā, ka neke te utu iti rawa ki te utu oranga
And ka piki te whakamatuatanga utua ki te 15 marama mō te kaitiaki matua, me te wā hoa kua rāhuihia me ngā utu kua piki
And ka kaha ake te whakamātautau kaituku-mahi ki te kaimahi mō ngā kaimahi gig me te papa mahi
And ka katihia ngā āputa utu ira tangata me te iwi mā te taurite utu, te mārama me te pūrongo
Outcome ka kapi te ture mahi i te wā, te utu me ngā mōtika whakahaere
