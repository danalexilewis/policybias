# What the page states

> Swedish NATO membership is supported, without restrictions on co-operation with the alliance's members. A NATO option was voted through before the invasion of Ukraine, and membership was backed when it became actual. NATO's missile defence is to be strengthened, the ability to meet the Chinese threat is to be strengthened, and NATO is to stick to core tasks — not environmental issues, which other organisations handle better.
