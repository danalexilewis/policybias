# What the page states

> Many older people meet care with too little time, high staff turnover and fragmented contact between home care, healthcare and housing. Staffing and the working environment are to be strengthened, minute-management and profit extraction stopped, and older people are to have more say over housing, food and company.

System: Elderly care with time and continuity

Scenario: Minute-management is dropped and the same staff come back
Given many older people meet care with too little time, high staff turnover and fragmented contact
When staffing is strengthened and minute-management is abolished
Then older people are not to meet new staff constantly
And staff may train in paid working time and a working-environment lift is introduced in elderly care
And tax money for elderly care goes to the service and surplus is reinvested
And home care, care homes and primary care cooperate better with medicines reviews
And more forms of housing, moving support, time outdoors and good food are to be part of care
Outcome a national older people's ombudsman watches older people's rights
And knowledge of violence, abuse and neglect in elderly care is to increase
