# What the page states

> The buy-back, merger into a Crown-owned "National Bank of New Zealand", blended funding stack, and self-financing claim are on the page. Context figures include 85% foreign bank share, Kiwibank's under-8% mortgage share, and BNZ's $1.5 billion annual cash earnings — none of these are purchase or capital commitments, so the stated spec has no Output.

System: Crown-owned bank at scale to compete with foreign-owned majors

Scenario: A buy-back of BNZ is proposed and merged with Kiwibank
Given four Australian-owned banks control around 85 percent of the banking system
And Kiwibank holds just under 8 percent of the mortgage market
And BNZ currently generates more than $1.5 billion in annual cash earnings
When a buy-back of BNZ from National Australia Bank is proposed
Then BNZ is merged with Kiwibank to form a fully Crown-owned, commercially run National Bank of New Zealand
And the buy-back is funded from a blended stack not drawn from the operating budget
Outcome real competitive pressure on the Australian-owned banks
And banking profits stay in New Zealand
