# What the page states

> The security situation is the most serious since the Second World War, and military capability is to be raised quickly.
> A preparedness tax is to secure the long-term and permanent financing so that welfare is not crowded out.
> Conscription is strengthened towards 12 000 trained and in the longer run 20 000, with new regiments in Kiruna and Kvarn and reinforcement of Gotland.

System: A preparedness tax and conscription

Scenario: Rearmament is financed with tax and more conscripts
Given the most serious security-policy situation since the Second World War, in which operational capability must be raised
When a preparedness tax is introduced and conscription is strengthened
Then the expanded financing becomes long-term and permanent, and welfare is not to be crowded out
Output 12 000 conscripts in training
And 20 000 conscripts in the longer run
Outcome new regiments in Kiruna and Kvarn, a new operational amphibious battalion on Gotland, and production of a further Swedish submarine
