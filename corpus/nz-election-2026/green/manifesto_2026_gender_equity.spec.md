# What the page states

> Gender and ethnic pay gaps would close through restored pay equity, transparency and reporting. Best Start would extend to every child under three, pā harakeke one-stop-shops would support whānau through hapūtanga and early parenthood, and endometriosis care would speed up through guidelines and specialised clinics. Family and sexual violence would meet kaupapa Māori and community-led resourcing, coercive control would be criminalised with consent law reform, and paid parental leave would rise to 15 months for the primary caregiver.

System: Gender equity and family wellbeing

Scenario: The gender equity plan is put into effect
When the government puts the gender equity plan into effect
Then gender and ethnic pay gaps are closed by undoing pay equity changes, resuming claims, pay transparency and pay gap reporting
And the Best Start payment is increased and extended to every child under three as a universal child benefit
And pā harakeke one-stop-shops provide wrap-around services through hapūtanga and early parenthood
And endometriosis and pelvic pain are diagnosed and treated faster with clinical guidelines and specialised clinics
And kaupapa Māori and community-led family and sexual violence solutions are resourced
And the Crimes Act is amended to criminalise coercive control and amend consent including precluding reluctant consent
And paid parental leave increases to 15 months for the primary caregiver with ring-fenced partner leave and increased payments
Outcome gender-based violence and economic inequality are reduced
