# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka whakamutua te ara e tango ana i ngā tamariki Māori i ō rātou iwi. Ka whakahokia te mana whānau, me te whakangao ki te taurima ārahitia e te whānau. Ka noho haepapa te kāwanatanga mō ngā mamae i puta i ngā tango tamariki me ngā noho tamariki.

System: Ngā mokopuna Māori me te mana whānau

Scenario: Ka whakamutua te ara e tango ana i ngā tamariki Māori i ō rātou iwi
Given he whakapapa, he kaiārahi āpōpō ngā tamariki, me tupu ora rātou i te taha o tō rātou iwi
When ka whakamutua te ara e tango ana i ngā tamariki Māori i ō rātou iwi
Then ka whakahokia te mana whānau, me te whakangao ki te taurima ārahitia e te whānau
And ka noho haepapa te kāwanatanga mō ngā mamae i puta i ngā tango tamariki me ngā noho tamariki
