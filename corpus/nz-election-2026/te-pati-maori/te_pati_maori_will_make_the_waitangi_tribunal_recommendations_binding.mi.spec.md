# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka herea ngā tūtohutanga a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi. Ka whakatūria he Kaikōmihana Pāremata mō Te Tiriti, ā, me noho he Tauākī Pānga ki Te Tiriti ki ia pire i mua i te Pāremata. I tēnei wā, ka taea e ngā kāwanatanga te whakakāhore noa i ngā kitenga a Te Rōpū.

System: Ngā tūtohutanga here a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi

Scenario: Kāore e taea te whakakāhore anō i ngā kitenga a Te Rōpū
Given ka taea e Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi te tūhura, te whakarongo ki ngā taunakitanga, me te tuku kitenga
And ka taea noa e ngā kāwanatanga te whakakāhore i aua kitenga
When ka herea ngā tūtohutanga a Te Rōpū Whakamana i te Tiriti o Waitangi
Then ka whakatūria he Kaikōmihana Pāremata mō Te Tiriti
And ka noho he Tauākī Pānga ki Te Tiriti ki ia pire i mua i te Pāremata
Outcome ka whakakaha ake ngā rōpū e hāpai ana i Te Tiriti, kaua e whakangoikoretia
