# What the page states

> Sweden needs new nuclear power as baseload to push down electricity prices, strengthen industry and electrify. The delivery phase should break ground, the electricity market should reward stability and electricity production should be able to double by 2045.

System: Delivery phase for new nuclear power and reformed electricity market

Scenario: New nuclear power is to be built in the delivery phase
Given the energy target is 100 percent fossil-free electricity production
When fossil-free electricity production is expanded with a focus on new nuclear power including small modular reactors
Then the delivery phase should break ground for new reactors
And the electricity market should reward stability, capacity and supply security, not only produced kilowatt-hours
And grid expansion should be accelerated through shorter permit processes and increased investment
Output 100 percent fossil-free electricity production
And electricity production doubles by 2045
Outcome it should be easy, attractive and profitable to establish nuclear power and electrification and net zero should become possible
