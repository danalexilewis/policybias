# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover wellbeing legislation, a renewable infrastructure agency, distributed solar, a fossil fuel extraction ban, LNG cancellation and a single regulator. Dollar amounts for Kiwipower and household programmes appear on other energy pages, not this source page.

System: Affordable renewable energy system reform

Scenario: The energy plan is put into effect
When the government puts the energy plan into effect
Then legislation puts public wellbeing outcomes first in the power system
And a renewable energy infrastructure agency ensures security of supply and lower prices
And distributed solar and batteries are enabled for households, marae and public buildings with community-led energy planning
And new fossil fuel extraction is banned, fossil fuel subsidies end and the LNG terminal is cancelled
And a single energy regulator and Ministry for Energy are established with Te Tiriti-based Māori and community ownership frameworks
And industry decarbonises with demand response and energy efficiency encouraged
Outcome communities have affordable, secure, clean renewable energy
Activates green-power-for-all-of-us
