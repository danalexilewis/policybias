# What the page states

> Insecurity has risen and respect for human dignity has been hollowed out. Fatal shootings have more than tripled since the 2010s. Criminal policy is recast around a stronger victim perspective, more police officers and judges, greater capacity in the Prison and Probation Service, and an end to generous bulk discounts on sentences. Expulsion is to be the main rule for anyone who is not a Swedish citizen and receives more than six months in prison.

System: Crime control with the victim at the centre

Scenario: Anyone who commits a crime meets a sentence that bites
Given insecurity has risen and respect for human dignity has been hollowed out
And fatal shootings have more than tripled since the 2010s
When criminal policy is recast around a stronger victim perspective
Then anyone who commits a crime is to receive a sentence that bites
And today's generous bulk discounts on sentences are to be abolished
And more police officers and judges are required, as well as greater capacity in the Prison and Probation Service
Outcome expulsion becomes the main rule for anyone who is not a Swedish citizen and receives more than six months in prison
And those locked up cannot commit new crimes or recruit children and young people into the gangs
