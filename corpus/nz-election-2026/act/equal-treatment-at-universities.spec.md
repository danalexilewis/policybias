# What the page states

> The 2024 government direction gap for tertiary institutions, the Equal Treatment Amendment Bill scope, preserved allocation grounds, and private endowment exemption are all on the source page. No figures or timetable appear on the page.

System: Equal treatment at taxpayer-funded tertiary institutions

Scenario: Parliament passes the Education and Training (Equal Treatment) Amendment Bill
Given universities can currently reserve scholarships, accommodation, study spaces and other opportunities on the basis of ethnicity
And in 2024 the Government directed public agencies to allocate support according to need and value rather than ethnicity but tertiary institutions were not covered
When the Education and Training Act is amended to prevent tertiary institutions conferring benefits on the basis of race or ethnic origin
Then the rule applies to scholarships, grants, financial assistance, accommodation, designated spaces and other benefits or opportunities
Outcome universities may still allocate support according to academic merit, academic need, geographic location, economic circumstances or disability
And endowments, bequests and other assistance from private individuals, iwi, charities or trusts for a targeted purpose remain exempt
