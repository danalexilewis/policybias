# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover disability rights law, income support, universal design housing, the Total Mobility scheme, Enabling Good Lives, emergency plans and supported decision-making. The page names a 75% Total Mobility taxi subsidy. No payment rates or budgets appear.

System: Disability rights and accessible services

Scenario: The disability plan is put into effect
When the government puts the disability plan into effect
Then a disability rights law based on Te Tiriti with enforceable accessibility standards is introduced
And easily accessible and timely income support meets all disability-related living and medical costs regardless of relationship status and cause of disability
And universal design standards support safe, accessible, affordable housing in communities of choice
And the Total Mobility taxi scheme continues with easier use and more mobility vehicles
And the Enabling Good Lives approach guides agencies and barriers to health, transport, education and disability support are removed
And national and local emergency response plans address disabled people's needs
And supported decision-making replaces legal capacity assessments
Output 75% subsidy for the Total Mobility taxi scheme
Outcome disabled people and whānau live with dignity and autonomy
