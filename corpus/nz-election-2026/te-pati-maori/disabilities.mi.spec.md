# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tautokona ngā ratonga tāngata whaikaha aratakina e te Māori me te whakatau aratakina e te Māori. Ka ngāwari ake te uru ki te tautoko, ā, ka iti iho te whakaiti i te mana. Ka hāngai te pūtea ki ngā āhuatanga o te whānau, kaua ki ngā wehenga tari.

System: Tautoko tāngata whaikaha aratakina e te Māori

Scenario: Ka tautokona ngā ratonga tāngata whaikaha aratakina e te Māori
Given ka tūtaki ngā tāngata whaikaha Māori ki tētahi pūnaha e whakaiti ana i te mana, i te ngana ki te uru ki te taurima me te tū rangatira
When ka tautokona ngā ratonga tāngata whaikaha aratakina e te Māori me te whakatau aratakina e te Māori
Then ka ngāwari ake te uru ki te tautoko, ā, ka iti iho te whakaiti i te mana
And ka hāngai te pūtea ki ngā āhuatanga o te whānau, kaua ki ngā wehenga tari
Outcome ka whiwhi ngā tāngata whaikaha Māori i te tautoko e whakanui ana i tō rātou mana, i ō rātou whānau, i tō rātou mōtika kia ora katoa
