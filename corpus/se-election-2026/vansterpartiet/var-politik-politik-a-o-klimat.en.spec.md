# What the page states

> The climate crisis is acute and emissions must come down quickly; the transition requires public investment, not only recycling. The target is a fossil-free society with zero emissions by 2035 at the latest, a national emissions budget per parliamentary term, and massive efforts in green industry, renewable energy, housing, transport and protected nature. Those who already have most carry the largest emissions, so more of the economy is to go to common investment and welfare. Climate-damaging subsidies are phased out.

System: Fossil-free by 2035 with an investment programme and a Sweden ticket

Scenario: A ten-year climate programme and a monthly ticket are rolled out
Given the climate crisis is acute and individual efforts such as recycling are not enough
And it is the rich who through their way of living cause the largest climate emissions
When the massive investment programme for the climate begins
Then transport, energy and housing are converted
And it becomes cheaper to take the train than to fly
And more charging points are built and support for Klimatklivet increases
And valuable forests are protected and wetlands restored so that the uptake of carbon dioxide is strengthened
Output 700 billion kronor over 10 years
And a Sweden ticket at 450 kr a month for regional public transport across the country
And a climate target of zero emissions by 2035 at the latest
Outcome a national emissions budget with measures that every government must present for the parliamentary term
And instructions to the AP funds for fossil-free pensions
And a new climate bonus for electric cars that does not reach the most expensive cars, plus a conversion premium for used cars
And a road-wear charge for heavy lorries and a progressive aviation tax
