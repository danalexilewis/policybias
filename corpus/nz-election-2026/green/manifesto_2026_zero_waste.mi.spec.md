# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Mā tētahi ōhanga porowhita e kawenga ngā kaihanga mā te kaitiakitanga e herea ana, tētahi kaupapa whakahoki ipu inu me te mōtika-ki-te-whakatika. Ka utua te whakaiti para e te pūtea tāke tuku para, ka piki te tāke ki te tikanga pai rawa o te ao, ā, ka whakamahia kia ōrite ki ngā karaehe whenua para. Ka pā tētahi aukati para-ki-te-pūngao i te wā e tautuhia ana ngā whāinga whakaheke taurangi here i raro i te whakahou Waste Minimisation Act e mōhio ana ki Te Tiriti. Kāore i whakapuakina ngā pāpātanga tāke me ngā nama whāinga. Kāore he wātaka.

System: Kore para me te ōhanga porowhita

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere kore para
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere kore para
Then ka whakatūria te kaitiakitanga hua e herea ana, tētahi kaupapa whakahoki ipu inu, ngā whakakore hua me te mōtika-ki-te-whakatika
And ka utua te whakaiti para e te pūtea tāke tuku para, ka piki te tāke ki te tikanga pai rawa o te ao, ā, ka ōrite te pāpātanga ki ngā karaehe whenua para
And ka pā tētahi aukati ki ngā wheketere para-ki-te-pūngao, ā, ka tūhuratia te haukoi mai i te para koiora
And ka tautuhia ngā whāinga whakaheke para taurangi here, me Te Tiriti i te Waste Minimisation Act me te kawenga-tiaki mō ngā awa para katoa
And ka whakawhitia te oneone kua mahue i ngā whenua para ina haumaru
And ka whakaarohia tuatahi te whakaora rawa me te tukatuka para koiora ā-rohe, ā-mana whenua, ā-hapori
Outcome ka kawenga ngā kaihanga mō ngā hua puta noa i tō rātou oranga
