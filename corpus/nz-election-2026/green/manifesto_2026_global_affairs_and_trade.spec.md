# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover foreign policy, defence, alliances, Palestine, decolonisation, trade and climate diplomacy. No figures appear.

System: Independent foreign affairs and trade

Scenario: The global affairs and trade plan is put into effect
When the government puts the global affairs and trade plan into effect
Then an independent foreign policy stands against human rights abuses and upholds peace and justice
And defence policy focuses on climate responses, humanitarian response and environmental monitoring
And military and security frameworks decouple from the United States including declining AUKUS and withdrawing from Five Eyes
And Pacific sovereignty and independence are supported and Pacific militarisation is opposed
And Palestine is recognised as a state and Israel is sanctioned
And decolonisation and democracy movements globally are supported especially Indigenous peoples in the Asia–Pacific region
And the Trade For All agenda enforces Te Tiriti, workers' rights, human rights and climate commitments in trade agreements
And climate initiatives progress through international institutions including reduced patent protections on green technologies and climate border adjustments
Outcome Aotearoa is not a member of AUKUS or Five Eyes
