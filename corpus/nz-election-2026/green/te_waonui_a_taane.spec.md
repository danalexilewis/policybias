> Dollar figures are from the source page: $2.44 billion total Crown funding, $400 million over four years for Te Rākau Whakarauora Restoration Fund, and $2 billion for Te Tōtara Haemata Intergenerational Fund.

System: Te Waonui a Tāne marae funding via Te Rangatiratanga Trust

Scenario: Crown establishes Te Rangatiratanga Trust and two complementary funds
When nationwide wānanga establish the trustee body, two funds and governance mechanisms
And Te Rangatiratanga Trust is created owned and governed by hapū and iwi of Aotearoa
Then dedicated Crown funding is provided through two complementary funds
Output $2.44 billion Crown funding for Te Rangatiratanga Trust
And $400 million over four years for immediate and urgent marae projects
And $2 billion in an intergenerational investment portfolio
Outcome marae are resourced as critical social and cultural infrastructure for generations
