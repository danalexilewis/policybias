System: Breakthrough Economy

Scenario: Science, competition and small business settings change
Given supermarket and other uncompetitive sectors currently cost the average family $30 a week
When research and development spending is lifted
And new technology investment tax credits are introduced and proven accelerator funding is doubled
And student-loan interest is forgiven for returning Kiwis
And polytechs become regional innovation hubs
And a cross-party population strategy is launched
And the Commerce Commission is given legal power and funding to break up duopolies and other uncompetitive markets through the High Court
And small-business budgeting services are funded, R&D tax-credit access is sped up, and ACC and the retail payment system are made fair
And an Impact Company legal structure is created for firms that make a profit while doing social and environmental good
Then supermarket and other uncompetitive sectors face a real threat of being split
Output research and development spending of at least 2% of GDP
And $12 to $31 a week saved for Kiwi families from more competition in supermarkets, banks and other sectors
Outcome scientists, returning graduates and small firms have more room to grow
