System: Justice and community safety reform

Scenario: The justice and community safety plan is put into effect
When the government puts the justice and community safety plan into effect
Then evidence-based rehabilitation and tikanga-based restorative justice including Te Pae Oranga are resourced for anyone convicted
And Te Ao Mārama and specialist youth courts expand nationwide
And military-style academies end, the age of criminal responsibility rises and youth are not detained with adults
And frontline police wear body cameras, IPCA powers strengthen and Māori and Pacific Wardens are resourced
And reintegration support including housing improves and the Steps to Freedom Grant covers at least 30 years of inflation
And legal aid, community law centres and protection and parenting order applications are expanded or made free
And mandatory minimums, life sentences, preventive detention, three strikes and sentence discount limits are abolished
And Christchurch Mosque attack royal commission recommendations are implemented with extended hate speech laws
And Abuse in Care royal commission recommendations are implemented in consultation with survivors
Outcome punitive sentencing is replaced with evidence-based approaches
