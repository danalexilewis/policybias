# Our understanding

> Safe, legal routes via the quota are the intended main path. The volume is "Nordic average", which is a comparison, not a number. Work-permit rules tighten in low-pay jobs and then loosen again for named shortage occupations in care. Return of people without protection grounds is asserted, not staffed.

System: Quota path with an unnumbered average

Scenario: Spontaneous asylum is pushed off the main path
Given the outer EU border is to be controlled more tightly
When the quota system becomes the main way to seek asylum
Then people without protection grounds are told to leave
Outcome intake is supposed to sit at a Nordic average
But that average is not written as a person count

Scenario: Care shortage occupations are carved out of the higher wage floor
Given rules for low-pay labour immigration are tightened against fraud and crowding-out
When certain shortage jobs, including care, are exempted
Then the floor is not uniform
Outcome care employers can hire on the old terms while other low-pay sectors cannot
But the wage level of the floor is not stated
