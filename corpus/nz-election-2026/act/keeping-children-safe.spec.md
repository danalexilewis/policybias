# What the page states

> The three-year check gap, national card, continuous Police checking, suspension on serious charges, child-protection training, and about $108 paid-worker fee are all on the source page. The linked PDF prints $12–20 million one-off establishment, about $50 million a year operating, about $37.5 million recovered in fees, and about $12.5 million net annual cost. No offence list or commencement date is given.

System: Working with Children Card for covered roles

Scenario: A paid worker or volunteer seeks to work with children
Given the current system can leave three years between safety checks even if worrying new Police information emerges
And safety checks are carried out by individual employers and generally only re-checked every three years
When one national card is created that paid workers and volunteers must have to work with children
Then the card belongs to the holder and lasts three years
Output cardholders checked continuously against new Police information
And card suspension when Police learn someone may be dangerous, stopping work with children and notifying every linked organisation
And practical child-protection training refreshed when the card is renewed
And applications exempt or discounted for volunteers and students
And about $108 paid by paid workers, often covered by employers
And $12–20 million one-off establishment cost
And about $50 million a year operating cost
And about $37.5 million a year recovered through application fees
And about $12.5 million a year net cost to taxpayers
Outcome people convicted of specified serious offences against children are kept out of covered roles
And employers receive immediate notice when a cardholder must stop working with children during assessment
