# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tautokona ngā pakihi ā-rohe mā Buy Kiwi Made, te whakawhānui i ngā tapanga whenua-taketake, me te nui ake o te hoko a te kāwanatanga i ngā pakihi o Aotearoa. Ka whakawhānuihia ngā takuhe me ngā whakahoki moni mō ngā pakihi ICT iti ake, ā, ka haere tonu te whanaketanga ā-rohe aratakina e te hapori i te hanga toitū me te hanganga iti-waro. Ka whakahoutia ngā ture whakataetae me ngā mana kaha ake o ngā tari waeture. Kāore he nama tāra, he rā rānei i homai.

System: Tautoko pakihi iti me te hoko ā-rohe

Scenario: Ka whakatinanatia te mahere pakihi iti
Given i tupu i te tau kua hipa te nui rawa o ngā whakakore pakihi i roto i te tata ki te 15 tau
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere pakihi iti
Then ka whakahoutia a Buy Kiwi Made, ā, ka whakawhānuihia ngā tapanga whenua-taketake
And ka hoko nui ake ngā tari kāwanatanga i ngā taonga me ngā ratonga i ngā pakihi o Aotearoa
And ka whakawhānuihia ngā takuhe me ngā whakahoki moni mō ngā pakihi ICT iti ake e whakatipu ana i ngā mahi toitū, iti-waro
And ka haere tonu te whanaketanga ā-rohe aratakina e te hapori i te hanga toitū, te ahuwhenua whakaora, te hanganga iti-waro, me ngā mahi aratakina e te iwi me te hapū
And ka whakahoutia ngā ture whakataetae me te nui ake o ngā rawa me ngā mana o ngā tari waeture
Outcome ka whai hua ngā pakihi ā-rohe i te tautoko a te kāwanatanga me te kaihoko
