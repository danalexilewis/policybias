# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka tiakina ngā hapori mā te whakatika i ngā take o te hara, mā te aukati me te whakaora, tēnā i te whiu tāwai. Ka whakawhānuihia te whakaora i runga i te taunakitanga me te whakawā whakatika i runga i te tikanga, i te taha o ngā kōti mātanga, te kawenga o ngā pirihimana, me te tautoko whakahoki ki te hapori. Ka tangohia ngā iti rawa here me ngā here rite, ā, ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga a ngā kōmihana kāwana. Me kapi i te Steps to Freedom Grant te pikinga utu o ngā tau 30 neke atu; kāore he nui tāra o te takuhe i homai. Kāore he wātaka i homai.

System: Te whakahou i Te Tāhū o te Ture me te haumaru hapori

Scenario: Ka whakatinanahia te mahere ture me te haumaru hapori
When ka whakatinana te kāwanatanga i te mahere ture me te haumaru hapori
Then ka whai pūtea te whakaora i runga i te taunakitanga me te whakawā whakatika i runga i te tikanga, tae atu ki Te Pae Oranga, mō te hunga kua whakataua te hara
And ka whakawhānuihia Te Ao Mārama me ngā kōti rangatahi mātanga ki te motu katoa
And ka mutu ngā whare ako ā-hoia, ka piki te pakeke mō te kawenga hara, ā, kāore ngā rangatahi e mauherea tahitia ki ngā pakeke
And ka mau ngā pirihimana o mua o Ngā Pirihimana o Aotearoa i ngā kāmera tinana, ka kaha ake ngā mana o te IPCA, ā, ka whai pūtea ngā Māori and Pacific Wardens
And ka pai ake te tautoko whakahoki ki te hapori, tae atu ki te whare noho, ā, ka kapi i te Steps to Freedom Grant te pikinga utu o ngā tau 30 neke atu
And ka whakawhānuihia, ka utukore rānei, te āwhina ture, ngā pokapū ture hapori, me ngā tono ota tiaki me ngā ota matua
And ka whakakorehia ngā iti rawa here, ngā whiu ora, te mauhere aukati, te three strikes, me ngā here whakaheke whiu
And ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga a te kōmihana kāwana mō te whakaeke i te whare karakia o Ōtautahi, me ngā ture kōrero kino kua whakawhānuihia
And ka whakatinanahia ngā tūtohutanga a te kōmihana kāwana mō te tūkinotanga i roto i te tiaki, mā te kōrero ki te hunga i ora
Outcome ka whakakapi te whiu tāwai i ngā huarahi i runga i te taunakitanga
