For jobs, the
climate and public
services across
the country.
                     Sweden
                     can do more.
We need more jobs
When more than half a million people are unemployed, it also affects
those in work. Because the more people we have in work, the stronger
the Swedish economy becomes. Which enables us to invest more in
public services. Furthermore: a person with a job has a context, greater
freedom and more scope to shape their future.

   → Make it cheaper to employ people. Employment taxes must
     be abolished for young people, the long-term unemployed and
     the first employees in growing companies.
   → It must always be worth it to take up a job. Reduce taxes on
     low incomes and make sure that it is always worth it to make
     the move from benefits to work.
   → Make it easier to run a company. Away with cumbersome
     regulations.

Emissions must be reduced
When we invest in the climate, we also invest in tomorrow’s jobs and
public services. Cheaper green electricity is good for you, for business
and for the climate.

   → More low-cost electricity. Large-scale expansion of electricity
     generation and halving the lead times for grid expansion and
     connections.
   → Reduce taxes and charges on electricity,so that both
     domestic and business users are better able to make the
     transition from fossil sources.
   → Encourage the growth of green industry. With long-term rules
     of play, clear climate ambitions and enhanced support. Halve
     the lead times for climate tech companies by speeding up
     permit processes.
   → Make it profitable to make the transition. More money must
     stay where the electricity is generated. Reduce taxes on
     renewables, bring in a proper electric vehicle bonus nationwide
     and a tax reduction for green technology in the home.

Healthcare, education and social
care must work for more people
When we are at our weakest, when we are ill, when we are children, when
we are older and finding it harder to manage the things we used to do,
this is when society must be there to lend a hand.

   → Healthcare, education and social care must work across the
     country. Wherever you live, you have a right to receive good
     healthcare when you need it, to your children being able to
     attend a school that both sees and teaches them, and to help
     and peace of mind for your grandmother.
   → Record investment in the mental health of young people.
     Initiate support promptly, boost the student health service and
      expand psychiatric services for both children and adults so
      that more people can access help when they need it.
   → Better conditions and higher wages in the welfare sector.
     Those who work in healthcare must be better paid. Older
      employees who are willing to do so must be able to work for
      longer.
Sweden deserves more.
You deserve a government that takes the major issues seriously.
That makes sure that people have a job to go to in the morning and
gets the country’s economy moving again. That makes sure that emis-
sions are reduced and Swedish business can be a world leader in the
climate transition. That cuts healthcare waiting lists, makes sure that
children have a better education, that grandma gets help in whichever
part of the country she lives, and that healthcare staff do not experi-
ence burnout.
You deserve a government with both mind and heart. That pursues
a tough legal policy, but does not try to put children behind bars. That
wants to sort out migration policy – recognising that, for example, ex-
pelling people who are law-abiding and who study, work and contribute
to society is both unreasonable and uneconomic. That believes that it
must always be worth going to work, which is precisely why we want to
cut taxes for those with the lowest wages.
You deserve a government that does not go to extremes, neither to
the right nor to the left. We are convinced that people benefit from
having more control over their everyday lives. You should be judged
by what you do, not by the colour of your skin or your title. Sweden
was built on a solid foundation of trust, openness and accountability.
This is why we want to keep those on the fringes of politics away from
executive power.

Sweden can do more.
Vote for Centerpartiet.
