# Our understanding

> The hard tools land on people already in the networks. The soft tools must arrive before the first errand. School as a shield only works while the child still attends. Where those clocks clash, the page does not say which duty comes first.

System: Detection of members versus interruption of recruitment

Scenario: A teenager is used for a first errand while still on the school roll
Given criminal networks recruit ever younger children
And passing school is named as one of the strongest protections against crime
When the teenager is offered cash for a delivery in the same week as a missed lesson
Then police tools target the adult who sent the child
And social services are meant to reach the family early
Outcome the child is both a witness in a case and a pupil the school must keep
But the page does not say which service may pull the child out of class
Activates liberalerna-politik-skola

Scenario: A network moves money and weapons across a border
Given organised crime operates across national borders
When European police cooperation is strengthened
Then guns and drugs are harder to move into the country
Outcome local shootings still depend on who is left on the estate to recruit
But choking the economy of the gang needs powers the page only names in outline
