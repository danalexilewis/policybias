### What we’ll do

*   Re-build and strengthen **Māori-led health authority and solutions.**
*   Shift the system toward **prevention and whānau wellbeing,** not just crisis response.
*   Make services accessible, culturally safe, and accountable to Māori.

### Why it matters

A health system that does not understand Māori will keep failing Māori. Our people deserve care that sees the whole person, strengthens whānau, prevents harm, and is accountable to the communities it serves.
