# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Kei te pēhia te maha o ngā papatipu ika, ā, he wāhi iti noa ngā rāhui moana o te rohe moana. Ka huri te hī ika ki ngā ture ā-pūnaha rauropi, me ngā kāmera i runga waka me te wehe āta i te tārawa-takere. Ka whakawhānui te tiaki moana, ka tupu te ōhanga kahurangi mā ngā tikanga toitū, ā, ka hoki mai tētahi Oceans Secretariat mō te kāwanatanga honohono.

System: Moana hauora

Scenario: Ka huri te tiaki ika me te tiaki moana
Given 0.5% o te rohe moana kei te rāhui moana
And tētahi pūnaha kuota e kīia ana e te whārangi kāore e mahi
And he paerewa toitū o te 40% o te papatipu taketake
And e toru ngā kamupene e pupuri ana i te 60% o te kuota
When ka huri te hī ika mai i ngā kuota momo-kotahi ki ngā ture ā-pūnaha rauropi
Then ka haere ngā kāmera ki ngā waka arumoni
And ka wehea āta te tārawa-takere
And ka whakahoungia te ture rāhui moana
And ka ātetehia te keri papa moana
And ka whakahokia tētahi Oceans Secretariat
Output 30% o te rohe moana hei rāhui moana i te tau 2035
And he paerewa toitū o te 50% o te papatipu taketake
And 10,000 heketea, nui ake rānei, o te nohoanga takutai kua whakaorangia
And $500 million ia tau o te whiwhinga ōhanga kahurangi tāpiri
And 100% te pūataata mō te hī ika arumoni
And kaua e nui ake i te $100 million ia tau o te utu Karauna poto
And 86% o ngā momo mōrearea e tiakina ana, me te 14% heke i te hao o nāianei
Outcome ka kite ngā kaihao arumoni, ngahau me ngā tikanga i ngā papatipu nui ake
And ko ngā kamupene e toru e pupuri nui ana i te kuota ka kawe nui i te whakatika poto
