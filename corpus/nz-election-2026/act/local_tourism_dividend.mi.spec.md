# Ko tā te whārangi e kī ana

> Ka pēhia e te tipu manuhiri ngā hanganga kaunihera, i a te kāwanatanga matua e kohi ana i te tāke hokohoko (GST) me te International Visitor Levy. Ka utu te Local Tourism Dividend ki ia mana ā-rohe mō ia pō manuhiri arumoni kua rēhitatia mā te Accommodation Data Programme, aunoa, kāore he tono, mai i te pūtea tāpoi o nāianei. Kāore he rā tīmata i homai.

System: Local Tourism Dividend ki ngā mana ā-rohe

Scenario: Ka whiwhi ngā kaunihera i ngā utu ā-tau mō ngā pō manuhiri arumoni i manaakitia
Given ka pēhia e te tokomaha manuhiri ngā rori ā-rohe, te wai para, ngā wharepaku tūmatanui, te kohikohi para, ngā whakaurunga noho wātea me ētahi atu hanganga kaunihera
And ka utu ngā manuhiri i te tāke hokohoko (GST), ā, ka utu hoki ngā manuhiri ā-ao i te International Visitor Levy e kohia ana e te kāwanatanga matua
And ka utu ngā kaunihera me ngā kaipākeha rēti i te maha o ngā utu ā-rohe
When ka utua aunoatia ia tau ki ia mana ā-rohe mō ia pō manuhiri arumoni kua rēhitatia mā te Accommodation Data Programme
Then ka ahu ia utu i ngā raraunga pō manuhiri o ngā 12 marama kua oti tata nei
Output $1 mō ia pō manuhiri arumoni ā-whenua, ā-ao hoki kua rēhitatia i te rohe o tētahi mana ā-rohe
And tata ki te $40.6 million i te tau ki ngā kaunihera i runga i ngā pō manuhiri 2026, tae atu ki te $8.2 million mō Tāmaki Makaurau, $4.9 million mō Queenstown me te $2 million mō Rotorua
Outcome ka taea e ngā kaunihera te pūtea i ngā rori ā-rohe, te tūnga waka, te wai me te wai para, ngā wharepaku tūmatanui, te kohikohi para, ngā whakaurunga noho wātea, ngā wāhi tūmatanui, ngā painga manuhiri, ngā hanganga huihuinga me ētahi atu ratonga e pā ana ki te manuhiri
And ka taea e te Dividend te whakakapi i te pūtea rēti kua whakapaua kētia ki ngā ratonga e āhei ana, e whakaheke ana i te pīkaunga ki ngā kaipākeha rēti ā-rohe
And ka ahu te pūtea i te pūtea tāpoi o nāianei, kāore he tāke moenga hōu
