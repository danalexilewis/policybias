# What the page states

> Education must prepare young people for a more complex world. Early childhood settings are rebalanced toward community centres with tighter size and ratio rules and pay parity for teachers. Schools teach civics from Year 6, cut classroom screen time, and community night schools support lifelong learning. Detailed policy is still to launch.

System: Future-fit education

Scenario: ECE, schools and adult learning change
When the education settings are changed
Then community-run ECE is levelled up against corporate centres
And centre size is limited, with tighter space and educator-to-child ratios
And Playcentre, Kōhanga Reo and community-owned centres get extra support
And ECE teachers have pay parity with primary and secondary teachers
And early childhood education is integrated with the early years of primary school
And civics is taught from Year 6
And disabled children have a wide range of learning support
And qualified teachers are paid for extra duties and given accredited development and mentoring
And schools are encouraged to cut screen time and use screens only for specific aims
And community night schools are funded
Outcome young people and adults have more room to keep learning
