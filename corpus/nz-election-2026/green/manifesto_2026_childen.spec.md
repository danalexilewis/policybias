# What the page states

> Plan items on the source page cover child voice in law, Best Start under three, workforce training, disability support access, and Oranga Tamariki Act reform. No dollar amounts appear.

System: Tamariki, rangatahi and whānau-first child policy

Scenario: The children plan is put into effect
When the government puts the children plan into effect
Then legislation makes the child's voice central using the Child Impact Assessment Tool and incorporating the Rights of the Child into legislation
And the Best Start payment increases and extends to every child under three as a universal child benefit
And training is funded and a workforce development strategy prioritises Māori, Pacific, rainbow, disabled and community provider pathways
And access to diagnosis and support improves for children with disabilities or learning support needs
And the Oranga Tamariki Act is amended to protect tamariki Māori and embed whānau participation and iwi or hapū authority
Outcome tamariki Māori are supported through culturally grounded, whānau-led solutions centred on whakapapa and identity
