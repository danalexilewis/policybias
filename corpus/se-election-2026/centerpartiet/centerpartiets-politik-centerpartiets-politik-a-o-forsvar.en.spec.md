# What the page states

> Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine shows that peace and freedom cannot be taken for granted. The threat to Sweden and Europe is real. Defence is to be present across the country, from plains in the south to forests in the north. Popular anchoring and volunteerism are the foundation. Appropriations are to be raised to three and a half percent of GDP, the broad parliamentary settlement carried through, and NATO's target met as soon as it is practical.

System: Higher appropriations, civil defence and NATO

Scenario: Appropriations are raised while civil defence is rebuilt
Given Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine
And a threat to Sweden and Europe that is real
When defence appropriations are raised and civil defence is strengthened with more resources for municipalities and regions
Then defence is to be present across the country, with regiments and personnel that can operate in every environment
And the army, navy and air force are developed with more personnel and better matériel
And co-operation within NATO is carried through and deepened
And the voluntary defence organisations are supported
Output three and a half percent of GDP
Outcome a modern and enduring total defence is to be able to meet every kind of threat, so that everyday life can continue as normally as possible
